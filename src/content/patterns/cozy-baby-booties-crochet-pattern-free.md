This cozy baby booties crochet pattern is perfect for keeping your little one's feet warm. The soft pastel blue color, complemented by a white trim and decorative pom-poms, adds a delightful touch. Plus, the simple slip-on design makes these booties easy for babies to wear.

This pattern is suitable for crocheters who have some experience with basic stitches and are ready to try a cable stitch. You’ll enhance your skills and create a beautiful pair of booties to brighten up your baby’s wardrobe!

## Is This Pattern Good for Beginners?
Yes! Even though we are using a cable stitch, the pattern mainly consists of basic stitches, and you'll feel accomplished creating these adorable booties.

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- sl st – slip stitch
- sc – single crochet
- dc – double crochet
- hdc – half double crochet
- sp – space
- inc – increase
- BLO – back loop only

Hdc – half double crochet: yarn over, insert hook into stitch, yarn over again, pull through, yarn over again, and pull through all three loops on your hook.

## Size and Gauge
- Finished size: 10 × 12 cm (4 × 4.7 in)
- Gauge: 15 dc and 10 rows = 10 cm (4 in)

To check your gauge, crochet a swatch and measure. Adjust your hook size if necessary to match the gauge.

## Step 1: Create the Sole
The sole is worked flat. 

Row 1: Ch 11, sc in the second ch from the hook and in each ch across. (10 sc)  
Row 2: Ch 1, turn, hdc in each sc across. (10 hdc)  
Row 3: Ch 1, turn, hdc in the first 3 hdc, *2 hdc in the next st, hdc in the next 3 st; repeat from * once more. (12 hdc)  
Row 4: Ch 1, turn, hdc in the first 4 hdc, *2 hdc in the next st, hdc in the next 4 st; repeat from * once more. (14 hdc)  
Row 5: Ch 1, turn, hdc in each st across. (14 hdc)  
Row 6: Ch 1, turn, hdc in the first 5 hdc, *2 hdc in the next st, hdc in the next 5 st; repeat from * once more. (16 hdc)  
Row 7: Ch 1, turn, hdc in each st across. (16 hdc)  
Row 8: Ch 1, turn, hdc in the first 6 hdc, *2 hdc in the next st, hdc in the next 6 st; repeat from * once more. (18 hdc)  
Row 9: Ch 1, turn, hdc in each st across. (18 hdc)  
Row 10: Ch 1, turn, hdc in the first 7 hdc, *2 hdc in the next st, hdc in the next 7 st; repeat from * once more. (20 hdc)  
Row 11: Ch 1, turn, hdc in each st across. (20 hdc)  
Row 12: Ch 1, turn, hdc in the first 8 hdc, *2 hdc in the next st, hdc in the next 8 st; repeat from * once more. (22 hdc)  
Row 13: Ch 1, turn, hdc in each st across. (22 hdc)  
Row 14: Ch 1, turn, hdc in the first 9 hdc, *2 hdc in the next st, hdc in the next 9 st; repeat from * once more. (24 hdc)  
Row 15: Ch 1, turn, hdc in each st across. (24 hdc)  
Fasten off and secure ends.

## Step 2: Create the Booties
The booties are formed by working in rounds from the sole.

Round 1: Position the sole upright and join yarn at the back, ch 2 (counts as first hdc), hdc in each st around, join with sl st to top of ch 2.  
Round 2: Ch 2, hdc in the BLO of each hdc around, join with sl st to top of ch 2.  
Round 3: Ch 2, hdc in the BLO of each hdc around, join with sl st to top of ch 2.  
Round 4: Ch 2, hdc in the first 12 hdc, *(hdc in the next 2 hdc, hdc in the next hdc with cable stitch); repeat from * once more, join with sl st to top of ch 2.  
Round 5: Ch 2, hdc in the first 12 hdc, *(hdc in the next 2 hdc, hdc in the next hdc with cable stitch); repeat from * once more, join with sl st to top of ch 2.  
Round 6: Ch 1, sc in each hdc around, join with sl st to first sc.

## How to Add the White Trim
The white trim will finish off the top of the booties nicely. 

Round 1: Join white yarn, ch 1, sc in each st around, join with sl st to first sc.  
Round 2: Ch 1, sc in each sc around, join with sl st to first sc.  
Fasten off and secure ends.

## Adding Pom-Poms
To create pom-poms:
1. Cut yarn into multiple strands about 15 cm (6 in) long.
2. Gather the strands together and tie them tightly in the center with a short piece of yarn.
3. Trim the ends to your desired puffiness.
4. Attach a pom-pom to each side of the booties.

## Finishing Tip: Secure Your Ends
Weave in all yarn ends using a tapestry needle for a neat finish. 

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Cozy Baby Booties
- Use stitch markers to keep track of your rounds.
- Count your stitches accurately, especially when working in the round.
- Maintain consistent tension throughout your work for an even appearance.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- My booties are too loose. - This may happen if your stitches are too loose. Keep your tension consistent or try a smaller hook size.
- The cable stitches look uneven. - This usually happens if the tension varies. Make sure you are pulling the yarn evenly when creating the cable stitches.
- The booties are not the right size. - Your gauge may not match. Always check your gauge and adjust as necessary before starting.

## How to Care for Your Cozy Baby Booties
To keep your booties looking great, gently hand wash them in cold water and lay them flat to dry. This will help maintain their shape and softness.

## FAQ
### How many stitches do I need for a scarf?
The number of stitches depends on the width you want. A good rule of thumb for a scarf is to use around 25 to 30 stitches for a width of about 20 cm (8 in).

### Can I make these booties larger?
Yes! To make larger booties, simply add more stitches when creating the sole. Just remember to maintain the same number of rounds for the body of the booties.

You can typically finish a pair of these booties in about 2 to 3 hours, depending on your pace and experience.
