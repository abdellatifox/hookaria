## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- sl st – slip stitch
- sc – single crochet
- hdc – half double crochet: yarn over, insert hook into stitch, yarn over again and pull through all three loops on the hook.
- dc – double crochet: yarn over, insert hook into stitch, yarn over and pull through, yarn over again and pull through two loops, yarn over once more and pull through the last two loops.

## The Pattern

### Step 1: Create the Base
The base is crocheted to give the appearance of a tree trunk.

Round 1: Ch 2, work 8 sc in the second ch from the hook. Join with sl st to the first sc. (8 sc)

Round 2: Ch 1, 2 sc in each sc around. Join with sl st to the first sc. (16 sc)

Round 3: Ch 1, *sc in next sc, 2 sc in next sc; repeat from * around. Join with sl st to the first sc. (24 sc)

Round 4: Ch 1, *sc in next 2 sc, 2 sc in next sc; repeat from * around. Join with sl st to the first sc. (32 sc)

Round 5: Ch 1, *sc in next 3 sc, 2 sc in next sc; repeat from * around. Join with sl st to the first sc. (40 sc)

Round 6: Ch 1, *sc in next 4 sc, 2 sc in next sc; repeat from * around. Join with sl st to the first sc. (48 sc)

Round 7: Ch 1, sc in each sc around. Join with sl st to the first sc. (48 sc)

Continue to work in sc for about 10 cm (4 in) or until desired height is reached. 

### Step 2: Crochet the Leaf Motif
The top part of the holder features a lovely leaf shape.

Round 1: Ch 3 (this counts as your first dc), work 11 dc in the same stitch. Join with sl st to the top of the ch 3. (12 dc)

Round 2: Ch 2 (this does not count as a stitch), *dc in next dc, 2 dc in next dc; repeat from * around. Join with sl st to the top of the first dc. (18 dc)

Round 3: Ch 1, *sc in next dc, hdc in next dc, dc in next 2 dc, hdc in next dc, sc in next dc; repeat from * around. Join with sl st.

Fasten off and leave a long tail for sewing.

### Step 3: Assemble the Paper Towel Holder
Attach the leaf motif to the top of the base using the long tail. Thread the tail into a tapestry needle and sew the leaf securely to the holder. 

## How to Join Your Granny Squares
To join squares, place them with right sides facing each other. Use a sl st to join, making sure to match stitches. 

## Finishing Tip: Secure Your Ends
When you finish, make sure to weave in all yarn ends carefully using your tapestry needle. This will keep your paper towel holder looking neat.

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Cozy Cabin Paper Towel Holder
- Use stitch markers to keep track of your rounds.
- Count your stitches regularly to maintain proper shape.
- Maintain consistent tension for an even look.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- My paper towel holder is too loose. – This may be due to too big of a hook. Consider using a smaller hook to achieve a tighter fabric.
- The leaf is not laying flat. – It’s possible the stitches are too tight. Gently steam block the leaf to help it flatten.
- My base seems uneven. – Check your stitch count for each round. Keeping counts accurate will help keep the shape consistent.

## How to Care for Your Cozy Cabin Paper Towel Holder
For best results, clean your paper towel holder by wiping it down with a damp cloth. Avoid submerging it in water to maintain its shape and texture.

### Can I resize the pattern?
Yes! You can adjust the foundation chain to make the base larger or smaller. Just keep the same increase rules for your rounds.
