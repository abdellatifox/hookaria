## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- dc – double crochet
- sl st – slip stitch
- sc – single crochet
- st – stitch

**Special Stitch: Dolphin Appliqué**  
1. Start with a magic ring.  
2. Round 1: Ch 3 (counts as dc), 11 dc into ring, pull tight to close (12 sts).  
3. Round 2: (2 dc in next st, 1 dc in next st) repeat around (18 sts).  
4. Rounds 3–5: dc in each st around (18 sts).  
5. Fasten off, leaving a long tail for sewing.

## Size and Gauge
- Finished size: 14 × 12 in (35.5 × 30.5 cm)
- Gauge: 14 dc and 7 rows in dc = 4 in (10 cm)

To check your gauge, make a swatch with 16 dc by 8 rows.

## How to Crochet a Dolphin Pod Summer Bag (Step by Step)

### Bag Body
The bag body is crocheted flat and then finished in rounds.  
1. Foundation Row: Ch 37. (36 ch)  
2. Row 1: Dc in 4th ch from hook and in each ch across (34 dc).  
3. Row 2: Ch 3, turn, dc in each st across (34 dc).  
4. Rows 3–20: Repeat Row 2. (34 dc each row)  
5. Row 21: Ch 1, turn, sc in each st across (34 sc).  
6. Cut yarn, fasten off.

### Dolphin Appliqué (Make 2)
Refer to the special stitch instructions for the Dolphin Appliqué above. You’ll want two dolphins for your bag.

### Bag Edging
The edging gives your bag a polished finish.  
1. With Color A, join yarn to any corner of the bag.  
2. Round 1: Ch 1, sc evenly around the entire top edge of the bag (approx. 36 sc).  
3. Round 2: Ch 1, sc in first st, *ch 2, skip next st, sc in next st; repeat from * around (18 ch-2 spaces).  
4. Round 3: Ch 1, sc in each sc and ch-2 space around (54 sts).  
5. Cut yarn, fasten off.

## Assembly & Finishing
- Position the dolphin appliqués on the front of the bag. Sew them in place using the tapestry needle and the long tail left for stitching.
- Attach the webbing straps to the sides of the bag using yarn or fabric glue for a secure hold.
- Pin the lining fabric to the inside of the bag, attaching it with a straight stitch.
- Sew in the magnetic snap closure at the top center of the lining for easy opening and closing.
- Weave in all ends and block the bag if desired.

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Summer Bag
- Use stitch markers to keep track of your rounds or rows.
- Count your stitches frequently, especially after completing a row, to maintain an even shape.
- Pay attention to tension; relax your grip to avoid tight stitches.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- My project is uneven. – This can happen if you're not counting your stitches. Make sure to keep track of each row!
- The bag feels too tight. – If your stitches are too tight, consider loosening your grip or using a larger hook size.
- I don’t like my dolphin appliqué. – It's easy to adjust! If you're not happy with the size or shape, rip it out and give it another try.

## How to Care for Your Summer Bag
To keep your Dolphin Pod Summer Bag looking its best, gently hand wash and lay it flat to dry. This will help maintain its shape and the integrity of the appliqués.
