This adorable Neutral Boho Baby Romper is a stylish and comfortable outfit for little ones, featuring a relaxed fit and bohemian-inspired details. This pattern is designed for babies aged 6–12 months and uses basic stitches like single crochet, half double crochet, and double crochet, making it enjoyable to create. 

You'll learn to work in one piece and how to attach straps and buttons, which are fun skills to add to your crochet repertoire. Let's dive into making this charming romper together!

## Why You'll Love This Baby Romper
- Quick and enjoyable – you'll have a lovely romper ready in just a weekend.  
- Great for gifting – perfect for sweet baby showers or welcoming little ones.  
- Easy to customize – use any color combinations you like for a unique touch.  
- Practical design – the relaxed fit allows for comfort and easy dressing.

## Is This Pattern Good for Beginners?
Yes! This pattern is beginner-friendly. It mainly uses basic stitches, and you'll be working in straight rows, which makes it easy to follow. If you've mastered a few stitches, you can confidently tackle this project.

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain  
- sc – single crochet  
- hdc – half double crochet  
- dc – double crochet  
- sl st – slip stitch  
- sp – space  
- st(s) – stitch(es)  
- inc – increase (2 stitches in the same stitch)  
- dec – decrease (2 stitches together)  

## Size and Gauge
- **Finished Size:** 6–12 months (approx. 20 inches / 51 cm in length from shoulder to crotch)  
- **Gauge:** 18 stitches and 10 rows = 4 inches (10 cm) in double crochet.  
  - To check gauge, crochet a swatch of 20 dc x 12 rows. Adjust your hook size if necessary.

## How to Crochet a Neutral Boho Baby Romper (Step by Step)
The romper is worked in one piece from the bottom up. Let's start!

### Main Body
1. **Ch 50** with Color A.  
2. **Row 1:** Hdc in the 3rd ch from hook, hdc in each ch across (48 hdc).  
3. **Rows 2–10:** Ch 2, turn, hdc in each st across (48 hdc).  
   - This piece should measure about 10 inches (25 cm) long at the end of this step.  
4. **Row 11:** Ch 2, hdc in next 20 st, (inc in next st, hdc in next 5 st) 3 times, hdc in last 2 st (54 hdc).  
5. **Rows 12–16:** Ch 2, turn, hdc in each st across (54 hdc).  
6. **Row 17:** Ch 2, hdc in next 6 st, (dec over next 2 st, hdc in next 10 st) 4 times, dec over last 2 st (50 hdc).  
7. **Row 18:** Ch 2, turn, hdc in each st across (50 hdc).  
8. **Row 19:** Ch 2, hdc in next 5 st, (dec over next 2 st, hdc in next 9 st) 4 times, dec over last 2 st (46 hdc).  
9. **Row 20:** Ch 2, turn, hdc in each st across (46 hdc).  
10. **Row 21:** Ch 2, hdc in next 4 st, (dec over next 2 st, hdc in next 8 st) 4 times, dec over last 2 st (42 hdc).  
11. **Row 22:** Ch 2, turn, hdc in each st across (42 hdc).

### Legs
For Leg 1:
12. **Row 23:** Ch 2, hdc in next 15 st, ch 10 (turn to work in round), sl st to first hdc.  
13. **Row 24:** Working in round, hdc in each ch and across the leg (25 hdc).  
14. **Rows 25–27:** Ch 2, hdc in each st (25 hdc).  
15. **Row 28:** Ch 2, hdc in next 5 st, (dec over next 2 st, hdc in next 6 st) 3 times (22 hdc).  
16. **Row 29:** Ch 2, hdc in each st (22 hdc).  
17. Fasten off and secure the yarn.

For Leg 2:
18. Attach yarn to the 1st hdc of leg 1. Repeat Rows 23–29 as above (22 hdc).

### Straps
19. **Row 1:** With Color B, ch 30.  
20. **Row 2:** Sc in 2nd ch from hook, sc across (29 sc).  
21. **Row 3:** Ch 1, turn, sc in first st, sc in next 27 st (29 sc).  
22. Fasten off. Repeat for 2nd strap.

### Assembly
1. Position the straps evenly on the back of the romper and pin them in place.  
2. **Sew the straps** to the desired length at the shoulder using a tapestry needle.  
3. Attach the button at the end of one strap to the corresponding position on the other strap using slip stitches, ensuring secure closure.

## Finishing
1. Weave in all ends using a tapestry needle.  
2. Block the romper with a damp cloth and straighten edges to achieve desired dimensions. 

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Baby Romper
- Use stitch markers to keep track of your rows.  
- Count your stitches regularly to avoid losing track.  
- Practice consistent tension to ensure even stitches.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- "My project is uneven." – This can happen if your tension varies; keep your grip consistent.  
- "I missed a stitch." – Check your work after each row to catch any mistakes early.  
- "The leg openings are too tight." – Ensure you follow the stitch counts and make adjustments for a comfortable fit.

## FAQ
### How do I adjust the size for a larger baby?
While this pattern is specifically designed for 6–12 months, you can increase your starting chain by adding more stitches in increments of 4 to create a larger size.

### Can I use a different yarn type?
Absolutely! Just make sure that the yarn weight remains similar (DK / 3) to maintain the right dimensions and gauge.

### What's the best way to care for my romper?
Hand wash in cold water and lay it flat to dry to maintain the shape and softness of the cotton yarn.
