## Gauge and Finished Size
- Finished blanket size: Approximately 60 × 60 in (150 × 150 cm)
- Individual granny square size: 12 × 12 in (30 × 30 cm)
- Gauge: 10 rows and 10 squares = 10 in (25 cm) in granny square pattern. Check by crocheting a 10 × 10 in (25 × 25 cm) square.

## Step-by-Step Instructions
### Granny Square
Each granny square measures approximately 12 × 12 in (30 × 30 cm). You will need to make a total of 25 squares for a 5 × 5 layout.

**Square: Start with Terracotta yarn.**

![Earthy Terracotta Boho Granny Square Blanket Free Crochet Pattern](/images/patterns/earthy-terracotta-boho-granny-square-blanket-free-crochet-pattern-2.jpg)

1. Ch 5, sl st to form a ring. (1 ring)
2. Rnd 1: (1 ch, 2 dc, ch 2) x 3, sl st to top of ch. (12 dc, 3 ch-2 sp)
3. Rnd 2: Sl st to first ch-2 sp, (ch 3, 2 dc, ch 2, 3 dc) in same sp; (3 dc in next ch-2 sp) x 2, (ch 2, 3 dc) in next ch-2 sp; repeat from * to * around; sl st to top of ch 3. (24 dc, 4 ch-2 sp)
4. Rnd 3: Sl st to first ch-2 sp, (ch 3, 2 dc, ch 2, 3 dc) in same sp; (3 dc in next sp, ch 1) x 3, (3 dc in next ch-2 sp) x 2; repeat from * to * around; sl st to top of ch 3. (36 dc, 4 ch-2 sp)
5. Fasten off.

**Repeat these steps to create squares in the following combinations:**
- 8 Terracotta 
- 9 Cream 
- 4 Olive Green 
- 4 Mustard Yellow 

### Joining Squares
Join the squares using the whip stitch method.

1. Lay out the squares in a 5 × 5 grid.
2. Thread your tapestry needle with a long length of yarn in a neutral color.
3. Place two squares together, right sides facing, and align the edges.
4. Insert needle through the top corner of both squares, pull through but do not tighten completely.
5. Repeat this for the top edge of the squares until all squares in the row are joined.
6. Repeat for all rows until your blanket is assembled.

## Assembly & Finishing
Once all squares are joined, weave in any ends with the tapestry needle. To block your blanket, dampen it with water, pin it to a flat surface, and allow it to dry fully to shape.

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Blanket
- Keep your tension consistent to ensure uniform squares.
- Use stitch markers to mark important stitches or rows.
- Count your stitches regularly to stay on track.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- "My squares are uneven." – This may happen if your tension varies; focus on maintaining a steady stitch size.
- "I lost track of my stitch count." – Try counting as you go or using stitch markers to keep your place.
- "The squares don’t match in size." – Ensure you are using the same hook and yarn consistently throughout your project.

## How to Care for Your Blanket
Machine wash cold on a gentle cycle. Tumble dry on low or lay flat to dry to keep your blanket looking its best.
