This stylish Boho Fringe Tote crochet pattern is perfect for your trips to the beach or running daily errands. The spacious rectangular shape and playful fringes at the bottom add a modern bohemian flair to this practical bag. You'll enjoy using basic stitches, combining double crochet and chain stitches to create a delightful texture.

This pattern is suitable for confident beginners and beyond. You'll enhance your skills by working with different stitch combinations and creating that lovely fringe detail at the end. Let's make a tote that is both functional and trendy!

## Is This Pattern Good for Beginners?
Yes! This pattern is great for beginners who are comfortable with basic crochet stitches like double crochet and chains. While working on this tote, you'll become more confident with your stitches and learn how to create a structured bag.

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- sl st – slip stitch
- dc – double crochet
- sp – space
- inc – increase

## Size and Gauge
- Finished Size: 35 × 45 cm (14 × 18 in)
- Gauge: 14 dc and 8 rows = 10 cm (4 in)

Measuring gauge can help ensure your tote holds its shape correctly. If your stitches are too tight or loose, you may need to adjust your hook size.

## Step 1: Create the Base
The base is worked in rows back and forth. 

Row 1: Ch 49, dc in 4th ch from hook (this counts as your first dc), and in each ch across. Turn. (47 dc)

Row 2: Ch 3 (this counts as your first dc), dc in each dc across. Turn. (47 dc) 

Repeat Row 2 until the piece measures 35 cm (14 in) from the starting chain.

## Step 2: Create the Sides
Now we will turn the base to form the sides of the tote.

Row 1: Ch 1 (does not count as a stitch), sc in first dc, *ch 1, skip next dc, sc in next dc; repeat from * across. Turn. 

Row 2: Ch 3 (this counts as your first dc), dc in each ch-1 sp across. Turn.

Continue to repeat Row 2 until the piece measures 10 cm (4 in) for the sides.

## Step 3: Add the Fringes
The fringes add a decorative touch at the bottom of the tote. 

For the fringe, cut 20 strands of yarn, each approximately 20 cm (8 in) long. 

Attach fringe: Insert your hook into the bottom edge of the tote. Fold a strand of yarn in half, pull the loop through, and then bring the ends through the loop. Pull tight to secure. Repeat every 2 cm (1 in) across the bottom edge.

## How to Join Your Sides
Once all parts are attached, place the two sides together. 

Join with a sl st across the edges and fasten off. 

## Finishing Tip: Secure Your Ends
Weave in any loose ends with a tapestry needle to keep your bag tidy. 

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Boho Fringe Tote
- Use stitch markers to keep track of your rows.
- Count your stitches accurately to maintain the shape.
- Maintain consistent tension for a uniform look.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- "My bag is too small." – Check your starting chain and make sure it matches the pattern instructions.
- "The edges are uneven." – Ensure you're consistently following the stitch count and using the same tension throughout the project.
- "My fringe looks messy." – Trim strands to uniform length after attaching for a clean appearance.

## How to Care for Your Boho Fringe Tote
This bag can be easily washed. Hand wash in cold water with mild detergent and lay flat to dry. Avoid wringing out to maintain the bag's shape.
