This cozy crochet beanie will keep you warm and stylish during the colder months. The textured stitch pattern creates a lovely visual interest, and the chunky yarn ensures it's soft and warm. The braided pom-pom adds a playful touch, making this beanie a great accessory for any winter outfit.

This pattern is suitable for confident beginners and those looking to improve their skills, as it combines basic stitches with a bit of texture work. Let’s dive into creating your own cozy beanie!

## Why You'll Love This Beanie
- Cozy and warm – made with chunky yarn to keep you snug in chilly weather.
- Fun texture – the stitch pattern adds dimension to your beanie.
- Customizable fit – adjust length for your desired coverage.
- Stylish finish – a braided pom-pom gives a playful touch to your look.

## Is This Pattern Good for Beginners?
Yes! This pattern is great for beginners who want to practice basic stitches and learn how to create texture in their work. You'll get plenty of practice with single crochet and double crochet while making a fashionable accessory.

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- sl st – slip stitch
- sc – single crochet
- hdc – half double crochet: yarn over, insert hook into stitch, pull up a loop, yarn over again, pull through all three loops on hook.
- dc – double crochet: yarn over, insert hook into stitch, pull up a loop, yarn over, pull through two loops, yarn over, pull through remaining two loops.
- BLO – back loop only
- sp – space

## Size and Gauge
- Finished Size: Adult size, approximately 56–58 cm (22–23 in) head circumference.
- Gauge: 12 dc and 7 rows = 10 cm (4 in) square.

To ensure your beanie fits properly, check your gauge by crocheting a small swatch. Gauge is important as it affects the final size of your project.

## The Pattern
The beanie is worked in the round, starting with the ribbed brim.

## Step 1: Create the Brim
The brim will be worked in a ribbed pattern.
Round 1: Ch 10, work 1 sc into the second ch from the hook and in each ch across. (9 sc)  
Round 2: Ch 1, turn; HDC in the BLO of each stitch across. (9 hdc)  
Repeat Round 2 until the piece measures approximately 10 cm (4 in) long. 

Now, we’ll join the ends to form a tube.
Join: Fold the brim in half and slip stitch the short ends together. Now we’ll continue working up the beanie.

## Step 2: Crochet the Body
Continue by working into the top edge of the brim.
Round 1: Ch 1, work 1 sc in each st around. Join with sl st to first sc. (9 sc)  
Round 2: Ch 2 (counts as first dc), work 1 dc in the next st and in each st around. Join with sl st to top of ch 2. (9 dc)  
Round 3: Ch 1, work 1 sc in each st around. Join with sl st to first sc.  
Round 4: Ch 2, work 1 hdc in the next st, *[2 dc in next st, 1 hdc in next st]*; repeat from * around. Join with sl st to top of ch 2.  
Repeat Rounds 3 and 4 until the body measures about 20 cm (8 in) from the brim. 

## Step 3: Decrease for the Crown
Now, we'll shape the crown of the beanie.
Round 1: Ch 1, *[sc in next 3 sts, sc2tog]*; repeat from * around. Join with sl st.  
Round 2: Ch 1, *[sc in next 2 sts, sc2tog]*; repeat from * around. Join with sl st.  
Round 3: Ch 1, *[sc in next st, sc2tog]*; repeat from * around. Join with sl st.  
Round 4: Ch 1, sc2tog in each st around. Join with sl st. 

What’s happening? You’re decreasing the number of stitches in each round to shape the top of the beanie, giving it a snug fit.

## Finishing the Beanie
Fasten off the yarn, leaving a long tail for sewing. Take the remaining stitches and gather them with the yarn tail, pulling tight to close the hole. 

## How to Make a Braided Yarn Pom-Pom
1. Cut two lengths of yarn, each about 60 cm (24 in) long.
2. Hold the strands together and tie a knot in the center.
3. Cut the ends to your desired pom-pom size.
4. Braid the yarn and secure the ends with a knot.
5. Attach to the top of the beanie using the remaining yarn tail.

## Finishing Tip: Secure Your Ends
Make sure to weave in any loose ends with a tapestry needle to give your beanie a neat finish.

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Beanie
- Use a stitch marker to keep track of the start of your rounds.
- Count your stitches regularly to avoid losing track.
- Maintain even tension for a consistent look.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
"My beanie is too tight." – This can happen if your gauge is off. Check this next time by making a gauge swatch before starting.  
"My pom-pom looks messy." – Trim it evenly after you secure it, and make sure to use the same length of yarn for braiding.  
"The beanie feels floppy." – Ensure you’re using the right yarn and maintain a consistent stitch tension throughout.

## How to Care for Your Beanie
To keep your beanie looking its best, hand wash in cold water and lay it flat to dry. Avoid machine washing to prevent deformity.

## FAQ

### Can I use a different yarn weight?
Yes! Just be mindful to check your gauge and adjust hook size accordingly to maintain the fit of the beanie.

The beanie typically takes about 4–6 hours, depending on your skill level and familiarity with the stitches used.
