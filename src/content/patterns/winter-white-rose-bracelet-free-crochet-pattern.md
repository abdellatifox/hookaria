## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- sl st – slip stitch
- sc – single crochet
- hdc – half double crochet
- dc – double crochet
- tr – treble crochet
- petal – (as made in the special stitch section)

## How to Make a Rose Petal
1. **Rose Petal**: (Make 5 petals)
   - Ch 3, 1 dc in 3rd ch from hook, ch 2, 2 dc in same stitch, sl st to join (creating a petal shape).

## Size and Gauge
- Finished bracelet size: approximately 18 cm (7 in) circumference, and 2.5 cm (1 in) wide.
- Rose diameter: approximately 5 cm (2 in).
- Gauge: 12 stitches and 8 rows in double crochet (dc) = 10 cm (4 in).

## How to Crochet a Winter White Rose Bracelet (Step by Step)

### Bracelet Band
The band is crocheted flat and then joined.  
1. **Ch 40** to start. (40 sts)
2. **Row 1:** Hdc in each chain across. (40 sts)
3. **Row 2:** Ch 1, turn, sc in each stitch across. (40 sts)
4. **Row 3:** Ch 2, turn, dc in each stitch across. (40 sts)
5. **Row 4:** Repeat Row 2. (40 sts)
6. **Row 5:** Repeat Row 3. (40 sts)
7. **Row 6:** Ch 1, turn, sc in each stitch across. (40 sts)
8. **Row 7:** Fold the band lengthwise and sl st along the edge to join it together.
   - **Finish off**, leaving a long tail for sewing on the rose.

### Crochet Rose
The rose is created in rounds, adding dimension and beauty.  
1. **Ch 5**, join with a sl st to form a ring.
2. **Round 1:** Ch 3 (counts as first dc), 11 dc in the ring. Join with a sl st. (12 sts)
3. **Round 2:** Ch 3, (2 dc in next dc) repeat around. Join with a sl st. (24 sts)
4. **Round 3:** Ch 3, dc in next 2 dc, (2 dc in next dc, dc in next 2 dc) repeat around. Join with a sl st. (32 sts)
5. **Round 4:** *For the petals:* Sl st into the next 4 stitches, ch 3, 1 dc, ch 2, 2 dc in the same stitch. Sl st into the next stitch. Repeat around to create 5 petals.
6. Finish off and weave in ends.

### Assembly
1. Sew the rose securely to the center of the bracelet band using the long tail.
2. Attach the button on one end of the band and create a small loop on the other end using jewelry elastic or thin ribbon if using.

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Bracelet
- Use stitch markers to keep track of your rows or rounds.
- Count your stitches after each row to avoid mistakes.
- Pay attention to your tension to ensure an even finish.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- "My bracelet is too tight." – This could be due to tight tension. Relax your grip on the yarn and try again.
- "The rose petals look uneven." – Check that all petals have the same number of stitches and follow the instructions carefully.
- "I can't get the rose to stay attached." – Be sure to sew the rose on securely, using the long tail to firmly stitch it in place.

## How to Care for Your Bracelet
Hand wash gently in cold water and lay flat to dry. Avoid wringing out the bracelet to maintain its shape.

## FAQ
### How can I adjust the bracelet size?

### Can I use a different yarn?
Yes! You can use any medium worsted weight yarn, but keep an eye on the gauge for the best fit.
