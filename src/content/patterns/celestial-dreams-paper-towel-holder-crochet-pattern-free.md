## The Pattern

### Step 1: Create the Base
The base of the paper towel holder is worked in rounds to form a sturdy foundation.

Round 1: Ch 3 (this counts as your first dc), work 11 dc into the first ch. Join with sl st to the top of the first dc. (12 dc)

Round 2: Ch 3 (counts as your first dc), dc in the same st, *2 dc in next st; repeat from * around. Join with sl st to the top of the first dc. (24 dc)

Round 3: Ch 3 (counts as your first dc), dc in the same st, *dc in next st, 2 dc in next st; repeat from * around. Join with sl st to the top of the first dc. (36 dc)

Round 4: Ch 3 (counts as your first dc), dc in the same st, *dc in next 2 st, 2 dc in next st; repeat from * around. Join with sl st to the top of the first dc. (48 dc)

## Step 2: Crochet the Body
The body of the towel holder features the celestial motifs.

Round 5: Ch 1 (does not count as a stitch), sc in the same st, *ch 2, skip 2 st, sc in next st; repeat from * around. Join with sl st to the first sc. (16 sc)

Rounds 6–12: Repeat Round 5, alternating colors between deep midnight blue and shimmering silver for each round.

### Adding Moon and Star Motifs
Round 12: Ch 1, sc in the same st, *ch 3, sl st in the first ch to create a loop (this is your star), sc in next st, ch 5, sl st in the 2nd ch from hook, sl st in next, sc in next st; repeat from * around, making sure to arrange the motifs evenly.

## Step 3: Add the Scalloped Edge
The scalloped edge at the top creates a lovely finish.

Round 13: Ch 1 (does not count as a stitch), sc in the same st, *ch 3, skip next st, sc in next; repeat from * around. Join with sl st to the first sc.

Round 14: Ch 1, sc in the same st, *ch 5, skip next 2 st, sc in next; repeat from * around. Join with sl st to the first sc.

## Finishing Tip: Secure Your Ends
To finish your paper towel holder, fasten off your yarn, weave in all ends with a tapestry needle, and trim excess yarn.

## Beginner Tips
- Use stitch markers to keep track of your rounds.
- Count your stitches to maintain even spacing in motifs.
- Maintain consistent tension for a uniform look.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- My stitches are too tight. – This often happens if you’re nervous. Try to relax your hands and enjoy the process.
- The shape is uneven. – Check that you’re counting your stitches correctly and following the increase instructions.
- Motifs look wonky. – If they aren’t coming out right, check if you’re placing them evenly and using the right hook size.

## How to Care for Your Celestial Dreams Paper Towel Holder
To maintain your paper towel holder, gently spot clean with a damp cloth. Avoid submerging it in water to preserve the yarn’s integrity.
