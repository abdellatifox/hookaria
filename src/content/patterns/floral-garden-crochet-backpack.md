This floral garden crochet backpack combines functionality with a whimsical design. It showcases a rounded shape adorned with an intricate floral stitch pattern. The soft yellows and greens will remind you of a vibrant garden in bloom. This project is not only beautiful but also practical, featuring adjustable straps and a drawstring closure for everyday use.

This pattern is suitable for intermediate crocheters seeking to enhance their skills. You’ll work with a variety of stitches and techniques, allowing you to create not only a stylish accessory but also to expand your crochet knowledge.

- Practical size – roomy enough for daily essentials.
- Adjustable features – customizable straps make it easy to wear.
- Fun project – enjoy the process of making a functional piece of art.

## Skill Level: Intermediate
You should already know how to work basic stitches like chain, single crochet, and double crochet. You'll also learn how to create a floral stitch.

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- sl st – slip stitch
- sc – single crochet
- dc – double crochet
- hdc – half double crochet
- inc – increase
- BLO – back loop only

## How to Crochet the Floral Stitch
1. Make a foundation chain in multiples of 6.
2. Row 1: Work 1 sc in the 2nd ch from the hook and in each ch across.
3. Row 2: Ch 3 (counts as first dc), turn, *(dc in next st, hdc in next st, sc in next st, hdc in next st, dc in next st) in the next st; repeat from * across.
4. Repeat Rows 1 and 2 for the floral stitch pattern.

## Size and Gauge
- Finished size: Approximately 30 × 40 cm (12 × 16 in).
- Gauge: 16 dc and 10 rows = 10 cm.

To measure gauge, crochet a small swatch. Count how many stitches and rows fit within a 10 cm square. Adjust your hook size if necessary.

## Step 1: Create the Backpack Body
The body of the backpack is worked in the round using the floral stitch.

Round 1: Ch 3 (this counts as your first dc), work 11 dc in a magic ring. Join with sl st to the top of the ch 3 (12 dc).

Round 2: Ch 3, dc in the same st, 2 dc in each st around. Join with sl st to the top of the ch 3 (24 dc).

Round 3: Ch 3, *(dc in next st, 2 dc in next st); repeat from * around. Join with sl st (36 dc).

Rounds 4–8: Work in floral stitch pattern. Join with sl st at the end of each round.

What’s happening? You’re increasing the stitch count to create a wider base while maintaining the beautiful floral design.

## Step 2: Create the Backpack Top
The top is made by continuing the floral stitch pattern.

Rounds 9–15: Continue working in floral stitch without increasing. Join with sl st at the end of each round.

## Step 3: Add the Drawstring Closure
To create the drawstring, work a long chain.

Ch 80 (or as needed for your desired length). Fasten off and leave a long tail. Weave the chain through the top stitches of the backpack body. Pull to gather and secure.

## How to Add Adjustable Straps
To make the straps, you’ll work a simple chain.

For each strap: Ch 100 (or your desired length). Fasten off. Attach each strap to the sides of the backpack using slip stitches.

## Finishing Tip: Secure Your Ends
Weave in all ends using a tapestry needle. This will ensure your backpack is neat and ready for use.

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Backpack
- Use stitch markers to mark important stitches.
- Count your stitches as you go to keep everything even.
- Maintain consistent tension to ensure a uniform look.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- My backpack is too loose. – This may happen if your tension is inconsistent. Try to maintain even tension in your stitches.
- The stitches look inconsistent. – Ensure you’re using the same hook size throughout, and double-check your stitch counts.
- My floral pattern is uneven. – Take your time with the floral stitch, and use stitch markers to mark where increases occur.

## How to Care for Your Backpack
Hand wash your backpack in cold water with mild soap. Lay flat to dry to maintain its shape and design.
