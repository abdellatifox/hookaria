## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- sl st – slip stitch
- sc – single crochet
- hdc – half double crochet: yarn over, insert hook into stitch, yarn over again, pull through all three loops on the hook.
- dc – double crochet: yarn over, insert hook into stitch, yarn over again, pull through, yarn over, pull through the first two loops, yarn over, pull through the last two loops.
- sp – space
- inc – increase
- BLO – back loop only

## The Pattern

### Step 1: Create the Base
The base is worked in rounds.  
Round 1: Ch 2, work 8 sc in the second ch from the hook. Join with sl st to the first sc. (8 sc)  
Round 2: Ch 1, 2 sc in each sc around. Join with sl st to the first sc. (16 sc)  
Round 3: Ch 1, *sc in next sc, 2 sc in next sc; repeat from * around. Join with sl st to the first sc. (24 sc)  
Round 4: Ch 1, *sc in next 2 sc, 2 sc in next sc; repeat from * around. Join with sl st to the first sc. (32 sc)  
Round 5: Ch 1, *sc in next 3 sc, 2 sc in next sc; repeat from * around. Join with sl st to the first sc. (40 sc)  
Round 6: Ch 1, *sc in next 4 sc, 2 sc in next sc; repeat from * around. Join with sl st to the first sc. (48 sc)  
Round 7: Ch 1, sc in each sc around. Join with sl st to the first sc. (48 sc)  
Fasten off.

### Step 2: Crochet the Body
The body is worked in the spiraled pattern.  
Round 1: Attach aqua yarn to any sc from the last round. Ch 1, sc in same stitch, work 2 sc in each sc around. (96 sc)  
Round 2: Ch 1, work in BLO, sc in each sc around. Join with sl st to the first sc. (96 sc)  
Round 3: Ch 1, *sc in next 3 sc, inc in next sc; repeat from * around. Join with sl st to the first sc. (120 sc)  
Rounds 4–8: Ch 1, sc in each sc around. Join with sl st to the first sc. (120 sc)  
Fasten off.

### Step 3: Add Shell Motifs
The top will feature shell motifs in sandy beige yarn.  
Round 1: Attach sandy beige yarn, ch 3 (counts as first dc), *dc in next sc, ch 1, skip next sc; repeat from * around. Join with sl st to the top of the ch-3.  
Round 2: Sl st into first ch-1 sp, ch 3 (counts as first dc), work 2 dc, ch 1, 3 dc in same sp (shell made). *Shell in each ch-1 sp around. Join with sl st to the top of the ch-3.  
Fasten off and weave in all ends.

## How to Join Your Granny Squares
If you make this with multiple pieces, lay them side by side and use a sl st to join the edges of the squares together working through both pieces.

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Paper Towel Holder
- Use stitch markers to keep track of your rounds.
- Count your stitches accurately to maintain an even shape.
- Maintain consistent tension throughout to avoid unevenness.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
"My base is too small." – This may happen if you skipped joining stitches. Count your stitches in each round to ensure accuracy.  
"My spirals are uneven." – This may be due to inconsistent tension. Make sure to keep a firm but comfortable hold on your yarn as you work.  
"The shells don't look defined." – Ensure you're working into the correct spaces and using the right yarn weight to achieve the best texture.

## How to Care for Your Paper Towel Holder
To keep your paper towel holder clean, simply wipe it down with a damp cloth. Avoid soaking it in water.
