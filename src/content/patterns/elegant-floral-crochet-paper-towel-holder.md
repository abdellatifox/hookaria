This elegant floral crochet paper towel holder features beautiful floral-inspired designs and intricate lace-like stitches. The holder's cylindrical shape allows for easy paper towel dispensing, while its soft pastel colors add a stunning touch to your kitchen or bathroom decor. You'll work with a mix of basic stitches and some advanced techniques to enhance your crochet skills.

## Is This Pattern Good for Beginners?
Yes! This pattern is perfect for beginners looking to enhance their skills. You will learn to work in the round, create lace-like stitches, and follow a simple floral motif. It’s a great project that can be completed relatively quickly, allowing you to see the results of your efforts fast.

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- sl st – slip stitch
- sc – single crochet
- hdc – half double crochet: yarn over, insert hook into stitch, yarn over again, pull through (3 loops on hook), yarn over, pull through all 3 loops.
- dc – double crochet: yarn over, insert hook into stitch, yarn over, pull through (3 loops on hook), yarn over, pull through 2 loops (2 loops left), yarn over, pull through the last 2 loops.
- sk – skip
- inc – increase
- BLO – back loop only
- sp – space

## Size and Gauge
- Finished Size: 30 cm tall × 15 cm diameter (12 in tall × 6 in diameter)
- Gauge: 14 dc and 6 rows = 10 cm (4 in)

To check your gauge, make a small swatch with your chosen yarn and hook. Adjust your hook size if necessary to match the gauge.

## The Pattern

## Step 1: Create the Base
The base is worked in the round. 
Round 1: Ch 2, work 8 sc into the second ch from the hook. Join with sl st to the first sc. (8 sc)  
Round 2: Ch 1 (does not count as a stitch), *2 sc in next st; repeat from * around. Join with sl st. (16 sc)  
Round 3: Ch 1, *sc in next st, 2 sc in next st; repeat from * around. Join with sl st. (24 sc)  
Round 4: Ch 1, *sc in next 2 st, 2 sc in next st; repeat from * around. Join with sl st. (32 sc)  
Round 5: Ch 1, *sc in next 3 st, 2 sc in next st; repeat from * around. Join with sl st. (40 sc)  

## Step 2: Crochet the Body
The body is made with a combination of stitches to create the floral design.  
Round 6: Ch 1, sc in same st as join, sc in next 39 st. Join with sl st. (40 sc)  
Round 7: Ch 1, sc in same st, *hdc in next st, sc in next st; repeat from * around. Join with sl st. (60 st)  
Round 8: Ch 1, *2 dc in next st, sk 1 st; repeat from * around. Join with sl st. (30 sets of 2 dc)  
Round 9: Work in BLO, ch 1, sc in each 2 dc, then ch 2 in between. Join with sl st. This creates your lace effect. (30 sc + 30 ch spaces)  
Rounds 10–15: Repeat Round 9 for the height of the holder, maintaining the lace design.

## Step 3: Add the Scalloped Edge
We will create a decorative edge at the top.  
Round 16: Ch 1, *sc in next st, hdc in next st, 3 dc in next st, hdc in next st, sc in next st; repeat from * around. Join with sl st. This will give you a scalloped appearance.

## Adding the Floral Overlay Motif
The floral overlay is worked separately.  
Round 1: Ch 2, work 6 sc into the second ch from the hook. Join with sl st. (6 sc)  
Round 2: Ch 1, *2 sc in each st; repeat from * around. Join with sl st. (12 sc)  
Round 3: Ch 1, sc in same st, *hdc in next st, 2 dc in next st, hdc in next st, sc in next st; repeat from * around. Join with sl st. Fasten off.

Attach your floral overlay to the top of the holder using a tapestry needle to sew it in place securely.

## Finishing Tip: Secure Your Ends
Fasten off all your work and weave in all loose ends using a tapestry needle. 

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Paper Towel Holder
- Use stitch markers to track rounds and stitches.
- Count your stitches as you go to avoid mistakes.
- Maintain consistent tension for a uniform finish.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
"My scalloped edge looks uneven." – This can happen if you change stitch counts. Count your stitches as you work the edge for uniformity.  
"My lace pattern is too tight." – This often occurs from using a smaller hook. Try a larger hook for more flexibility in the lace.  
"The holder is not tall enough." – If that’s the case, simply add more rounds to suit your needs. 

## How to Care for Your Paper Towel Holder
To care for your paper towel holder, spot clean with a damp cloth. Avoid soaking it in water.
