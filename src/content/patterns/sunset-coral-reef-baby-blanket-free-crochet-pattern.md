## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- CH – chain
- DC – double crochet
- SL ST – slip stitch
- FO – finish off
- sts – stitches

## Size and Gauge
- **Finished Size**: 30 × 36 in (76 × 91 cm)
- **Gauge**: 12 DC and 7 rows = 10 cm (4 in)
To measure gauge, work a 4-inch square using the main stitch pattern. Adjust your hook size if your gauge does not match.

## How to Crochet a Sunset Coral Reef Baby Blanket (Step by Step)
The blanket is created using a series of color blocks and alternating colors for a dynamic effect. 

### Foundation Chain
- CH 104 with Color 1.

### Row 1
- DC in the 4th CH from the hook, DC in each CH across. (102 DC)

### Row 2
- CH 3 (counts as first DC), turn. DC in each stitch across. (102 DC)

### Row 3
- Change to Color 2. CH 3, turn. DC in each stitch across. (102 DC)

### Row 4
- Change to Color 3. CH 3, turn. DC in each stitch across. (102 DC)

### Row 5
- Change to Color 4. CH 3, turn. DC in each stitch across. (102 DC)

### Repeat
- Repeat Rows 2–5 for a total of **7 color cycles**.

### Final Row
- Change to Color 1. CH 3, turn. DC in each stitch across. (102 DC)

### Edge
- SL ST in the top of the first CH of the final row, FO.

## How to Weave in Ends and Block
- **Weaving in Ends**: Use a tapestry needle to weave in all yarn ends securely.
- **Blocking**: Optionally block the blanket to achieve smooth edges and even dimensions.

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Baby Blanket
- Use stitch markers to keep track of rows or color changes.
- Count your stitches consistently to ensure an even width.
- Maintain a consistent tension to avoid ruffles or tight spots.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- "My blanket is too small." – Check your starting chain and ensure you're using the correct gauge.
- "The blanket has uneven edges." – This is often caused by inconsistent tension; make sure to maintain an even pull on your yarn.
- "Colors don't transition well." – Make sure to change colors carefully and ensure stitches are not too tight at the color change.

## How to Care for Your Baby Blanket
This blanket is machine washable on a gentle cycle and can be tumble dried on low. Avoid bleach to maintain the vibrant colors.

## FAQ
### How do I adjust the size of the blanket?
