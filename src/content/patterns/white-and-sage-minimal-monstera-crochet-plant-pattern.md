This White and Sage Minimal Monstera Crochet Plant pattern is a lovely addition to your home decor. It's a minimalist design featuring oversized leaves, perfect for brightening up a shelf or table. You'll be working with simple stitches like single crochet and double crochet, and soon you'll have a charming plant to admire.

This project is suitable for intermediates who are ready to take on a fun challenge. You'll learn how to shape leaves, add wire for support, and create a delightful plant that brings a touch of nature indoors.

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain  
- sc – single crochet  
- dc – double crochet  
- inc – increase (2 sc in same stitch)  
- dec – decrease (sc 2 together)  
- sl st – slip stitch  
- fphdc – front post half double crochet: yarn over, insert hook from the front around the post of the stitch below, yarn over and pull up, yarn over and pull through all 3 loops.

## Size and Gauge
- Finished height: 12 inches (30 cm)  
- Finished width: 10 inches (25 cm)  
- Gauge: 4 sc and 4 rows = 4 inches (10 cm). Measure gauge by crocheting a swatch of 10 × 10 cm.

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### Part 1: Leaves (Make 3)
The leaves are crocheted individually. 

**Leaf 1 - Large Leaf**  
1. With Sage Green, ch 10.  
2. Row 1: Sc in 2nd ch from hook, sc in next 7 ch, 3 sc in last ch; working on opposite side of foundation ch, sc in next 7 ch. (18 sts)  
3. Row 2: Ch 1, inc, sc in next 6 sts, inc, sc in next 6 sts, inc. (21 sts)  
4. Row 3: Ch 1, sc in next 2 sts, inc, sc in next 6 sts, inc, sc in next 6 sts, inc, sc in last 2 sts. (24 sts)  
5. Row 4: Ch 1, sc in next 3 sts, inc, sc in next 6 sts, inc, sc in next 6 sts, inc, sc in last 3 sts. (27 sts)  
6. Row 5: Ch 1, sc in next 4 sts, inc, sc in next 6 sts, inc, sc in next 6 sts, inc, sc in last 4 sts. (30 sts)  
7. Fasten off and leave a long tail for sewing.

**Leaf 2 - Medium Leaf**  
1. With Sage Green, ch 8.  
2. Row 1: Sc in 2nd ch from hook, sc in next 5 ch, 2 sc in last ch; working on opposite side of foundation ch, sc in next 5 ch. (14 sts)  
3. Repeat rows 2–5 from Leaf 1, with inc on last row only. (17 sts)  
4. Fasten off and leave a long tail for sewing.  

**Leaf 3 - Small Leaf**  
1. With Sage Green, ch 6.  
2. Row 1: Sc in 2nd ch from hook, sc in next 3 ch, 2 sc in last ch; working on opposite side of foundation ch, sc in next 3 ch. (10 sts)  
3. Repeat rows 2–5 from Leaf 1, with inc on last row only. (13 sts)  
4. Fasten off and leave a long tail for sewing.  

### Part 2: Assembly
Arrange your leaves.  
1. Shape each leaf as desired. Use a tapestry needle to thread the long tails through the base to form a curve. You can embellish with fphdc for veins if you wish.  
2. Insert floral wire into the base of each leaf for added stability and wrap it with green floral tape.  
3. Arrange leaves, starting with the largest at the back. Attach them to the pot with stuffing to secure them in place.  

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Monstera Plant
- Use stitch markers to keep track of your rows.
- Count your stitches regularly to avoid gaps or unevenness.
- Keep your tension consistent to help the plant maintain its shape.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- "My leaves are uneven." – Check your stitch count; make sure you’re following the pattern correctly.
- "The main plant is too floppy." – Make sure to insert the floral wire for support.
- "My tension is too tight." – Try relaxing your grip on the yarn as you work to keep stitches loose.

## How to Care for Your Monstera Plant
This crochet plant doesn't require much care! Hand wash with mild soap if it gets dirty and allow it to air dry.
