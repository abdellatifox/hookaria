## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain  
- sl st – slip stitch  
- sc – single crochet  
- dc – double crochet  
- hdc – half double crochet  
- sp – space  
- ( ) – indicates number of stitches in the indicated stitch  

## How to Make a Magic Ring
1. Wrap the yarn around your fingers to form a loop.
2. Insert your hook into the loop and yarn over.
3. Pull the yarn through the loop to create a slip knot.
4. Ch 3 to start your first round.

## Size and Gauge
- **Finished Size:** Approximately 48 × 60 in (122 × 152 cm)
- **Gauge:** 14 stitches and 7 rows = 4 in (10 cm) in hexagon pattern.  
To measure gauge, create a swatch using hexagons in both lavender and white. Adjust your hook size if needed.

## How to Crochet a Lavender Dreams Hexagon Throw (Step by Step)

### Hexagonal Motif
The hexagonal motif is the building block of the throw. Begin by making a magic ring.

1. **Round 1:** Ch 3 (counts as first dc), 11 dc in ring, join with sl st to the top of ch 3. (12 dc)
2. **Round 2:** Ch 3, dc in same stitch, *2 dc in each of the next 11 stitches; join. (24 dc)
3. **Round 3:** Ch 3, dc in same stitch, *dc in next st, 2 dc in next st; repeat from * around; join. (36 dc)
4. **Round 4:** Ch 3, dc in same stitch, *dc in next 2 sts, 2 dc in next st; repeat from * around; join. (48 dc)
5. **Fasten off.** 

Repeat to make **20 hexagons** in lavender and **10 hexagons** in white.

### Assembly
Now that you have your hexagons ready, let's piece them together.

- Lay out the hexagons in a pattern that pleases you, alternating between lavender and white.
- Join the hexagons using a slip stitch or whip stitch: 
  - Position hexagons in 5 rows of 6 hexagons.
  - Stitch through the sc edges, ensuring the sides fit perfectly together.

### Edging
Once you’ve joined all the hexagons, it’s time to finish the throw.

- Attach lavender yarn at any corner.
- **Round 1:** Ch 1, sc evenly around the edge, placing 3 sc in each corner to maintain the shape. Join with sl st.

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Throw
- Use stitch markers to keep track of rounds or important stitches.
- Count your stitches regularly to avoid mistakes.
- Maintain consistent tension to ensure even coverage.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- "My hexagons seem misshapen." – This may happen if you don’t count your stitches accurately. Always double-check your rounds.
- "My throw feels too tight." – If your stitches are too tight, try using a larger hook to achieve the desired look.
- "Some of my edges don't match." – Make sure you are joining the hexagons at the right points. Take your time to position them correctly.

## How to Care for Your Throw
Hand wash your blanket in cold water with a mild detergent. Lay it flat to dry to maintain its shape and avoid any stretching. If you've added a fleece backing, you can wash it on a gentle cycle.
