This Autumn Harvest Tote with Sunflower pattern is perfect for carrying your seasonal goodies or everyday essentials. Its beautiful sunflower applique brings a delightful touch of fall charm. This project uses basic stitches like double crochet and single crochet, making it enjoyable for crocheters.

This pattern is suitable for easy-level crocheters. You’ll learn how to make a sturdy tote bag while exploring fun color combinations and stitches.

## Why You'll Love This Tote
- Functional and stylish – use it for groceries, outings, or crafts.
- Quick to crochet – finish this project in about 5.5 hours.
- Colorful sunflower applique – add a personal touch to your bag.
- Perfect for gifting – a thoughtful handmade item for friends and family.

## Is This Pattern Good for Beginners?
Yes! This pattern features basic stitches and clear instructions. You'll have the chance to practice your skills while creating a lovely tote.

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- sl st – slip stitch
- sc – single crochet
- dc – double crochet
- hdc – half double crochet
- st – stitch
- sk – skip
- yo – yarn over

#### Special Stitch - Sunflower Applique
- Petal: (dc, ch 1, dc) in the same stitch (creates a cluster).

## Size and Gauge
- Finished size: 35.5 × 30.5 cm (14 × 12 in) with a base of 12.7 cm (5 in).
- Gauge: 12 dc and 7 rows = 10 cm (4 in).
- To check gauge: Make a swatch of 20 dc x 12 rows.

## Step 1: Tote Body
The tote body is worked flat.  
1. With Color A, ch 52.  
2. Row 1: dc in 4th ch from hook and in each across (50 dc).  
3. Rows 2–18: ch 3, turn, dc in each st across (50 dc).  
   - At the end of row 18, fasten off Color A.  
   - The tote body should measure 30.5 cm (12 in) tall.

## Step 2: Base
The base is crocheted in the round.  
4. Join Color A at the bottom corner of one side.  
5. Round 1: ch 1, sc around base, placing 3 sc in each corner (total: 62 sc), join with sl st to the first sc.  
6. Rounds 2–5: ch 1, sc in each st around (62 sc). After round 5, fasten off Color A.

## Step 3: Handles
The handles are worked flat as well.  
7. With Color B, ch 40.  
8. Row 1: hdc in the 3rd ch from the hook and in each across (38 hdc).  
9. Rows 2–5: ch 2, turn, hdc in each st across (38 hdc).  
   - Fasten off.  
   - Make another handle the same way.

## Step 4: Attaching Handles
The handles will attach to the top of the tote.  
10. Position handles 10 cm (4 in) from each edge on top of the tote opening. Use sl st to attach handles securely, ensuring even spacing.

## Step 5: Sunflower Applique
The sunflower is a fun embellishment.  
11. With Color C, ch 5, join to form a ring.  
12. Round 1: 12 dc in the ring, join with sl st (12 dc).  
13. Round 2: *(dc in next st, ch 1, dc in same st)*, repeat from * around (12 petals).  
    - Fasten off Color C. Change to Color D for the center.  
14. In the center of the flower, join Color D and sc in the center of the petals to secure. Fasten off.

## Step 6: Assembly
The final step is putting it all together.  
15. Sew the sunflower onto the front of the tote with a tapestry needle. Weave in any remaining ends.

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Tote
- Use stitch markers to keep track of important stitches or rounds.
- Count your stitches regularly to stay on track.
- Maintain consistent tension for an even finish.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- "My project is uneven." – This might happen from inconsistent tension. Regularly check your stitch counts and grip.
- "The base feels too tight." – You might be pulling your stitches too tight. Try crocheting a bit looser and reassessing your tension.
- "The sunflower is floppy." – This could indicate too few stitches or incorrect fastening. Ensure your flower is securely secured in the center.

## How to Care for Your Tote
For best results, hand wash the tote in cold water and lay it flat to dry. Consider applying fabric stiffener to help maintain its shape over time.

## FAQ
### How do I adjust the size of the tote?
You can increase or decrease the starting chains for the body. Just make sure to maintain the same stitch count across.

### Can I use different yarn weights?
Yes, different yarn weights will change the size and drape of the tote. Just be mindful of your gauge!

### How can I embellish the tote further?
You can add pockets, additional appliques, or personalized patches. Just make sure they are securely attached.
