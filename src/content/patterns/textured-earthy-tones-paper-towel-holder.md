## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- sl st – slip stitch
- dc – double crochet
- fdc – front post double crochet: yarn over, insert your hook around the front of the stitch below, yarn over and pull up a loop, yarn over and pull through two loops on the hook, yarn over and pull through the last two loops.
- bdc – back post double crochet: yarn over, insert your hook around the back of the stitch below and around to the front, yarn over and pull up a loop, yarn over and pull through two loops on the hook, yarn over and pull through the last two loops.

## The Pattern
The holder is worked in the round and uses alternating front and back post double crochet stitches for texture.

### Step 1: Create the Base
Round 1: Ch 4, join to form a ring. Ch 2 (this does not count as a stitch), work 12 dc into the ring. Join with sl st to the top of the first dc. (12 dc)

Round 2: Ch 2, *fdc in the next stitch, bdc in the next stitch; repeat from * around. Join with sl st to the top of the first fdc. (24 stitches)

Round 3: Ch 2, *fdc in the next st, bdc in the next st; repeat from * around. Join with sl st. (24 stitches)

Repeat Round 3 for 5 more rounds until you reach your desired height.

### Step 2: Add the Twisted Rope Effect
Round 10: Ch 1, work 1 sc in each stitch around. Join with sl st to the first sc. (24 sc)

Round 11: Ch 2, *fdc in the next st, bdc in the next st; repeat from * around. Join with sl st. (24 stitches)

### Step 3: Create the Hanging Loop
Round 12: Ch 10, sl st in the same st as the join to form a loop. Fasten off and cut the yarn. 

## Finishing Tip: Secure Your Ends
Weave in all ends with a tapestry needle. Make sure to secure the starting yarn tail and any ends from color changes. 

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Paper Towel Holder
- Use stitch markers to keep track of your rounds.
- Count your stitches after each round to prevent any mistakes.
- Maintain an even tension throughout your work for a polished look.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
"My edges are uneven." – This may happen if your stitch count varies. Count your stitches to ensure you have the right number every round.

"My holder is too tall." – If you want a shorter holder, simply stop working rounds earlier. Measure as you go to achieve your desired height.

"I can’t get the texture right." – This often comes from inconsistent tension. Practice working the front and back post stitches to achieve an even texture.

## How to Care for Your Paper Towel Holder
To keep your paper towel holder looking fresh, hand wash it with mild soap and lay flat to dry. Avoid bleach or harsh chemicals to preserve the yarn’s color and texture.
