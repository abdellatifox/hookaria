## Skill Level: Intermediate
This pattern is suitable for those familiar with basic stitches like single crochet (sc) and double crochet (dc). You will also learn how to create shell stitches and V-stitches, enhancing your crochet skills along the way.

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- sc – single crochet
- dc – double crochet
- sl st – slip stitch
- st(s) – stitch(es)
- sk – skip
- inc – increase (2 stitches in the same stitch)
- dec – decrease (2 stitches together)
- Shell Stitch – (5 dc in the same stitch)
- V-Stitch – (1 dc, ch 1, 1 dc in the same stitch)

## Size and Gauge
- **Finished Size:** Women’s size 8–10 (adjustable)
- **Measurements:** Approximately 25 cm (10 in) long
- **Gauge:** 16 stitches and 8 rows = 10 cm (4 in) in single crochet.

To check your gauge, make a swatch of 10 cm x 10 cm (4 in x 4 in) in single crochet. Adjust your hook size if necessary.

## How to Crochet the Sole (Step by Step)
The sole of the sandal is worked in the round for a comfortable fit.

### Foundation Round
1. Ch 10, sl st to the first ch to form a ring. (10 ch)

### Round 1
2. Ch 1, sc in the same st, sc in the next 8 ch, and 3 sc in the last ch. Work along the opposite side of the ch: sc in the next 8 ch, sl st to join. (20 sc)

### Round 2
3. Ch 1, *sc in next 9 sc, inc in next sc; repeat from * around, sl st to join. (22 sc)

### Round 3
4. Ch 1, *sc in next 10 sc, inc in next sc; repeat from * around, sl st to join. (24 sc)

### Round 4
5. Ch 1, sc in each sc around, sl st to join. (24 sc)

### Rounds 5–6
6. Repeat Round 4. (24 sc)

## How to Crochet the Straps (Step by Step)
The straps are added in the next round to hold the sandals securely on your feet.

### Joining the Straps
7. In the first st of the next round, ch 1, *dc in next st, ch 4, sk 2, dc in next st; repeat from * around. You should have a total of 6–8 ch spaces, depending on foot size. Sl st to join.

### Strap Creation
8. In each ch 4 space, work 5 dc to create a shell stitch, then ch 1 to separate. Repeat around.

### Finishing Straps
9. At the last ch space, sc to join and fasten off.

## How to Embellish Your Sandals (Step by Step)
Adding decoration gives your sandals a refined final touch.

### Gold Embellishment
10. With gold yarn, create 3 chains of 5 for small loops. Attach one at each strap side and one at the center.

### Finish with Buttons
11. Sew the gold buttons over the center of each strap loop.

## Assembly & Finishing
Weave in all yarn ends using a tapestry needle. Be sure to block the sandals to set their shape and achieve a neat finish. For added sparkle, consider attaching beads along the straps.

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Crochet Wedding Sandals
- Use stitch markers to keep track of your rounds as you work.
- Count your stitches to maintain the correct stitch count and avoid uneven edges.
- Maintain consistent tension for neat, even stitches throughout your work.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- "My stitches are too tight." – This often happens with new tension. Try crocheting with a larger hook.
- "The sandals don't fit." – Adjust the strap length accordingly if the initial size is off.
- "My project looks uneven." – This may stem from losing count. Use stitch markers to help remember where you are.

## How to Care for Your Crochet Wedding Sandals
Hand wash in cool water and lay flat to dry. Avoid wringing the yarn to keep the sandals looking their best.

### FAQ
### How can I resize the sandals?
You can adjust the size by adding or removing stitches in the initial rounds of the sole. Make sure to confirm your gauge for the best fit.

### Can I use different yarn for this project?
Yes! Just ensure that the yarn weight is similar to DK/light weight, and check your gauge.

### What if I don't have beads for embellishment?
That's completely fine! The sandals will still look beautiful without them. You can always add them later if you choose.
