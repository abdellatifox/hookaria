## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain  
- sc – single crochet  
- sl st – slip stitch  
- dc – double crochet  
- sp – space  
- inc – increase (2 sc in the same st)  
- daisy stitch – (To make a daisy flower)  
  1. Make a magic ring.  
  2. Ch 2, then work 12 dc into the ring.  
  3. Pull the tail to close the ring, sl st to join.  

## How to Make a Magic Ring
1. Wrap the yarn around your fingers to form a loop.  
2. Insert your hook into the loop and yarn over.  
3. Pull the yarn through the loop on your hook.  
4. Ch 2 (this counts as your first stitch) and work your stitches into the ring.

## Size and Gauge

## How to Crochet a Pastel Rainbow Daisy Bracelet (Step by Step)

### Part 1: Making the Daisies
The daisies are created using the daisy stitch.  
1. Daisy 1 (Light Pink):  
   - Make a magic ring.  
   - Ch 2, 12 dc into the ring. Pull tight, sl st to join. (12 dc)  
2. Daisy 2 (Mint Green):  
   - Repeat step 1 with Mint Green yarn. (12 dc)  
3. Daisy 3 (Lavender):  
   - Repeat step 1 with Lavender yarn. (12 dc)  
4. Daisy 4 (Pale Yellow):  
   - Repeat step 1 with Pale Yellow yarn. (12 dc)  
5. Daisy 5 (White):  
   - Repeat step 1 with White yarn. (12 dc)  
6. Leave tails long for stitching flowers later.

### Part 2: Creating the Bracelet Base
The bracelet base is simple and sturdy.  
7. Using Light Pink yarn, chain 60 (or adjust to desired wrist size).  
8. Row 1: Sc in the 2nd ch from hook and in each ch across. (59 sc)  
9. Row 2: Ch 1, turn. Sc in each sc across. (59 sc)  
10. Repeat Row 2 until the strip measures 2.5 cm (1 in) wide, about 8 rows.  
11. Fasten off, leaving a 30 cm (12 in) tail for sewing.

### Part 3: Attaching Daisies
Now we’ll attach the delightful daisies to the bracelet.  
12. Position the daisies evenly along one side of the bracelet base.  
13. Sew each daisy to the bracelet using the tails left from making them.  
14. Space the daisies about 2.5 cm (1 in) apart.  

### Part 4: Finishing the Bracelet
Finishing touches complete our bracelet.  
15. Sew one side of the elastic cord to the end of the bracelet base, pulling gently to create some stretch.  
16. Sew the button to the opposite end of the bracelet base.  
17. Weave in all loose ends using a tapestry needle.

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Bracelet
- Use stitch markers to keep track of your daisies' placement.
- Count your stitches as you work to ensure even spacing.
- Check your tension to make sure your stitches are consistent.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- My project is uneven. – This often happens from inconsistent tension. Try to keep your tension steady throughout.
- The daisies are not sitting flat. – Ensure you’re sewing them on securely and not pulling the yarn too tight.
- My bracelet is too small. – If that’s the case, remember to adjust the starting chain to fit your wrist size.

## How to Care for Your Bracelet
To keep your bracelet looking fresh, hand wash it in cold water and lay it flat to dry. Avoid wringing it out, as this can alter its shape.

## FAQ
### How do I resize the bracelet?

### How can I add more daisies?
You can add more daisies by simply increasing the number of daisies you make and space them out evenly along the bracelet base.
