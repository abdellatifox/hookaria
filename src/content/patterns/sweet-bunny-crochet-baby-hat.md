## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- sl st – slip stitch
- sc – single crochet
- hdc – half double crochet: yarn over, insert hook into stitch, yarn over again, pull through all three loops on the hook.
- dc – double crochet: yarn over, insert hook into stitch, yarn over again, pull through (three loops on the hook), yarn over again, pull through two loops, yarn over again, pull through the last two loops.
- inc – increase
- BLO – back loop only
- sp – space

## Size and Gauge
- Finished Size: 38–40 cm (15–16 in) circumference, fits babies approximately 0–6 months.
- Gauge: 12 dc and 7 rows = 10 cm (4 in).

Make sure to check your gauge by creating a small swatch. If your tension is different, adjust your hook size accordingly to achieve the desired measurements.

## The Pattern
The hat is crocheted in the round. 

### Step 1: Create the Base
Round 1: Ch 3 (this counts as your first dc), work 11 dc into the third ch from the hook. Join with sl st to the top of the first dc. (12 dc)

Round 2: Ch 2 (does not count as a stitch), *2 dc in the next stitch; repeat from * around. Join with sl st to the top of the first dc. (24 dc)

Round 3: Ch 2, *dc in the next stitch, 2 dc in the next stitch; repeat from * around. Join with sl st to the top of the first dc. (36 dc)

Round 4: Ch 2, *dc in the next 2 stitches, 2 dc in the next stitch; repeat from * around. Join with sl st to the top of the first dc. (48 dc)

### Step 2: Work the Body
Round 5: Ch 1, sc in the same stitch, sc in each stitch around. Join with sl st to the first sc. (48 sc)

Rounds 6–12: Repeat Round 5.

### Step 3: Add the Ears
For each ear, work in rows.
**Ear 1:** (using contrasting white yarn) 
Row 1: Ch 15, hdc in the second ch from the hook, hdc in the next 13 chs. (14 hdc)

Row 2: Ch 1, turn, hdc in each stitch across. (14 hdc)

Row 3: Ch 1, turn, hdc in the first 5 stitches, hdc in the next 4 stitches, hdc in the last 5 stitches. (14 hdc)

Row 4: Ch 1, turn, hdc in each stitch across. Fasten off, leaving a long tail for sewing.

**Ear 2:** Repeat the same steps as Ear 1.

## How to Join Your Ears
Position the ears on top of the hat where desired. Using the tail from each ear, sew the ears securely to the hat. 

## Finishing Tip: Secure Your Ends
Fasten off your work and weave in all ends using your tapestry needle to keep the hat looking neat.

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Baby Hat
- Use a stitch marker to keep track of the beginning of each round.
- Count your stitches every few rounds to ensure you aren’t increasing accidentally.
- Maintain consistent tension to help your stitches look even throughout the hat.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
"My hat is curling up." – This can happen if your tension is too tight. Try loosening your grip on the yarn or using a larger hook next time.

"My ears are uneven." – This usually happens from not counting stitches. Always check your counts after each row to ensure both ears match.

"My hat is too large." – You may have used a larger hook than recommended or crocheted too loosely. Refer to the gauge and adjust your hook size as necessary.

## How to Care for Your Baby Hat
For best results, hand wash the hat in cold water and lay it flat to dry. This will help maintain its shape and softness.

### FAQ
### How do I resize this hat for larger babies?
To resize, simply increase the number of rounds in the body after Round 5. You can also add more chains to the beginning round to expand the base.

### Can I use different yarn for this hat?
Yes! You can use any soft yarn for this pattern, but ensure it is a similar weight to maintain the size.

### How long will it take to make this hat?
It should take about 2–3 hours, depending on your speed and familiarity with the stitches.
