This Whale and Wave Crochet Ocean Tote is a delightful project that combines fun design with practicality. With a charming whale and wave motif, it’s perfect for beach trips or casual outings. We’ll take you through the enjoyable process of crafting this spacious tote using basic crochet stitches, along with some intermediate techniques.

This pattern is suitable for those with a bit of experience in crocheting. You’ll learn how to create a textured body, work with color changes, and apply fun embellishments for a unique touch.

## How to Crochet the Whale and Wave Tote (Step by Step)

### Body of Tote
The body of the tote is crocheted in rows. 
1. **Foundation Chain**: Ch 70.
2. **Row 1**: Dc in 3rd ch from hook and in each ch across. Turn. (68 dc)
3. **Row 2**: Ch 2 (counts as first dc), dc in each dc across. Turn. (68 dc)
4. **Rows 3–10**: Repeat Row 2 for 8 more rows. (68 dc)
5. **Row 11**: Ch 2, dc in the first 20 dc, change to light blue (Color D) and dc in the next 28 dc, change to white (Color B) and dc in the last 20 dc. Turn. (68 dc)
6. **Row 12**: Ch 2, dc in the first 20 dc, change to Color D and dc in the next 28 dc, change to Color B and dc in the last 20 dc. Turn. (68 dc)
7. **Row 13**: Repeat Row 12. (68 dc)
8. **Row 14**: Ch 2, dc in all dc across. Turn. (68 dc)
9. **Rows 15–20**: Repeat Row 12 for 6 more rows, maintaining colors as established.
10. **Row 21**: Change to gray (Color C), repeat Row 2 for 6 rows. (68 dc)
11. **Row 22**: Ch 1, sc in each dc across. Turn. (68 sc)
12. **Rows 23–24**: Repeat Row 22 for 2 more rows. (68 sc)

### Creating the Waves and Whale Appliqué
We'll make the wave and whale to embellish the tote. 
1. **Wave Part**: Ch 14 with light blue (Color D).
2. **Row 1**: Hdc in 2nd ch from hook, hdc in next 12, 3 hdc in last ch. Work down the other side (12 hdc), sl st to join with starting ch. Turn.
3. **Rows 2–3**: Ch 1, hdc in each hdc across, sl st to join at the end.
4. **Whale Body** (with gray (Color C)):
   - Ch 10.
   - Row 1: Hdc in 2nd ch, hdc in next 7. (8 hdc)
   - Row 2: Ch 1, turn, hdc in each. (8 hdc)
   - Row 3: Repeat Row 2 until you have 5 rows. Fold in half and sew edges together. Use white yarn to embroider eyes.

### Assembly
Now let's put the tote together. 
1. Place the whale and wave appliqués on one side of the tote body.
2. Sew them down using a tapestry needle and the corresponding yarn colors.
3. Fold the body in half and slip stitch the sides together.
4. Create a strap by chaining 90, working a long single crochet down the length, then attaching each end to the top of the tote on each side.

## Beginner Tips
- Use stitch markers to keep track of color changes.
- Count your stitches to ensure evenness and avoid mistakes.
- Maintain consistent tension for a neat finish.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- "My project is uneven." – This often happens due to inconsistent tension. Focus on keeping tension steady throughout.
- "I changed colors and it doesn't look right." – Ensure you’re following the color change instructions closely; it can help to keep the colors visible as you work.
- "The item is too tight." – This may be the result of using too small of a hook. Check your gauge and adjust as needed.

## How to Care for Your Tote
To keep your tote looking fresh, machine wash it on a cold cycle and lay it flat to dry. Avoid direct sunlight to prevent fading.
