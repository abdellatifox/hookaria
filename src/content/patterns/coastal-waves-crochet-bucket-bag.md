This Coastal Waves crochet bucket bag is perfect for summer outings. It features a stylish design with a rounded base and gently flaring sides, making it both roomy and chic. Crafted with an ocean-blue and sandy beige yarn, this bag showcases a wave stitch pattern that beautifully mimics the gentle undulations of sea waves.

This pattern is suitable for intermediate crocheters looking to enhance their project with a unique texture and shape. You’ll practice your crochet skills while making a functional accessory that you can use every day.

## Skill Level: Intermediate

You should be familiar with basic stitches such as chain, single crochet, and double crochet, as well as the wave stitch. A new technique introduced in this pattern is the wave stitch.

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)

- ch – chain
- sl st – slip stitch
- sc – single crochet
- dc – double crochet
- hdc – half double crochet
- inc – increase
- sp – space
- BLO – back loop only

## How to Crochet the Wave Stitch

1. **Foundation**: Start by making a foundation chain that is a multiple of 3.
2. **Row 1**: Ch 3 (counts as first dc), dc in the next two stitches, *skip 2 stitches, work 3 dc in the next stitch*. Repeat from * across to the last 2 stitches, skip 1, and dc in the last. 
3. **Row 2**: Ch 1, sc in the first dc, *(ch 2, skip 3 dc, sc in the next 3 dc)*. Repeat from * across to the last stitch, finish with a sc in the last dc.
4. **Repeat**: Continue alternating Rows 1 and 2 to create the wave effect.

## Size and Gauge

- Finished Size: 30 × 25 cm (12 × 10 in)
- Gauge: 15 dc and 12 rows = 10 cm (4 in)

To ensure your bag is the correct size, always check your gauge. Adjust your hook size if necessary.

## Step 1: Create the Base

The base is worked in the round to form a sturdy bottom. 

Round 1: Ch 4, join with sl st to form a ring. Ch 1, work 10 sc into the ring. Join with sl st to the first sc. (10 sc)

Round 2: Ch 1, *2 sc in each sc around*. Join with sl st to the first sc. (20 sc)

Round 3: Ch 1, *sc in next sc, 2 sc in next sc; repeat from * around. Join with sl st to the first sc. (30 sc)

Round 4: Ch 1, *sc in next 2 sc, 2 sc in next sc; repeat from * around. Join with sl st to the first sc. (40 sc) 

### What’s happening?
We are creating a circular base for the bag. As we increase, the base will expand, allowing it to hold more at the sides.

## Step 2: Crochet the Sides

The sides will be made with a combination of the wave stitch.

Round 5: Ch 3 (counts as first dc), dc in the next 2 sc, *skip 2 sc, work 3 dc in the next sc; repeat from * around. Join with sl st to the top of ch 3. (40 dc)

Round 6: Ch 1, sc in the first dc, *ch 2, skip 3 dc, sc in the next 3 dc; repeat from * around. Join with sl st to first sc.

Rounds 7–12: Repeat Rounds 5 and 6 until you reach a height of 25 cm.

## How to Join Your Granny Squares

At the end of your desired height, fasten off and weave in all ends. For a neat join, position your stitches where they align best. 

## Step 3: Adding the Handles

For the handles, we will make two sturdy straps.

Handle: Ch 51 (or desired length), hdc in the second ch from hook and in each ch across. Fasten off.

Repeat for the second handle. Attach the handles to the top edge by sewing them securely.

## Finishing Tip: Secure Your Ends

Weave in any remaining ends using a tapestry needle to keep your bag tidy and professional.

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Bucket Bag

- Use stitch markers – mark each round to keep your stitches even.
- Count your stitches – this helps ensure the right shape and size.
- Maintain consistent tension – try to keep your stitches uniform for a polished look.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)

"My bag is too floppy." – This could be due to using a lighter yarn. Make sure to choose medium-weight fiber for sturdiness.

"My edges are uneven." – This might happen if you don’t count stitches accurately. Double-check each thing as you go.

"Stitch pattern isn't defined." – To achieve the full texture of the wave stitch, ensure you are keeping your tension consistent.

## How to Care for Your Bucket Bag

To keep your bucket bag looking its best, hand wash it in cold water with mild detergent. Lay it flat to dry.
