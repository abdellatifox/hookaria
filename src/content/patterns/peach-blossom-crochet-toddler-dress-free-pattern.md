This pattern is suitable for beginners looking to expand their skills and for those with a little experience in crochet. You'll learn how to crochet a complete dress, including the body, skirt, and edging.

## Is This Pattern Good for Beginners?
Yes! This pattern uses basic stitches and simple construction techniques. You'll follow straightforward steps, making it a great project for those new to crocheting.

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain  
- dc – double crochet  
- sc – single crochet  
- sl st – slip stitch  
- st(s) – stitch(es)  
- inc – increase (2 stitches in same stitch)  
- dec – decrease (2 stitches together)  
- fpdc – front post double crochet: yarn over, then insert your hook around the front post of the stitch below, yarn over again, pull through, yarn over, pull through two loops, yarn over, pull through two remaining loops.  

## Size and Gauge
- Finished dimensions: 
  - Chest: 61 cm (24 in)  
  - Length: 56 cm (22 in)  
- Size Measurements:
  - 18 months: Chest 56 cm (22 in), Length 52 cm (20.5 in)  
  - 3T: Chest 66 cm (26 in), Length 61 cm (24 in)  
- Gauge: 14 dc and 8 rows = 10 cm (4 in) in double crochet. Check your gauge by crocheting a swatch of 10 dc in 10 rows. Adjust your hook size if necessary.

## How to Crochet the Body
The body of the dress is crocheted in rows, starting from the top down.

1. **With Color A**, ch 70 (70 sts).  
2. Row 1: dc in 4th ch from hook (this counts as your first dc), dc in next 66 ch (68 total stitches).  
3. Row 2: ch 3, turn, dc in the same st, dc in next 67 sts (69 sts).  
4. Row 3: ch 3, turn, *fpdc around next st*; repeat across (69 sts).  
5. Rows 4–15: Repeat Row 3 until the piece measures 35.5 cm (14 in) from the foundation ch.  
6. Row 16: ch 1, sc across (69 sts).  
7. **Change to Color B**: Repeat Row 2 (68 sts).  
8. **Change to Color C**: Repeat Row 2 (68 sts).  
9. **Change to Color A**: Repeat Row 2 (68 sts).  
10. Row 17: ch 3, turn, dc in next 67 (68 sts).  

## How to Crochet the Skirt
The skirt is worked by creating scallops.

11. **With Color A**: ch 1, sc in the same st, (sc, ch 1, sc) in each end st across (68 scallops).  
12. Row 18: ch 1, turn, (sc, ch 1, sc) in each ch-1 space across (68 scallops).  
13. Rows 19–22: Repeat Row 18 two more times for added length.  

## How to Crochet the Edging
The edging gives a finished look to the neck and sleeves.

14. **With Color A**, switch to 5 mm hook: ch 1, sc evenly around the neck and sleeves, sl st to the top of the first sc to join. Fasten off.  

## Assembly & Finishing
If using a lining, pin the lining fabric inside the dress and sew with a running stitch around the armholes and hem. Weave in all ends. Attach a button at the back if desired for added closure. For embellishment, sew a fabric flower on the front or use leftover yarn for a small crochet flower.

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Dress
- Use stitch markers to keep track of your rows.  
- Count your stitches regularly to avoid any gaps.  
- Maintain consistent tension for a polished look.  

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- "My dress is too small." – Check your gauge; you may need a larger hook.  
- "The skirt is not flaring." – Ensure you are making the scallops correctly; try to space them evenly.  
- "I have extra stitches." – Count your stitches at the end of each row to catch mistakes early.  

## How to Care for Your Dress
Machine wash on a gentle cycle and lay flat to dry. This will help maintain the shape of the dress.
