## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- dc – double crochet
- sl st – slip stitch
- sc – single crochet
- st(s) – stitch(es)
- dc2tog – double crochet two together (decrease)

**Special Stitch – Gradient Change**: To create the ombre effect, change colors every 4 rows, making an even transition from light peach to dark peach.

## Size and Gauge
- **Finished Dimensions**:
  - 0-3 months: Chest 46 cm, Length 25 cm, Sleeve 18 cm
  - 6-12 months: Chest 51 cm, Length 28 cm, Sleeve 20 cm
  - 12-18 months: Chest 56 cm, Length 30.5 cm, Sleeve 23 cm
- **Gauge**: 16 dc and 11 rows = 10 cm

To check your gauge, work a swatch with the main stitch pattern. If it doesn’t match, adjust your hook size until the gauge is met.

## How to Crochet a Baby Hooded Cardigan (Step by Step)
The body of the cardigan is crocheted flat, then the sleeves and hood are made separately before assembly.

### Body
1. **Foundation Chain**: Chain 60 (66, 72).
2. **Row 1**: Dc in the 4th ch from the hook and in each ch across. Turn. (58, 64, 70 sts)
3. **Row 2 - 4**: Ch 3 (counts as first dc), dc in each st across. Change to Medium Peach after Row 4. Turn. (58, 64, 70 sts)
4. **Row 5 - 8**: Repeat Row 2 with Medium Peach.
5. **Row 9 - 12**: Change to Dark Peach and repeat Row 2. 
6. **Row 13**: Ch 1, sc in each st across. Fasten off. (58, 64, 70 sts)

### Sleeves (make 2)
1. **Foundation Chain**: Chain 30 (34, 38).
2. **Row 1**: Dc in the 4th ch from hook and in each ch across. Turn. (28, 32, 36 sts)
3. **Row 2 - 4**: Ch 3, dc in each st across. Change to Medium Peach after Row 4. 
4. **Row 5 - 6**: Repeat Row 2 with Medium Peach.
5. **Row 7 - 8**: Change to Dark Peach and repeat Row 2. 
6. **Row 9**: Ch 1, sc in each st across. Fasten off. (28, 32, 36 sts)

### Hood
1. **Foundation Chain**: Chain 30 (34, 38).
2. **Row 1**: Dc in the 4th ch from the hook and in each ch across. Turn. (28, 32, 36 sts)
3. **Row 2 - 3**: Ch 3, dc in each st across. Change to Medium Peach after Row 3.
4. **Row 4 - 5**: Repeat Row 2 with Medium Peach.
5. **Row 6 - 7**: Change to Dark Peach and repeat Row 2. 
6. **Row 8**: Ch 1, sc in each st across. Fasten off. (28, 32, 36 sts)

## Assembly & Finishing
1. **Joining Body and Sleeves**: Lay the body flat, pin the sleeves to the side edges, aligning the center of the sleeve with the upper edge of the body. 
2. **Sewing**: Using a tapestry needle, sew the sleeves to the body with slip stitches or sc.
3. **Attach Hood**: Sew the long edge of the hood to the back of the body, centered. 
4. **Border**: Sc around the entire front opening of the cardigan for a polished edge. 
5. **Button Closure**: Attach a button to the right side, and create a buttonhole on the left side by skipping 2 sc and chaining 2.
6. **Weave in ends**: Use a tapestry needle to weave in all loose ends. Block if desired.

## Beginner Tips
- Use stitch markers to keep track of your rows.
- Count your stitches regularly to avoid mistakes.
- Maintain consistent tension for even stitches.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- "My project is too big." – Check your gauge and use the appropriate hook size for your tension.
- "The color change looks uneven." – Ensure to change colors according to the pattern for a smooth gradient.
- "My stitches aren’t even." – Practice maintaining a consistent tension and count while working.

## How to Care for Your Baby Hooded Cardigan
Hand wash in cold water and lay flat to dry to maintain the integrity of the yarn and the shape of the cardigan.

## FAQ
### How can I customize the cardigan size?
You can adjust the foundation chain and the number of rows to fit a custom size. Just remember that this may change the overall proportion of the cardigan.

### What yarn can I use for this pattern?
Any worsted weight (category 4) yarn works well. You can experiment with different textures and colors to make it unique!

### How do I make the buttonhole?
Skip 2 single crochets where you want the buttonhole and chain 2 before continuing to ensure that the button fits securely.
