## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain  
- sc – single crochet  
- dc – double crochet  
- sl st – slip stitch  
- sp – space  
- inc – increase (2 dc in the same st)  
- dec – decrease (sc two stitches together)

## Size and Gauge
- Finished Size: 25 × 20 cm (10 × 8 in) at base and height
- Gauge: 12 dc and 8 rows = 10 cm (4 in) in double crochet

Gauge helps ensure your finished item is the correct size. To check gauge, make a small swatch in double crochet. If your gauge doesn’t match, try adjusting your hook size.

## How to Crochet the Autumn Harvest Floral Bucket Bag (Step by Step)

### Part 1: Bag Base
The base of the bag is worked in the round using Color A. 
1. With Color A, ch 4, join to form a ring.
2. Round 1: Ch 3 (counts as first dc), 11 dc in the ring, join with sl st to the top of ch 3. (12 sts)
3. Round 2: Ch 3, dc in the same st, 2 dc in each st around, join. (24 sts)
4. Round 3: Ch 3, dc in the same st, *dc in next st, 2 dc in next st; repeat from * around, join. (36 sts)
5. Round 4: Ch 3, dc in the same st, *dc in next 2 sts, 2 dc in next st; repeat from * around, join. (48 sts)
6. Rounds 5–8: Ch 3, dc in each st around, join. (48 sts each round)

### Part 2: Bag Sides
Next, we’ll create the sides of the bag.
7. Round 9: Ch 1, sc in each st around, join. (48 sts)
8. Rounds 10–14: Ch 1, sc in each st around, join. (48 sts)

### Part 3: Bag Edging
The edging adds a nice finish to your bag.
9. Round 15: Ch 1, sc in same st, *ch 1, skip next st, sc in next st; repeat from * around, join. (24 ch-1 spaces)

### Floral Appliqués (Make 3)
These flowers will beautifully embellish your bag.
1. With Color C, ch 5, join to form a ring.
2. Round 1: Ch 3, 11 dc in the ring, join with sl st. (12 sts)
3. Round 2: Ch 1, *sc in next st, 2 sc in next st; repeat from * around, join with sl st. (18 sts)
4. Fasten off, leaving a long tail for sewing.

### Part 4: Assembly & Finishing
Let’s put everything together for the final touches.
1. Sew the floral appliqués onto the front of the bag using the tapestry needle and yarn tail.
2. Attach the leather or cotton strap through the bag sides, securing with a knot.
3. Weave in all ends and block your bag to shape.

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Bucket Bag
- Use stitch markers to keep track of your rounds.
- Count your stitches frequently to ensure you're staying on track.
- Pay attention to your tension to keep your stitches even.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- "My bag is too small!" – This may happen if your gauge was off. Check your gauge and adjust your hook size.
- "The sides of my bag are uneven." – This usually means you may have skipped stitches. Count stitches as you work to ensure consistency.
- "My flower appliqués don't lay flat." – This can be fixed with blocking; pin them into shape while wet to dry.

## How to Care for Your Bucket Bag
Hand wash your bag in cold water and lay it flat to dry. This will help maintain its shape and colors for seasons to come.
