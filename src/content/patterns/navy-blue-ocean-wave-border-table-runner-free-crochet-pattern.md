## Skill Level: Intermediate
This pattern uses double crochet (dc), slip stitch (sl st), single crochet (sc), and includes chain (ch) techniques. The challenging parts include the wave stitch, but you'll feel accomplished once completed!

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- dc – double crochet
- sk – skip
- sp – space
- st(s) – stitch(es)
- *(…)* – repeat instructions within the parentheses as indicated.

## Size and Gauge
- Finished size: 35.6 × 122 cm (14 × 48 in)
- Gauge: 12 dc and 8 rows = 10 cm (4 in) in double crochet.
- To achieve the correct gauge, work a swatch of 20 dc for 10 rows.

## How to Crochet a Navy Blue Ocean Wave Border Table Runner (Step by Step)

### Body of Table Runner
The body of the table runner is crocheted in rows.  
1. **Foundation Row**: Ch 130. Join with a sl st to the first ch to form a ring, ensuring it’s not twisted.
2. **Row 1**: Ch 3 (counts as first dc), dc in each ch around (130 dc). Join with a sl st to the top of ch-3.
3. **Row 2**: Ch 3, dc in next dc, *ch 1, sk 1 dc, 2 dc in next dc; repeat from * around. Join with a sl st to top of ch-3.
4. **Row 3**: Ch 3, dc in next dc, *ch 1, sk 1 sp, 2 dc in next dc; repeat from * around. Join.
5. **Row 4**: Repeat Row 2. 
6. **Row 5**: Repeat Row 3. 
7. **Row 6**: Repeat Row 4. 
8. **Row 7**: Repeat Row 3. 
9. **Row 8**: Ch 1, sc in each dc and ch-1 sp around. Join. 
10. **Row 9**: Repeat Row 1.
11. Continue this pattern until the runner measures 122 cm (48 in) long (approximately 40 rows).

### Ocean Wave Border
The border adds a stunning finish to your runner.  
1. **Row 1**: With navy blue, ch 3, dc in each st around. Join.
2. **Row 2**: Ch 1, sc in each dc across the top, at corners make 3 sc for a smooth turn.
3. **Row 3**: Ch 3, dc in each sc around. Join.
4. **Row 4**: Ch 3, *dc in next 3 stitches, ch 1, sk 1 st; repeat from * around. Join.
5. **Row 5**: Ch 1, sc in each dc and ch-1 sp around. Join.
6. **Row 6**: Repeat Row 3. 
7. **Row 7**: Sl st to next ch-1 sp, ch 3, *dc in next 3 stitches, ch 1, sk 1 st; repeat from * around. Join.
8. **Row 8**: Repeat Row 5. 
9. **Row 9**: Repeat Row 3. 

## Finishing
- Weave in all yarn ends using a tapestry needle.
- Block the table runner using blocking pins to shape it evenly along the edges.
- Lay flat to dry or use steam blocking as preferred.

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Table Runner
- Use stitch markers to keep track of your rows.
- Count your stitches regularly to ensure your edges stay straight.
- Keep a consistent tension to maintain an even look.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- "My project is uneven." – This can happen due to inconsistent tension. Focus on relaxing your grip.
- "The corners look messy." – Ensure to place 3 sc stitches in the corners for a smooth finish when joining.
- "My waves don't look right." – Make sure to follow the pattern closely and count your repeated stitches accurately.

## How to Care for Your Table Runner
Hand wash in cold water, lay flat to dry, and do not bleach for best results.
