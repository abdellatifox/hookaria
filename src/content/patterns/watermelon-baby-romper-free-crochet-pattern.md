## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- sc – single crochet
- dc – double crochet
- sl st – slip stitch
- st(s) – stitch(es)
- inc – increase (2 stitches in the same stitch)
- dec – decrease (join 2 stitches together)

## Size and Gauge
- Finished Size: 6 months (will fit 3–6 months)
- Chest Circumference: 50 cm (20 in)
- Leg openings: 20 cm (8 in)
- Length from shoulder to bottom: 45 cm (18 in)
- Additional Sizes: 
  - 12 months: Chest Circumference: 55 cm (22 in), Length: 51 cm (20 in)
  - 18 months: Chest Circumference: 61 cm (24 in), Length: 56 cm (22 in)
- Gauge: 14 dc and 10 rows = 10 cm (4 in)  
To check gauge, crochet a swatch of 14 double crochets across and measure. If necessary, adjust your hook size.

## How to Crochet a Watermelon Baby Romper (Step by Step)

### Body
The body of the romper is worked in rows.  
1. With Watermelon Pink, ch 60.  
2. **Row 1:** dc in 4th ch from hook (counts as first dc), dc in next 55 ch, 2 dc in last ch. Turn. (58 sts)  
3. **Row 2:** ch 3 (counts as first dc), dc in next 56, 2 dc in last st. Turn. (60 sts)  
4. **Rows 3–10:** Repeat Row 2. You should have 60 stitches by Row 10.

### Armholes
Next, we create the armholes.  
5. **Row 11:** ch 3, dc in next 23 sts, ch 12 (skip next 12 for armhole), dc in next 24 sts. Turn. (47 sts)  
6. **Row 12:** ch 3, dc in next 23 sts, ch 12 (do not work in this chain), dc in next 24. Turn. (47 sts)

### Legs
Let’s move on to the legs.  
7. **Row 13:** ch 1, sc in first 23 sts, 2 sc in next st, ch 12 (for leg), skip 12 ch, 2 sc in next st, sc in remaining 23 sts. Turn. (48 sts)  
8. **Row 14:** ch 1, sc in same st and next 22, 2 sc in next st, (ch 12, skip over leg opening), 2 sc in next st, sc in remaining 22 sts. Turn. (48 sts)

### Start Straps
Now, we’ll crochet the straps.  
9. **Row 15:** Using Dark Green, ch 40, sc in 2nd ch from hook and each ch across. Fasten off.  
10. Attach the straps to the corners of armholes using sl st.

### Trim
We’ll finish the romper with a crochet trim.  
11. With the 4.0 mm hook and White, attach yarn to the bottom hem of the romper.  
12. **Round 1:** sc evenly around the bottom of the romper, join with sl st to first sc.  
13. **Round 2:** ch 1, *sc in next st, 2 sc in next st; repeat from * around, join with sl st.

## Finishing the Assembly
- Sew buttons onto the straps for closure at the front.  
- Weave in all ends with a tapestry needle.  
- Block the romper to shape.

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Watermelon Baby Romper
- Use stitch markers to keep track of your rows.
- Count your stitches after every row to avoid mistakes.
- Check your tension regularly for consistent stitches.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- "My project is uneven." – This often happens from skipping stitches or not counting. Take your time and check your work regularly.
- "The item is too tight." – If your stitches feel snug, try using a larger hook size.
- "My corners are turning out ruffled." – This could be due to too many increases. Make sure to follow the stitch counts closely.

## How to Care for Your Watermelon Baby Romper
Hand wash cold and lay flat to dry. Avoid wringing or twisting to keep the shape intact.
