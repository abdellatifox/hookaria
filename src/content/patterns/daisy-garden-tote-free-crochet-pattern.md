## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- dc – double crochet
- sc – single crochet
- sl st – slip stitch
- st(s) – stitch(es)
- sp – space
- * – repeat instructions between asterisks as directed

## Size and Gauge
- **Finished Size:** 35.5 cm × 30.5 cm × 10 cm (14 in × 12 in × 4 in)
- **Gauge:** 14 dc and 8 rows = 10 cm (4 in)  
To check gauge, measure the stitches and rows in the main stitch pattern on a flat piece of fabric. Adjust your hook size if your gauge doesn’t match.

## How to Crochet a Daisy Garden Tote (Step by Step)

### Tote Body
The tote body is worked in rows from the bottom up. 
1. With Green yarn, ch 49.
2. Row 1: Sc in 2nd ch from hook and in each ch across (48 sc).
3. Row 2: Ch 2 (counts as dc), turn, dc in each st across (48 dc).
4. Row 3–10: Repeat Row 2 (8 rows total).
5. Row 11: Ch 1, turn, sc in each st across (48 sc).
6. Row 12: Ch 2, turn, dc in each st across (48 dc).
7. Row 13: Ch 1, turn, sc in each st across (48 sc).
8. Row 14: Ch 2, turn, dc in each st across (48 dc).
9. Row 15: Ch 1, turn, sc in each st across (48 sc).
10. Row 16: Ch 2, turn, dc in each st across (48 dc).
11. Rows 17–22: Repeat Row 2 (6 rows total).
12. Finish off, leaving a long tail for sewing, approximately 30 cm (12 in).

### Daisy Appliqué (Make 6)
These cute daisies will add a lovely touch to your tote.
1. **Daisy Center:**
   - With Yellow yarn, ch 4, sl st to form a ring.
   - Round 1: Ch 1, 8 sc in the ring. Join with sl st to the first sc (8 sc). Finish off.
  
2. **Daisy Petals:**
   - With White yarn, attach yarn to any stitch of the yellow center.
   - Round 1: (Ch 3, sl st in the same stitch) repeat 8 times around (8 petals). Finish off.
  
3. Weave in ends and sew a daisy onto the front side of the tote body using the tapestry needle.

### Assembly
The assembly brings your tote together beautifully.
1. Fold the tote body in half, right sides together; seam the sides and bottom together using sc. Leave the top open.
2. Turn the tote right side out.
3. Attach the wooden handles on both sides to secure.

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Daisy Garden Tote
- Use stitch markers to keep track of your rows and stitches.
- Count your stitches often to ensure a correct shape.
- Keep your tension consistent for even stitching across the tote.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- "My project is uneven." – This can happen with inconsistent tension. Practice keeping an even hold on your yarn.
- "The bag feels too tight." – This often results from too few stitches in your base chain. Ensure you’re counting accurately when chaining.
- "The daisy petals are floppy." – This may mean that the petals need to be pulled slightly tighter when stitching them on. Be gentle, as too much force can distort them.

## How to Care for Your Daisy Garden Tote
Hand wash with mild detergent and lay flat to dry for best results. This will keep your tote looking fresh and new for a long time.

## FAQ
### How long will it take to make this tote?
It will take about 6 hours total: approximately 5 hours for the tote body and assembly, and about 30 minutes per daisy appliqué.

### Can I add a lining to my tote?
Absolutely! A light blue lining adds stability and a nice pop of color.

### What should I do if my gauge is off?
If your gauge does not match, adjust your hook size. A smaller hook will make tighter stitches while a larger one will create looser stitches.
