Its unique shape and beautiful color combination make it suitable for both elegant evenings and casual outings. You’ll be using basic stitches like double crochet (DC) and single crochet (SC), making it fun and enjoyable to create.

## Size and Gauge
- Finished Bag: 25 × 18 cm (10 × 7 in) with a 5 cm (2 in) base depth.
- Gauge: 12 DC and 8 rows = 10 cm (4 in) in double crochet.

## How to Crochet a Half Moon Bag (Step by Step)

### Body of Bag
The body of the bag is worked in rows starting from the foundation chain.  
1. **Foundation Chain:** Ch 36.  
2. **Row 1:** Dc in 4th ch from hook, dc across to the end. (34 dc)  
3. **Row 2:** Ch 3 (counts as dc), turn, dc in each dc across to the end. (34 dc)  
4. **Row 3–12:** Repeat Row 2. (34 dc each row)  
5. **Row 13:** Change to Color B, ch 3, dc in each dc across. (34 dc)  
6. **Row 14:** Ch 3, turn, dc in each dc across. (34 dc)  
7. **Row 15:** Change back to Color A, ch 3, dc in each dc across. (34 dc)  
8. **Row 16–18:** Repeat Row 2. (34 dc each row)  
9. **Row 19:** Ch 1, sc in first dc, sc across to the end. (34 sc)  

### Base of Bag
The base is smaller and rounded to fit the bag's contour.  
10. **Foundation Chain:** Ch 16.  
11. **Row 1:** Sc in 2nd ch from hook, sc in next 14 chs, ch 2, turn. (15 sc)  
12. **Row 2:** Sc in next 14 sc, 2 sc in last sc. (16 sc)  
13. **Row 3:** Sc in each sc across. (16 sc)  
14. **Row 4:** Ch 1, sc in first sc, 2 sc in last sc. (17 sc)  
15. **Row 5–6:** Repeat Row 3. (17 sc each row)  

### Assembly
To construct the bag, align the top edges carefully.  
16. Align the top edges of the bag body with the base.  
17. Use a whip stitch to sew the base to the bag body, ensuring it is secure all around.  
18. Weave in the ends with a tapestry needle.  

### Lining (Optional)
Adding a lining gives your bag a professional touch.  
19. Cut lining fabric to size, approximately 45 × 30 cm (18 × 12 in).  
20. Fold fabric in half, sew the sides together, leaving the top open.  
21. Insert lining into the bag and secure with pins, sewing along the top edge of the bag.  

### Closure
Finish off your bag with a snap closure.  
22. Sew a magnetic snap closure to the inside flap of the bag and the corresponding position on the body.  

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Half Moon Bag
- Use stitch markers to keep track of your rounds.
- Count your stitches after each row to ensure accuracy.
- Maintain consistent tension for an even finish.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- "My stitches are too tight." – This may be caused by using the wrong hook size; try going one size up for a looser gauge.
- "The bag is not the right size." – If you need to adjust the size, add or subtract chains in the foundation chain for a larger or smaller bag.
- "It looks uneven." – Check that you are working the same number of stitches in each row; counting will help.

## How to Care for Your Half Moon Bag
Hand wash in cold water and lay flat to dry to keep your bag looking its best.
