The design features beautiful floral motifs that look like peonies, and it’s perfect for any occasion. We'll be working with basic stitches like double crochet and single crochet, making it an enjoyable process.

This pattern is suitable for intermediate crocheters. You'll strengthen your skills as we create and assemble lovely floral motifs, learning how to join them effectively for a stunning table runner.

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain  
- dc – double crochet  
- sl st – slip stitch  
- sc – single crochet  
- st(s) – stitch(es)  
- tr – treble crochet  
- ( ) – work the instructions in the parentheses into the same stitch or space   
- [ ] – work the instructions in the brackets into the same stitch or across multiple stitches 

## Size and Gauge
- Finished size: Approximately 38 × 114 cm (15 × 45 in)  
- Gauge: 16 dc and 8 rows = 10 cm (4 in) in the main stitch pattern.  
To check gauge, make a swatch of 24 double crochets by 12 rows in the main stitch pattern. If it does not match, adjust your hook size accordingly.

## How to Crochet the Floral Motifs (Step by Step)
The floral motifs are crocheted in rounds. Here’s how to create them:

### Motif (Make 9)
1. With Color A, make a magic ring.  
2. Ch 3 (counts as 1 dc), work 11 dc into the ring. Pull the ring tight. Sl st to top of ch-3. (12 sts)  
3. Round 2: Ch 3 (counts as 1 dc), 1 dc in the same st. *2 dc in next st.* Repeat from * to * around. Sl st to top of ch-3. (24 sts)  
4. Round 3: Ch 3, 1 dc in the next 2 sts. *2 dc in next st, 1 dc in next 2 sts.* Repeat from * to * around. Sl st to top of ch-3. (36 sts)  
5. Round 4: Ch 3, 1 dc in the next 3 sts. *2 dc in next st, 1 dc in next 3 sts.* Repeat from * to * around. Sl st to top of ch-3. (48 sts)  
6. Round 5: Change to Color B. Ch 1, sc in same st. *Ch 2, skip 1 st, sc in next st.* Repeat from * to * around. Sl st to first sc. (24 ch-2 spaces)  
7. Round 6: In each ch-2 space, work (1 dc, 1 tr, 1 dc) to create the peony petals. Sl st to first dc.  

**Beginner tip:** It’s helpful to keep track of the number of stitches using a stitch marker.

### Assemble Motifs (9 pieces)
1. Join the motifs in a straight line using Color A. Sl st between the adjoining petals in corresponding spaces.  
2. Leave an open space (18 cm / 7 in) between the motifs for spacing.  

### Final Row
1. Once all motifs are joined, with Color C, sc around the entire table runner.  
2. Work 3 sc in each corner. Sl st to join at starting chain.  

## Assembly & Finishing
- Weave in all ends using a tapestry needle.  
- Block the table runner if desired to shape the motifs evenly.  

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Table Runner
- Use stitch markers to keep track of rounds.
- Count your stitches regularly to avoid losing track.
- Maintain consistent tension for a beautiful finish.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- "My project is uneven." – This often happens from inconsistent tension. Try to keep your stitches uniform.
- "The motifs are not connecting well." – Ensure you're working the slip stitches securely between motifs.
- "There's a gap in the center of my motifs." – Make sure to pull the magic ring tight when you finish the first round.

## How to Care for Your Table Runner
Hand wash or machine wash on a delicate cycle; lay flat to dry.
