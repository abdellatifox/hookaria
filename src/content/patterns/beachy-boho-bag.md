This Beachy Boho Bag crochet pattern is perfect for your summer outings. Its oversized, slouchy shape and soft, sandy beige cotton yarn create a relaxed style that’s both functional and fashionable. You’ll love the mix of openwork lattice stitches and solid sections, adding delightful texture to your creation.

This pattern is suitable for intermediate crocheters who want to enhance their skills in working with open stitches and constructing bags. If you’re comfortable with basic stitches, this project will give you a great opportunity to practice and refine those techniques.

## Why You'll Love This Beachy Boho Bag
- Functional design – spacious enough to carry all your summer essentials.
- Stylish look – the mix of openwork and solid stitches adds texture and interest.
- Easy to personalize – feel free to adjust the size or colors to fit your style.
- Relaxed construction – the slouchy shape makes for a quick and enjoyable project.

## Is This Pattern Good for Beginners?
Yes! While some skills are required, the basic stitches and straightforward construction make it accessible. You'll become more confident as you practice the techniques included.

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- sl st – slip stitch
- sc – single crochet
- dc – double crochet
- sk – skip
- sp – space
- inc – increase
- BLO – back loop only

## Size and Gauge
- Finished size: Approximately 40 × 40 cm (16 × 16 in) when laid flat.
- Gauge: 12 dc and 8 rows = 10 cm; check your gauge to ensure your bag is the correct size.

## Step 1: Create the Base
The base of the bag is worked in a circular fashion.  
Round 1: Ch 4, join with sl st to form a ring.  
Round 2: Ch 3 (counts as first dc), work 11 dc into ring. Join with sl st to top of ch 3. (12 dc)  
Round 3: Ch 3, *dc in next dc, inc in next dc; repeat from * around. Join. (18 dc)  
Round 4: Ch 3, *dc in next 2 dc, inc in next dc; repeat from * around. Join. (24 dc)  
Round 5: Ch 3, *dc in next 3 dc, inc in next dc; repeat from * around. Join. (30 dc)  
Round 6: Ch 3, *dc in next 4 dc, inc in next dc; repeat from * around. Join. (36 dc)

## Step 2: Create the Body
The body is worked using a mix of openwork and solid stitches.  
Row 1: Ch 1, sc in each dc around. Join.  
Row 2: Ch 3, *dc in next sc, ch 1, sk next sc; repeat from * around. Join.  
Rows 3–10: Repeat Row 1.  
Rows 11–20: Repeat Row 2.

## Step 3: Add Fringe and Strap
To add fringe along the top edge, cut lengths of yarn approximately 15 cm (6 in) long. Attach them by folding them in half and pulling the ends through any of the open stitches around the top edge. Trim as necessary.

For the strap, chain 90. Work 1 sc in the second chain from hook, then sc in each chain across. Fasten off.

## How to Join Your Granny Squares
To attach the strap to the bag, use a tapestry needle to sew the ends to the inside of the bag, ensuring it is securely fastened.

## Finishing Tip: Secure Your Ends
Be sure to weave in all ends with your tapestry needle to create a polished finish. Block lightly if necessary to maintain the shape.

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Beachy Boho Bag
- Use stitch markers to keep track of your rounds.
- Count your stitches regularly to avoid any hiccups.
- Keep a consistent tension for even stitches.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
"My bag is too small." – This may happen due to incorrect gauge. Always check your gauge before starting and adjust your hook size if necessary.  
"My stitches are uneven." – This is often due to tension inconsistencies. Try to maintain the same tension throughout.  
"The strap feels weak." – Ensure your strap is securely attached and consider reinforcing it with extra stitches if needed.

## How to Care for Your Beachy Boho Bag
To clean your bag, hand wash it in cold water with mild detergent. Avoid wringing it out; instead, lay it flat to dry to maintain its shape.

### FAQ

The number of stitches can vary based on the pattern, but for this bag, starting with 12 dc in your base is a good foundation.

### Can I use a different yarn?
Yes! You can substitute with any similar weight cotton yarn; just ensure it has a similar gauge for the correct size.
