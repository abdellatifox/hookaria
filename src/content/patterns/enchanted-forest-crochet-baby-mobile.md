## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- sl st – slip stitch
- sc – single crochet
- dc – double crochet
- hdc – half double crochet: yarn over, insert hook into stitch, yarn over again, pull through, yarn over and pull through all three loops on the hook.
- inc – increase: work 2 stitches in the same stitch.
- BLO – back loop only
- magic ring – create a ring by making a loop and working into it to start a piece.

## How to Make a Magic Ring
1. Make a loop with the yarn, leaving a tail.
2. Insert your hook through the loop from front to back.
3. Yarn over and pull through the loop to form a ring.
4. Work your stitches into the ring as specified in your pattern.

## Size and Gauge
- Finished size: Approximately 30 cm (12 in) in diameter including animals.
- Gauge: 14 dc and 8 rows = 10 cm (4 in).

Now that we've gone through the basics, let’s jump into making our whimsical mobile!

## Step 1: Crochet the Fox
The fox is made using orange and brown yarn.

Round 1: Make a magic ring, work 6 sc into ring. Join with sl st to the first sc. (6 sc)

Round 2: Ch 1, inc in each sc around. Join with sl st to the first sc. (12 sc)

Round 3: Ch 1, *sc in next sc, inc*; repeat from * around. Join with sl st to the first sc. (18 sc)

Round 4: Ch 1, *sc in next 2 sc, inc*; repeat from * around. Join with sl st to the first sc. (24 sc)

Round 5: Ch 1, sc in each sc around. Join with sl st to the first sc. (24 sc)

Rounds 6–7: Repeat Round 5. 

Round 8: Ch 1, *sc in next 2 sc, dec*; repeat from * around. Join with sl st to first sc. (18 sc)

Round 9: Ch 1, *sc in next sc, dec*; repeat from * around. Join with sl st to first sc. (12 sc)

Round 10: Ch 1, sc in each around. Fasten off, leaving a long tail for sewing.

Add eyes and nose using black yarn.

## Step 2: Crochet the Owl
The owl features lovely shades of cream and brown.

Round 1: Make a magic ring, work 8 sc into ring. Join with sl st to the first sc. (8 sc)

Round 2: Ch 1, inc in each sc around. Join with sl st to the first sc. (16 sc)

## Step 3: Crochet the Deer
The deer combines soft brown and cream colors.

Round 1: Make a magic ring, work 6 sc into ring. Join with sl st to first sc. (6 sc)

Round 2: Ch 1, inc in each sc around. Join with sl st to the first sc. (12 sc)

Round 3: Ch 1, *sc in next sc, inc*; repeat from * around. Join with sl st to the first sc. (18 sc)

Rounds 4–5: Ch 1, sc in each around. Join with sl st to first sc. (18 sc)

Round 6: Ch 1, *sc in next 2 sc, dec*; repeat from * around. Join with sl st to the first sc. (12 sc)

Fasten off, leaving a long tail for sewing, and add antlers using brown yarn.

## How to Assemble the Mobile
1. Cut a length of yarn about 30 cm (12 in) for each animal.
2. Use the tails of the creatures to secure them to a circular frame.
3. Space them evenly around the frame, using additional yarn as needed.
4. Ensure to tie knots securely to prevent them from falling.

## Finishing Tip: Secure Your Ends
Weave in any loose ends with a tapestry needle to keep your mobile tidy and neat.

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Baby Mobile
- Use stitch markers – they help keep track of your rounds.
- Count your stitches regularly – it prevents mistakes from piling up.
- Keep tension consistent – this will make your pieces look uniform.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
"My animals are lopsided." – This often happens if rows are uneven. Check your stitch count and make adjustments as you go.

"My yarn is tangling." – Ensure you’re not working too tightly. Looser tension can help the yarn flow better.

"My magic ring is too loose." – Practice holding the starting yarn tight as you work your first stitches to create a more secure circle.

## How to Care for Your Baby Mobile
To keep your mobile looking its best, gently wipe it with a damp cloth. Avoid soaking it, as the yarn may stretch or lose shape.
