This Forest Jewel Tone Shell Crossbody crochet pattern is both stylish and functional! Featuring beautiful shell stitches in rich jewel tones, this handbag is perfect for adding a splash of vibrant color to your daily outfits while keeping your essentials handy. You'll mainly work with chains, single crochet, and double crochet as you create this lovely piece.

This intermediate-level project is ideal for those who have some experience with crochet but are looking to challenge themselves. You'll see your skills grow as you work through the shell stitch design and assemble a practical accessory.

## Why You'll Love This Crossbody Bag
- Unique design – the shell stitch brings lovely texture and visual interest.
- Versatile colors – mix and match your favorite jewel tones to make it truly yours.
- Functional and stylish – perfect for everyday use while keeping your hands free.
- Achievable in just a few hours – make this bag in a weekend or in quick sessions throughout the week.

## Is This Pattern Good for Beginners?
Yes! While this pattern is categorized as intermediate due to the shell stitch, the steps are clearly outlined and manageable. If you’re ready for a bit of a challenge, you’ll find it rewarding and fun to make.

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- **ch** – chain  
- **sl st** – slip stitch  
- **sc** – single crochet  
- **dc** – double crochet  
- **Shell stitch** – (5 dc) all in the same stitch or space  
- **inc** – increase (2 sts in the same stitch)  
- **dec** – decrease (1 stitch over the next 2 sts)  

## Size and Gauge
- **Finished Size:** Approximately 25 × 20 cm (10 × 8 in)  
- **Gauge:** 14 shells and 12 rows in shell stitch pattern = 10 cm (4 in).  
To check your gauge, make a small swatch of shell stitches (4 shells x 6 rows). Adjust your hook size if the gauge does not match.

## How to Crochet a Forest Jewel Tone Shell Crossbody (Step by Step)
The body of the bag is created first using a foundation chain and shell stitches in alternating colors.

### Body of the Bag
1. **Foundation Chain:** Using Color A (Forest Green), ch 65.  
2. **Row 1:** Sc in 2nd ch from hook, sc in next 2 ch, *shell in next ch (5 dc), sc in next 3 ch; repeat from * across to last 2 ch, sc in last ch. (Total 16 shells)  
3. **Row 2:** Ch 2 (counts as first dc), turn, *shell in the center of the next shell; sc in next 3 dc; repeat from * across to last shell, shell in last shell. (Total 16 shells)  
4. **Rows 3–11:** Repeat Row 2, alternating colors B (Amethyst Purple), C (Sapphire Blue), and D (Ruby Red) for each subsequent row:  
   - Row 3: Color B   
   - Row 4: Color C  
   - Row 5: Color D  
   - Row 6: Color A  
   - Row 7: Color B  
   - Row 8: Color C  
   - Row 9: Color D  
   - Row 10: Color A  
   - Row 11: Color B  
5. **Row 12:** Change back to Color A. Ch 2, turn, sc in each stitch across (65 sc).  

### Sides of the Bag
6. **Next Rows (Sides):** Ch 1, turn. Sc in each stitch across (65 sc).  
7. **Continue this until you have completed 8 rows of sc (or reach your desired height).**

### Finishing the Bag
8. **Fold:** Fold the bag in half with the right sides together.  
9. **Join Sides:** Using a slip stitch, join both sides up to the bottom of the bag, leaving the top open.  
10. **Create Lining (optional):** Cut a fabric piece to match the dimensions of the finished bag. Pin the lining inside the bag and hand sew it in place.

### Assembly & Finishing
11. **Add Magnetic Snap:** Attach the magnetic snap button to the top edges of the bag, carefully measuring center before placement.  
12. **Add Strap:** Attach the adjustable crossbody strap, connecting it to both sides of the bag.  

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Crossbody Bag
- Use stitch markers to keep track of your rows, especially when working with multiple colors.
- Count your stitches regularly to avoid losing track.
- Keep your tension consistent to ensure even shells.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- "My project is uneven." – This can happen if your tension varies. Try to keep it consistent throughout.
- "The shells don't look right." – Make sure you're adding the correct number of double crochets in each shell.
- "The bag is too small." – If you want a larger bag, start with a longer foundation chain in multiples of 8.

## How to Care for Your Crossbody Bag
Hand wash your bag in cool water and lay it flat to dry. This will help maintain its shape and keep those vibrant colors looking bright.

## FAQ
### How do I change colors in this pattern?
When you switch colors, simply finish the last stitch of the previous color and use the new color to complete the stitch. It will create a smooth transition.

### Can I use fewer colors?
Absolutely! Feel free to use any combination of colors that you like, even if you simplify it down to one or two.

### How do I make the bag larger?
To make a larger bag, add more chains to the foundation chain in multiples of 8. This will increase the shell count and width.
