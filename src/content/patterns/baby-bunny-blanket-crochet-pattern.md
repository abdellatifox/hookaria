This adorable Baby Bunny Blanket features a soft, plush texture with a lovely bunny head design. The pastel color palette combines light pink and mint green, creating a soothing and cheerful atmosphere for any nursery. The blanket is intricately designed with cute bunny ears and a gentle bobble stitch pattern, making it a perfect cozy companion for little ones.

This pattern is suitable for crocheters with some basic skills. If you’re just starting out, don’t worry! You'll get to learn and practice new stitches while creating a beautiful item that baby's will love.

## Why You'll Love This Baby Bunny Blanket
- Cuddly and soft – it's made from plush yarn that feels great against the skin.
- Charming design – the bunny head and ears make it visually appealing.
- Quick and easy – works up fast, perfect for gifts or cuddle time.
- Versatile colors – customize with different pastel shades to suit any nursery.

## Skill Level: Easy
To make this blanket, you should know how to:
- ch – chain
- sc – single crochet
- dc – double crochet
- bobble – complete this fun stitch for the bunny design.

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- sl st – slip stitch
- sc – single crochet
- dc – double crochet
- bobble – yarn over, insert hook in stitch, yarn over and pull through, yarn over and pull through two loops twice, yarn over and pull through all three loops.

## Size and Gauge
- Finished size: approximately 80 × 100 cm (32 × 39 in)
- Gauge: 12 dc and 10 rows = 10 cm (4 in)
To check gauge, make a small swatch before starting your blanket to ensure accuracy.

## Step 1: Create the Base
The blanket is worked in rows.
Ch 108.
Row 1: Sc in the second ch from the hook and in each ch across. Turn. (107 sc)
Row 2: Ch 2 (counts as first dc), dc in the next stitch, *bobble in the next stitch, dc in the next stitch; repeat from * across to the last stitch, dc in the last stitch. Turn. (107 stitches)
Row 3: Ch 1, sc in each stitch across. Turn. (107 sc)
Repeat Rows 2 and 3 until the blanket measures 80 cm (31.5 in), ending with a Row 3.

## Step 2: Create Bunny Ears
Making the ears will add to the charm of the blanket.
Row 1: Ch 15.
Row 2: Sc in the second ch from the hook and in each ch across. Turn. (14 sc)
Row 3: Ch 2 (counts as first dc), dc in the next stitch, *bobble in the next stitch, dc in the next stitch; repeat from * across until last stitch, dc in the last stitch. Turn. (14 stitches)
Row 4: Ch 1, sc in each stitch across. Turn. (14 sc)
Repeat Rows 3 and 4 until the ear measures around 22 cm (8.5 in) long. Fasten off, leaving a long tail for sewing.

Make another ear following the same steps.

## Step 3: Assemble the Bunny Face
Position the ears on the blanket where you want the bunny face to be.
Sew the ears securely on the top edge of the blanket using the long tails.

For the bunny face, you can embroider simple eyes and a little nose using black and pink yarn, or use safety eyes suitable for babies.

## How to Join Your Bunny Ears
Place the two ears on the blanket about 10 cm (4 in) apart, making sure they look even. Use a tapestry needle and the yarn tails to stitch the base of each ear down to the blanket securely.

## Finishing Tip: Secure Your Ends
Once you’ve attached the ears and embroidered the face, fasten off any remaining yarn. Weave in all ends with your tapestry needle to ensure a clean finish.

## Beginner Tips
- Use a stitch marker to keep track of your rows.
- Count your stitches often to ensure you haven’t missed any.
- Maintain consistent tension for a uniform look.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
"My blanket looks uneven." – This might be due to changing tension. Keep your tension consistent and check your gauge.
"I can’t get the bobbles to stand out." – Make sure you're pulling up enough yarn when creating your bobble. Practice will help!
"My stitches are too tight." – Try using a larger hook size for looser stitches or relax your grip.

## How to Care for Your Baby Bunny Blanket
To keep this blanket soft and cuddly, it’s best to hand wash it in cool water and lay it flat to dry. Avoid wringing it out to retain its shape.

### FAQ
### How can I resize this blanket?
You can increase the starting chain in multiples of 2 to make it wider or add more rows to make it longer.

### What yarn can I use instead of plush?
You can use worsted weight yarn for a lighter feel or choose acrylic blends for easy washing.

### How long will this blanket take to make?
It typically takes about 8–12 hours to complete, depending on your speed.
