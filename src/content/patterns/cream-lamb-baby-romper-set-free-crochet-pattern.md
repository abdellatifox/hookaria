## Is This Pattern Good for Beginners?

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- dc – double crochet
- sc – single crochet
- st – stitch
- sl st – slip stitch
- inc – increase (2 stitches in the same st)
- dec – decrease (2 stitches together)
- FO – finish off

**Lamb Appliqué:**
1. Begin with a magic ring.
2. Round 1: ch 1, 6 sc in the ring, pull tight, and join with sl st (6 sts).
3. Round 2: (inc) repeat around (12 sts).
4. Round 3: (sc in next st, inc) repeat around (18 sts).
5. Round 4: (sc in next 2 sts, inc) repeat around (24 sts).
6. FO, leaving a long tail for sewing.

## Size and Gauge
- **Finished Size:** 0-3 months (approximately 20" in chest and 18" in length)
- **Gauge:** 16 dc and 10 rows = 4 inches (10 cm). To check, create a swatch of 20 dc. Use a smaller hook if your swatch is larger than the gauge, and a larger hook if smaller.

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### Romper Body
The body of the romper is worked in rows.  
1. **With Yarn A,** Ch 46.
2. Row 1: Starting from the 2nd ch from hook, dc in each ch across (45 dc).
3. Row 2: Ch 3, turn, dc in each st across (45 dc).
4. Row 3–20: Repeat Row 2 for 18 more rows.  
   *After Row 20, the piece should measure approximately 18" long.*
5. **Front Panel:** 
   - Repeat Rows 1–20 in the same manner, but stop at Row 16 to create the neck opening.  
   *At this point, the width of the front panel should match the back.*
6. **Shape Neck:** 
   - Row 17: Ch 3, dc in the next 15 sts, ch 9, skip next 15 sts, dc in last 15 sts (30 dc, ch 9).
7. Row 18: Ch 3, dc in the first 30 dc, then dc in the ch space (31 dc).
8. Row 19: Repeat Row 2 for 8 more rows.
9. **Assembly of the Body:** Fold the front panel over the back panel and join the sides through the shoulder seam with sl st.

### Legs
1. Attach Yarn A to the bottom of the romper.
2. Ch 1, sc around the leg opening, join with sl st (approx. 36 sc).
3. Round 2: Ch 1, sc in each st around (36 sc).
4. Repeat Round 2 until leg length measures 3" long.
5. FO and repeat for the other leg.

### Lamb Appliqué (make 2)
1. Follow the Lamb Appliqué instructions to create two lambs.
2. Attach safety eyes to each lamb using Yarn C as pupils.
3. Stuff lightly with polyester fiberfill.
4. Sew lambs onto the romper's front panel, positioning them towards the sides.

### Hat
1. **With Yarn A,** Ch 30.
2. Row 1: Sc in 2nd ch from hook, sc across (29 sc).
3. Row 2: Ch 1, turn, sc across (29 sc) for every row until the piece measures 6" long.
4. Shape brim by changing to Yarn B:
   - Ch 1, sc around (29 sc).
   - Repeat this for 3 rows.
5. Add lambs to the front of the hat as you did for the romper.

## Assembly & Finishing
1. Weave in all ends and secure with a tapestry needle.
2. Block all pieces as desired for a clean finish.
3. Sew the button at the back for closure.

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Romper Set
- Use stitch markers to mark the beginning of your rows.
- Count your stitches after each row to ensure accuracy.
- Practice maintaining consistent tension, especially when changing stitches.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- "My project is too tight." – This could be due to your tension. Try relaxing your grip when crocheting or switch to a larger hook.
- "The romper is uneven." – Check you’ve maintained the same number of stitches for each row and correct any missed or added stitches.
- "My lambs don’t look even." – If they appear different, use the same stitch count and technique when making each one.

## How to Care for Your Romper Set
Hand wash in cold water and lay flat to dry. Avoid wringing or twisting the fabric to maintain its shape and softness.

### FAQ

### How can I resize the romper?
To make it larger, increase the foundation chain in multiples of 5 and add or subtract rows accordingly.

### How do I make the lambs different colors?
You can definitely play with colors! Simply substitute different colors for Yarn A, B, and C.

The total estimated time is about 8 hours, depending on your crochet speed.
