## Skill Level: Easy
This pattern is great for beginners and those with some experience. You’ll be using basic stitches: chain (ch), single crochet (sc), and slip stitch (sl st). The rose motifs and leaves bring a fun challenge without being overwhelming.

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- sc – single crochet
- sl st – slip stitch
- rsc – reverse single crochet
- st(s) – stitch(es)
- inc – increase (2 sc in the same st)

## Size and Gauge
- Finished length: approximately 7 inches (18 cm) long; adjustable up to 8 inches (20 cm).
- Finished width: approximately 1 inch (2.5 cm).
- Gauge: 24 stitches and 30 rows = 10 cm (4 in) using single crochet. To measure gauge, crochet a 4-inch (10 cm) square using the main stitch pattern.

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### Part 1: Rose Motifs (Make 4)
The rose motifs are made in the round for a lovely floral touch.

1. **Start with Rose Pink yarn.**
2. Ch 4, join with sl st to form a ring.
3. Round 1: Ch 1, 10 sc into the ring, join with sl st. (10 sts)
4. Round 2: Ch 1, *sc in next st, inc in next st* repeat from * to * around, join with sl st. (15 sts)
5. Round 3: Ch 1, *sc in next 2 sts, inc in next st* repeat from * to * around, join with sl st. (20 sts)
6. Round 4: Ch 1, *sc in next 3 sts, inc in next st* repeat from * to * around, join with sl st. (25 sts)
7. Finish off, leaving a long tail for sewing.

**To create petals:**
8. Lay out the petals slightly overlapping at the ends and sew them together using the tail.

### Part 2: Leaves (Make 2)
Leaves add a fresh look to your roses.

1. **Start with Emerald Green yarn.**
2. Ch 5, join with sl st to form a ring.
3. Round 1: Ch 1, 8 sc into the ring, join with sl st. (8 sts)
4. Round 2: Ch 1, *sc in next st, inc in next st* repeat from * to * around, join with sl st. (12 sts)
5. Round 3: Ch 1, *sc in next st, inc in next st* repeat from * to * around. (18 sts)
6. Finish off, leaving a long tail for sewing.

**To shape the leaf:** 
7. Fold the leaf in half and seam along one side using the tail from the starting chain.

### Part 3: Bracelet Base
The base of the bracelet is simple and fun to crochet.

1. **Using Cream yarn, create a chain:**
2. Ch 25 for a 7-inch (18 cm) bracelet.
3. Row 1: Sc in each ch across. (25 sts)
4. Row 2: Ch 1, turn, sc in each st across. (25 sts)
5. Repeat Row 2 until you have 1 inch (2.5 cm) tall.
6. Finish off, leaving a long tail.

### Assembly
1. **Attach the rose motifs:**
   - Sew the roses evenly spaced along the bracelet base.
  
2. **Attach the leaves:**
   - Position the leaves under each rose motif, sewing through both the rose and the bracelet base.

3. **Add charms:**
   - Use jump rings to attach charms to desired locations on the bracelet.

4. **Attach the clasp:**
   - Use the last tail end from the bracelet to sew one half of the clasp at one end; attach the other half to the opposite end.

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Bracelet
- Use stitch markers to keep track of your rounds and rows easily.
- Count your stitches after each round to ensure you have the correct number.
- Pay attention to your tension; practice ensures consistent stitches.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- "My project is uneven." – This may be due to inconsistent tension; try to keep your yarn tension steady as you work.
- "The rose motifs look flat." – Ensure you're creating them in the round and following the increases; adding an extra round can help shape them better.
- "The bracelet is too tight." – If this happens, consider using a larger hook next time, or adjust the chain length for a looser fit.

## How to Care for Your Bracelet
For the best care, gently hand wash the bracelet in cold water and lay it flat to dry. Avoid twisting or wringing it out.
