## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- sc – single crochet
- dc – double crochet
- sl st – slip stitch
- st(s) – stitch(es)
- rep – repeat
- Tapestry Crochet Technique – Alternating between colors for a wave effect by working over the unused yarn.

## How to Crochet the Ocean Wave Tapestry Tote Bag (Step by Step)
### Body of the Bag
The body of the bag is crocheted flat in rows.  
1. **Foundation Row:** Ch 36. (36 sts)  
2. **Row 1:** Sc in second ch from hook and in each ch across. Turn. (35 sts)  
3. **Row 2:** Ch 1, sc in each st across. Change to Seafoam Green in last st. Turn. (35 sts)  
4. **Row 3:** Ch 1, sc in each st across, carrying Ocean Blue for the next row. Turn. (35 sts)  
5. **Row 4:** Ch 2, dc into the same st, *ch 1, skip next st, dc in next st*; repeat from * to * across, ending with ch 1, skip last st, dc into last st. (35 dc)  
6. **Row 5:** Ch 1, *sc in ch-1 space, sc in next st*; repeat from * to * across. Change to White in last st. Turn. (35 sts)  
7. **Rows 6–15:** Repeat Rows 2–5 until you have 15 total rows. You will end on Row 5 with Seafoam Green.

### Create the Bag Sides
The sides are continued from the body to create the wave pattern.  
8. **Row 16:** Continue with the tapestry method. Ch 1, sc in the first two sts with Ocean Blue, switch to Seafoam Green, sc in next 31 sts, switch back to Ocean Blue for last 2 sts. Turn. (35 sts)  
9. **Rows 17–30:** Repeat Row 16, alternating colors to create the wave stitch pattern.  
10. End off, leaving a long tail for sewing the sides.

### Assembly
The assembly brings your beautiful work together.  
11. **Body Assembly:** Fold the piece in half, aligning the edges. Using the long tail from Row 30, whip stitch the sides together. Ensure that the wave designs match up.  
12. **Top Edging:** Join White yarn to the top edge of the bag. Ch 1, sc in the same st, sc around the opening, placing 1 sc in each stitch and 2 into the corners, join with sl st. (Approximately 60 sts)  
13. **Straps:**  
    - Ch 50.  
    - Row 1: Sc in second ch from hook and across. (49 sts)  
    - Row 2: Ch 1, sc in each st across.  
    - Repeat Row 2 for 24 rows or desired strap width.  
    - End off and create a second strap identical to the first.  
14. **Attach Straps:** Position straps about 5 in (12.7 cm) apart on each side of the bag. Use a slip stitch to attach securely.

## Beginner Tips
- Use stitch markers to keep track of your rows.
- Count your stitches as you go to ensure you don’t lose track.
- Keep your tension consistent for a neat finish.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- "My stitches are too tight." – This can result from pulling the yarn too tightly. Try to relax your grip on the yarn.
- "The pattern looks messy." – Ensure you're carrying the unused yarn loosely so it doesn’t pucker.
- "My bag isn't facing the right way." – Double-check which side is the front and back when assembling to maintain the wave pattern direction.

## How to Care for Your Tote Bag
Handwash your Ocean Wave Tapestry Tote Bag in cold water and lay it flat to dry. Avoid machine drying to maintain its shape.
