## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- sc – single crochet
- dc – double crochet
- sl st – slip stitch
- sp – space
- magic ring – start by creating a loop and inserting your hook into it

## How to Make a Magic Ring
1. Hold the yarn in your hand, making a loop with the working yarn over the standing yarn.
2. Insert your hook through the loop from front to back.
3. Yarn over and pull up a loop.
4. Yarn over again and pull through the first loop on your hook to complete your first stitch.

## Size and Gauge
- Finished bracelet length: 7.5 inches (19 cm)
- Bracelet band width: 1 inch (2.5 cm)
- Camellia flower diameter: approx. 2.5 inches (6.5 cm)
- Gauge: 14 sc and 16 rows = 4 inches (10 cm)

To check gauge, create a 4 × 4 in (10 × 10 cm) square of single crochet. If your gauge does not match, adjust your hook size accordingly.

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### Main Bracelet Band
The bracelet band is worked flat in rows.  
1. **Ch 23.**  
2. **Row 1:** Sc in 2nd ch from hook and in each ch across. (22 sc)  
3. **Row 2:** Ch 1, turn, sc in each sc across. (22 sc)  
4. **Rows 3–30:** Repeat Row 2 until the band measures approx. 1 inch (2.5 cm) in width.  

### Camellia Flower
This flower is created using a magic ring to start.  
1. **Flower Base:** Create a magic ring.  
2. Work 1 sc into the ring.  
3. **Petals:** Repeat the following instructions 5 times:  
   - Ch 3, 1 dc, 1 tr, 1 dc, 1 sc into the ring.  
4. **Finish Petals:** Pull the magic ring tight and sl st in the first petal base to complete the flower.  

### Assembly
1. Attach the flower to one end of the bracelet band using the tapestry needle and white yarn.  
2. Weave in any ends.  
3. (Optional) Sew a small piece of felt to the back of the flower for added stability, using craft glue if desired.  
4. Attach a decorative button at the opposite end of the bracelet for closure (if using).  

## Beginner Tips
- Use stitch markers to keep your place in your work.
- Count your stitches often to ensure evenness.
- Keep a loose tension; tight stitches can make your project difficult to work with.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- "My project is uneven." – This may happen if your tension changes. Try to keep your tension consistent throughout your work.
- "I can't get the magic ring right." – Practice makes perfect! If it's challenging, you can substitute a ch 4 loop for the magic ring.
- "My flower petals are floppy." – Ensure that you're pulling the magic ring tight enough to close it completely.

## How to Care for Your Bracelet
To keep your bracelet looking fresh, gently hand wash it in cold water and lay it flat to dry. Avoid pulling tightly on the yarn while working to maintain the flower's shape.
