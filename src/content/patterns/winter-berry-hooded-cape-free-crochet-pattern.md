This cozy Winter Berry Hooded Cape pattern is the perfect accessory for chilly days. With its unique berry stitch and snug hood, you’ll stay warm while looking stylish. The cape is enjoyable to crochet and features only basic stitches like half double crochet (hdc) and slip stitches (sl st). 

This pattern is suitable for those with intermediate skills. You’ll learn how to work the berry stitch and create a lovely hooded cape that fits most adult sizes.

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- hdc – half double crochet
- dc – double crochet
- sl st – slip stitch
- tr – triple crochet
- sp – space
- Berry Stitch – (1 dc in same st, 1 dc in next st, 1 dc in same st) = 1 berry stitch.

## Size and Gauge
- **Finished Size**: Approximately 71 × 76 cm (28 × 30 in) 
- **Hood Size**: 30 × 61 cm (12 × 24 in) 
- **Gauge**: 16 hdc and 10 rows = 10 cm (4 in). 
To measure gauge, crochet a swatch of 20 × 20 sts in hdc. Adjust your hook size if necessary.

## How to Crochet a Winter Berry Hooded Cape (Step by Step)

### Cape Body
The body of the cape is crocheted first. 
1. **Foundation Chain**: With main color (MC), ch 64.
2. **Row 1**: Hdc in the 2nd ch from the hook and in each ch across (63 hdc).
3. **Row 2**: Ch 1, turn. (Hdc, berry stitch) in the first st (this will create a slight ruffle). *Berry stitch in the next st, hdc in the next st; repeat from * across (32 berry stitches, 31 hdc) - end with hdc in the last st. (63 sts)
4. **Rows 3–16**: Repeat Row 2. (Always start with a berry stitch and end with a hdc.)
5. After Row 16, fasten off MC.

### Hood
The hood is crocheted next.
1. **Row 1**: With contrast color (CC), attach yarn at the center back of the neck, ch 2, hdc in each of the next 31 sts for the back of the hood (32 hdc).
2. **Row 2**: Ch 2, turn. (Hdc, berry stitch) in the first st. *Berry st in the next st, hdc in the next st; repeat from * across (16 berry stitches, 16 hdc) - finish with hdc in the last st (32 sts).
3. **Rows 3–8**: Repeat Row 2. Fasten off CC.

### Edging
Switch to the smaller hook size.
1. **With 4.0 mm (G/6) Hook**: Work a sc edge around the cape, working 3 sc in each corner for rounded edges.
2. For the hood, work in the same manner but with an additional row of sc in accent color (AC) starting from the front edge.

### Assembly
Align the hood at the neck edge of the cape and attach it by crocheting together or sewing in place.

### Leaf Embellishments (Optional)
Add a charming touch with leaf embellishments.
1. With accent color (AC), ch 4, join to form a ring.
2. **Round 1**: Ch 1, work 6 sc in the ring (6 sc).
3. **Round 2**: (Sc, hdc, dc) in each st around (18 sts).
4. Fasten off, and stitch 2 leaves on each shoulder of the cape.

### Finishing
Weave in all ends using a tapestry needle. Block the cape to shape it properly, lightly misting with water if necessary.

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Hooded Cape
- Use stitch markers to keep track of your rows.
- Count stitches often to ensure you’re on track.
- Pay attention to your tension to avoid uneven stitches.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- "My project is too small." – You might have too few stitches; ensure you’re counting correctly.
- "The cape is losing its shape." – This can happen with inconsistent tension; blocking can help reshape it.
- "The stitches look uneven." – This could be due to tension issues; practice a few rows to find your comfortable tension.

## How to Care for Your Hooded Cape
Hand wash with cold water and lay flat to dry. This will help maintain the shape and pattern.
