## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain  
- dc – double crochet  
- sc – single crochet  
- sl st – slip stitch  
- st(s) – stitch(es)  
- inc – increase (2 sts in the same st)  
- dec – decrease (crochet 2 sts together)

## Size and Gauge
- **Finished Size:** 30 × 25 cm (12 × 10 in)  
- **Strap Length:** 61 cm (24 in)  
- **Gauge:** 12 dc and 8 rows = 10 cm (4 in) in double crochet  
To check gauge, crochet a swatch of 16 double crochets for 20 rows. If it doesn’t match, adjust your hook size.

## How to Crochet the Charcoal Gray Bow Shoulder Bag (Step by Step)

### Body of the Bag
The body of the bag is worked flat.

1. **Foundation Chain:**  
   Ch 41.  
   
2. **Row 1:**  
   Dc in 4th ch from hook (this counts as your first dc) and in each ch across. Turn. (39 dc)

3. **Rows 2–12:**  
   Ch 3, dc in each dc across. Turn. (39 dc)  

4. **Row 13:**  
   Ch 1, sc in each dc across. Turn. (39 sc)  

5. **Row 14:**  
   Ch 3, dc in each sc across. Turn. (39 dc)  

6. **Rows 15–20:**  
   Repeat Rows 13 and 14.  

7. **Fasten off** and leave a long tail for seaming.  

### Strap (Make 2)
The straps are worked flat as well.

1. **Foundation Chain:**  
   Ch 6.  
   
2. **Row 1:**  
   Sc in 2nd ch from hook, sc in next 4 ch. Turn. (5 sc)  

3. **Rows 2–50:**  
   Ch 1, sc in each sc across. Turn. (5 sc)  

4. **Fasten off.**  

### Bow Decoration
Now, let's make the bow for the front.

1. **Bow Center:**  
   Ch 5.  
   Row 1: Sc in 2nd ch from hook and in the next 2 ch, ch 1, turn. (3 sc)  
   Row 2: Ch 1, inc in first sc, sc in next sc, inc in last sc. Turn. (5 sc)  
   Row 3: Ch 1, sc in each sc across. Turn. (5 sc)  
   Fasten off, leaving a long tail for sewing.

2. **Bow Ends:**  
   Ch 18.  
   Row 1: Sc in 2nd ch from hook and in each ch across. Turn. (17 sc)  
   Row 2: Ch 1, inc in first sc, sc in next 15 sc, inc in last sc. Turn. (19 sc)  
   Fasten off and leave a long tail for sewing. Make 2.

3. **Sew Bow Together:**  
   Pin the bow ends to the bow center and gather, securing with the yarn tail.

## Assembly & Finishing
- **Join Bag Sides:**  
  Place the two edges of the bag body together. Using the long tail from the body, whip stitch the sides together, ensuring a neat finish. Secure the top edges as well.

- **Attach Straps:**  
  Position the straps on the inside of the bag, approximately 10 cm (4 in) from the side seams. Whip stitch them into place securely.

- **Sew Bow onto Bag:**  
  Position the bow on the front of the bag and sew it securely with yarn.

- **Add Lining (optional):**  
  Cut the fabric to the dimensions of the bag, sewing the edges with a sewing machine or by hand. Pin the lining to the top of the bag and sew it in place, folding the raw edges over for a neat finish.

- **Weave in all ends** and block lightly if needed.

## Beginner Tips
- Use stitch markers to mark row beginnings for easier counting.
- Count your stitches after each row to keep track of your progress.
- Maintain even tension by practicing until you feel comfortable.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- "My project is uneven." – This might be due to inconsistent tension. Practice keeping your stitches uniform for better results.
- "The bag is too small." – Make sure your gauge matches the recommended size. If not, try a different hook size.
- "The bow isn't turning out right." – Take your time gathering and securing the bow; it's okay if it needs a little adjusting.

## How to Care for Your Bag
Hand wash your bag in cold water and lay it flat to dry. Avoid machine washing or tumble drying to preserve its shape and texture.
