## Is This Pattern Good for Beginners?
Yes! This pattern is suitable for beginners who have a bit of experience crocheting in the round. You’ll use basic stitches and learn to incorporate color changes as you create flowers and leaves.

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- sl st – slip stitch
- sc – single crochet
- dc – double crochet
- inc – increase
- BLO – back loop only

## Size and Gauge
- Finished size: 20 cm (8 in) tall and 12 cm (4.75 in) wide
- Gauge: 12 dc and 6 rows = 10 cm (4 in)

To measure your gauge, make a small swatch of double crochet, and ensure you achieve the correct stitch count in your fabric.

## The Pattern

### Step 1: Create the Base
The base is crocheted in the round. 

Round 1: Ch 2, work 6 sc into the second ch from the hook. Join with sl st to the first sc. (6 sc)

Round 2: Ch 1, inc in each sc around. Join with sl st to the first sc. (12 sc)

Round 3: Ch 1, *sc in next sc, inc*; repeat from * around. Join with sl st to the first sc. (18 sc)

Round 4: Ch 1, *sc in next 2 sc, inc*; repeat from * around. Join with sl st to the first sc. (24 sc)

Rounds 5–10: Ch 1, sc in each sc around. Join with sl st to the first sc. (24 sc)

### Step 2: Create the Cylindrical Body
The body is crocheted in the round. 

Round 11: Ch 1, BLO, sc in each sc around. Join with sl st to the first sc. (24 sc)

Round 12: Ch 1, BLO, sc in each sc around. Join with sl st to the first sc. (24 sc)

Rounds 13–20: Repeat Round 11 for a total of 8 rounds of BLO sc.

### Step 3: Add Floral Accents
The flowers and leaves are worked separately and attached to the holder. 

#### Flower (Make 3)
The flower is created using a combination of dc and chain stitches.

Round 1: Ch 4, sl st to the first ch to form a ring. 

Round 2: Ch 3 (counts as first dc), work 11 dc into ring. Join with sl st to top of ch 3. (12 dc)

Round 3: Ch 1, sc in next dc, *ch 3, sl st in same dc; repeat from * around. Join with sl st to the first sc. 

#### Leaf (Make 3)
Leaves are small and quick to crochet.

Ch 6, sc in 2nd ch from hook, hdc in next ch, dc in next ch, hdc in next ch, sc in last ch. 

Fasten off with a long tail for sewing.

### Step 4: Assemble Your Paper Towel Holder
Sew the flowers and leaves onto the holder, spacing them evenly around the cylindrical body. 

To create the top twist, ch 12. Join to form a ring, working 4 sc in each ch around for three rounds.

## How to Join Your Floral Accents
Use the long yarn tails to sew the flowers and leaves onto the body of the holder. Position them so they appear to cascade down for a playful look.

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Paper Towel Holder
- Use stitch markers to keep track of your rounds.
- Count your stitches often to maintain the shape.
- Maintain consistent tension for a uniform appearance.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
"My paper towel holder came out lopsided." – This may happen if you increase unevenly. Count your stitches each round to ensure even increases.

"My flowers look uneven." – This can occur with inconsistent tension when changing colors. Practice your tension or try using a stitch marker to help guide you.

"My yarn keeps unraveling." – Be sure to secure your yarn ends well and weave in the loose ends to prevent any unraveling.

## How to Care for Your Paper Towel Holder
To clean, simply wipe with a damp cloth. If needed, you can hand wash gently in cool water and lay flat to air dry.
