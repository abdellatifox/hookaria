## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- dc – double crochet
- sc – single crochet
- sl st – slip stitch
- sp – space
- st – stitch

**Special Stitch:**
- **Lace Shell:** 5 dc in the same stitch – this creates a fan-like shell pattern.

## Size and Gauge
- **Finished Size:**  
  - Chest: 53 cm (21 in)  
  - Length: 25 cm (10 in)  
  - Sleeves: 18 cm (7 in) from underarm to wrist
- **Gauge:** 14 dc and 8 rows of dc = 10 cm (4 in).  
Make a swatch of 20 dc for 10 rows. Measure the dimensions after lightly blocking. Adjust your hook size if needed.

## How to Crochet a Lavender Lace Baby Cardigan (Step by Step)

### Body
The body is worked flat from the bottom up.  
1. **Foundation Chain:** Ch 64.  
2. **Row 1:** dc in 4th ch from hook, dc in each ch across. (62 sts)  
3. **Row 2:** Ch 3 (counts as 1st dc), turn. dc in each st across. (62 sts)  
4. **Row 3:** Ch 3, turn. *Lace Shell in next st, skip 2 sts.* Repeat from * to * across, ending with 2 dc in last st. (21 Lace Shells, 2 dc)  
5. **Row 4:** Ch 3, turn. *dc in sp of next Lace Shell, ch 1.* Repeat across, ending with dc in top of turning chain. (62 sts)  
6. **Row 5:** Ch 3, turn. *Lace Shell in next st, skip 1 st.* Repeat across. (21 Lace Shells)  
7. **Row 6:** Repeat Row 4.  
8. **Row 7:** Repeat Row 5.  
9. **Row 8:** Repeat Row 4.  
10. **Repeat Rows 3–8** until the body measures 25 cm (10 in) from the beginning.

### Sleeves (Make 2)
The sleeves are also crocheted flat.  
1. **Foundation Chain:** Ch 28.  
2. **Row 1:** dc in 4th ch from hook, dc in each ch across. (26 sts)  
3. **Row 2:** Ch 3, turn. dc in each st across. (26 sts)  
4. **Row 3:** Ch 3, turn. *Lace Shell in next st, skip 2 sts.* Repeat from * to * across. (8 Lace Shells, 2 dc)  
5. **Row 4:** Ch 3, turn. *dc in sp of next Lace Shell, ch 1.* Repeat across, ending with dc in top of turning chain. (26 sts)  
6. **Row 5:** Repeat Row 3.  
7. **Rows 6–11:** Repeat Rows 4–5.  
8. **Row 12:** Repeat Row 4.  
9. **Repeat until sleeve measures 18 cm (7 in).**

### Assembly & Finishing
- **Joining Sleeves to Body:** Align the sleeves with the body and join using slip stitches at the underarm.  
- **Seaming:** Use a tapestry needle to sew the bottom of each sleeve seam closed.  
- **Edging:**  
  - Using Color B, attach yarn to the bottom of the cardigan with a sl st.  
  - Ch 1, sc around the entire bottom edge of the cardigan. Join with sl st to the first sc.  
  - Repeat for the sleeves for a clean finish.  
- **Buttons:** Sew 4 buttons along the right side for closure.  
- **Weaving in Ends:** Weave in all yarn ends securely with a tapestry needle.  
- **Blocking:** Lightly block the cardigan to shape and open the lace pattern.

### Beginner Tips for a Neat Baby Cardigan
- Use stitch markers to keep track of your progress.
- Count your stitches after each row to ensure you're on track.
- Don’t worry if the first try isn’t perfect; practice makes progress!

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- “My project is uneven.” – This may happen if you accidentally skipped stitches. Count your stitches regularly.
- “The lace doesn’t show up well.” – This can occur if your tension is too tight. Try relaxing your grip on the yarn.
- “My sleeves are too tight.” – Ensure you followed the row counts properly, and check your gauge before starting.

## How to Care for Your Baby Cardigan
Hand wash and lay flat to dry to preserve the fabric and lace structure. Avoid bleach to maintain color integrity.

## FAQ
### How can I adjust the size of the cardigan?
You can increase the foundation chain in multiples of 3 for every 1-inch increase in chest size. 

### Can I substitute the yarn?
Yes, you can use any light DK yarn, but check the gauge to ensure the size remains consistent.
