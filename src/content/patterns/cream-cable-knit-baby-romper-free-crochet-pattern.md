## Is This Pattern Good for Beginners?
Yes! Although this romper is labeled as intermediate, you only need to know basic stitches like chains, single crochet, and double crochet. The cable stitch may seem challenging, but once you follow the instructions, you'll master it!

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- sc – single crochet
- dc – double crochet
- cable – special stitch pattern
- C4B – cable 4 back: front post double crochet around the first two stitches, place them on a cable needle held at back, double crochet the next two stitches, then double crochet the two stitches from the cable needle
- sl st – slip stitch

## How to Crochet a Cream Cable Knit Baby Romper (Step by Step)

### Body
The body is worked in rows starting from the foundation chain.  
1. Foundation Chain: Ch 56.  
2. Row 1: Sc in 2nd ch from hook and across (55 sc).  
3. Row 2: Ch 2, dc in the first st, *cable, 4 dc* to end (55 sts).  
4. Row 3: Ch 1, sc in first st and across (55 sc).  
5. Row 4: Ch 2, cable in first 4 sts, 12 dc, cable in next 4 sts, 25 dc (55 sts).  
6. Repeat Rows 3–4 until the piece measures 30.5 cm (12 in) long.  
7. Row 5 (Decrease for Legs): Ch 1, sc in first 27 sts, ch 20 (leg opening), sc in next 27 sts (54 sts).  
8. Row 6: Ch 2, dc across until leg opening (27 dc, 20 skipped, 27 dc) (54 sts).  
9. Repeat Pattern from Rows 3–6 until desired length from the bottom of leg openings until work measures 56 cm (22 in) from shoulder seam.

### Straps
The straps are worked separately from the body.  
1. First Strap: Attach yarn at the shoulder seam, ch 10, sc back and forth for 6 rows (10 sc), fasten off.  
2. Repeat for the second strap.

### Edges
Finish by adding a decorative edge around the openings.  
1. Finish edges by sc around the leg openings and neckline (58 sc total).  
2. Fasten off.  

### Assembly & Finishing
- Sew button onto one shoulder strap for easy dressing.
- Weave in all loose ends.
- Block the romper to shape if desired.

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Romper
- Use stitch markers to keep track of your rows and prevent mistakes.
- Count your stitches regularly to ensure you remain consistent.
- Practice maintaining even tension for a neat finish.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- "My stitches look loose." – Make sure you're not holding the yarn too loosely; try tightening your grip.
- "The cable looks messy." – Ensure you’re following the cable stitch steps precisely; practice helps!
- "My romper is too small." – Always check your gauge before starting; if it’s not right, adjust your hook size.

## How to Care for Your Romper
For best care, machine wash on a gentle cycle and lay flat to dry. Avoid wringing it out tightly, as this can stretch the fabric.

### FAQ
### How do I resize the romper?
To make a larger or smaller size, increase or decrease the starting chain in increments of 4.
