## How to Crochet a Granny Square (Step by Step)
The granny square is worked in rounds to create a vibrant piece.
### Round 1
Ch 4, join to form a ring. Ch 3 (counts as 1 dc), work 2 dc in ring, *ch 2, work 3 dc in ring; repeat from * 2 more times. Ch 2, join with sl st to the top of beginning ch-3. (12 dc, 3 ch-2 sp)

### Round 2
Change to Color B. Sl st to the first ch-2 sp, ch 3 (counts as 1 dc), 2 dc in the same sp, ch 2, *3 dc in the next ch-2 sp, ch 2; repeat from * around, ending with ch 2, join with sl st to the top of beginning ch-3. (24 dc, 6 ch-2 sp)

### Round 3
Change to Color C. Sl st to first ch-2 sp, ch 3, 2 dc in the same sp, ch 2, *3 dc in the next ch-2 sp, ch 2; repeat from * around, ending with ch 2, join with sl st to the top of beginning ch-3. (36 dc, 6 ch-2 sp)

### Finish
Fasten off and weave in ends.

## The Cardigan
The cardigan is made by assembling the granny squares together.
1. **Assemble Squares:** Join 4 granny squares into a rectangle (2 squares wide, 2 squares tall) using slip stitch or whip stitch.
2. **Sleeve Squares:** Attach 1 granny square at each end of the rectangle for sleeves.
3. **Back and Front Edging:** With Color A, attach yarn in any corner, ch 1 and work 1 sc around the edge. Place stitches evenly in corners to maintain shape. Join with sl st.
4. **Button Holes (Optional):** In the front edging, create button holes by chaining 3 stitches and skipping 2 sc, then continue to sc. Space evenly across.
5. **Finishing:** Weave in all ends.

## The Hat
The hat is constructed using rounds of double crochet.
1. **Round 1:** Using Color A, ch 4, join. Ch 3, 11 dc in loop. (12 dc)
2. **Round 2:** Ch 3, dc in the same st, *2 dc in next st; repeat from * around, join. (24 dc)
3. **Round 3:** Ch 3, dc in next 2 st, *2 dc in next st, 3 dc in next; repeat from * around, join. (36 dc)
4. **Rounds 4–5:** Ch 3, dc in each st around, join. (36 dc each round)
5. **Round 6:** Ch 1, sc around for a finished edge. Fasten off and weave in ends.

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Toddler Set
- Use stitch markers to keep track of your rounds and avoid confusion.
- Count your stitches after every round to ensure you’re on track.
- Check your tension regularly, especially when switching colors.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- "My squares aren’t lying flat." – Ensure you’re placing the correct number of double crochets in each round.
- "The cardigan is too tight." – This may be due to tight stitches; try using a larger hook or working with a looser tension.
- "My hat is too small." – Check your gauge before starting and adjust your hook size if needed.

## How to Care for Your Toddler Set
Hand wash and lay flat to dry. While the cotton yarn is machine washable, it may shrink or lose shape if washed on high heat.
