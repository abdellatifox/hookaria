- sl st – slip stitch
- sc – single crochet

## The Pattern

### Step 1: Create the Base
The base of the paper towel holder is worked in rounds. 

Round 1: Ch 4, join with sl st to form a ring. Ch 3 (this counts as your first dc), work 11 dc into the ring. Join with sl st to the top of ch 3. (12 dc)

Round 2: Ch 3, dc in the same st. *2 dc in the next st; repeat from * around. Join with sl st to the top of ch 3. (24 dc)

Round 3: Ch 3, *dc in the next st, 2 dc in the following st; repeat from * around. Join with sl st to the top of ch 3. (36 dc)

Round 4: Ch 3, *dc in the next 2 sts, 2 dc in the following st; repeat from * around. Join with sl st to the top of ch 3. (48 dc)

What’s happening? In this section, we’re creating a flat circle as the base of the holder. The increases help widen the piece evenly.

### Step 2: Crochet the Body
The body of the holder features a shell-like stitch pattern.

Round 5: Ch 1 (does not count as a stitch), *sc in next st, skip the next st, 5 dc in the next st (forming a shell), skip the next st; repeat from * around. Join with sl st to the first sc. (12 shells)

Round 6: Ch 1, *sc in the center of the shell, ch 1; repeat from * around. Join with sl st to the first sc. (12 ch-1 spaces)

Round 7: Ch 3, *dc in the next ch-1 space, ch 2, sk the next sc; repeat from * around. Join with sl st to the top of ch 3. (12 ch-2 spaces)

Beginner tip: Count your stitches after each round to ensure accuracy.

### Step 3: Add Hanging Loops
We'll now create the unique round hanging loops for added charm.

Round 8: Ch 3, dc in the next 2 dc, *ch 10, sl st to the same dc; repeat from * around, ending with sl st to the top of ch 3.

## How to Join Your Granny Squares
If you want to hang multiple holders, join them with a simple slip stitch. Lay the pieces together, insert the hook through both, and pull through to create a neat connection.

## Finishing Tip: Secure Your Ends
After completing your paper towel holder, fasten off your yarn and weave in any loose ends using a tapestry needle.

## Beginner Tips
- Use stitch markers to keep track of your rounds.
- Keep your tension consistent to ensure even stitches.
- Don't hesitate to check your progress by hanging the holder to see if the size fits your space.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- "My holder is too small." – This may happen if your gauge is off. Check your stitches and adjust your hook size.
- "The shape is uneven." – Make sure to count your stitches in each round carefully to ensure you're maintaining the increases.
- "The loops aren't looking nice." – Ensure you’re chaining the correct number of stitches for the loops; adjust if necessary.

## How to Care for Your Paper Towel Holder
To keep your paper towel holder looking its best, hand wash gently with mild soap and lay flat to dry. Avoid wringing it out, which may affect its shape.

## FAQ
### How can I change the color of my towel holder?
You can change colors at the beginning of each round. Just fasten off the previous color and join the new one with a slip stitch.

### How many stitches do I need to start?
For the base, you start with a ring containing 12 double crochet stitches. Make sure to count them during your first round.

### Can I use different yarn weights?
Absolutely! Just remember that changing yarn weight will affect the finished size and may alter your gauge, so adjust accordingly.
