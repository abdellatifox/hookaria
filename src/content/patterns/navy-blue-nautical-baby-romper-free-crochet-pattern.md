This Navy Blue Nautical Baby Romper is a delightful one-piece outfit designed for infants, featuring a charming nautical theme with stripes and an anchor motif. It's both practical and stylish, making it a fun project for intermediate crocheters. The stitches used in this pattern include single crochet and double crochet, allowing you to practice your skills while creating something special for a little one.

This romper is perfect for those who have some crochet experience and want to take on a slightly more challenging project. You’ll learn how to create adjustable straps and an adorable anchor motif, perfect for adventurous little ones aged 0-3 months.

## Why You'll Love This Romper
- Cute and timeless design – the nautical theme adds a playful touch.
- Practical and fun – perfect for everyday wear or special occasions.
- Customizable sizes – easily adjust the length and width to fit a growing baby.
- Quick project – the entire outfit can be completed in about 6 hours.

## Is This Pattern Good for Beginners?
While this pattern is designed for intermediate crocheters, it’s still approachable for confident beginners. If you’re comfortable with single crochet and double crochet, you can absolutely tackle this romper with determination. Plus, you'll have the chance to learn about assembly and adding embellishments.

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- sc – single crochet
- dc – double crochet
- sl st – slip stitch
- st(s) – stitch(es)
- FO – finish off
- inc – increase (2 sc in the same stitch)
- dec – decrease (sc2tog)

## Size and Gauge
- **Finished Size:** 0-3 months: 35.5 cm wide (chest), 43 cm long from top of strap to bottom.  
- **Gauge:** 14 sc and 18 rows in sc = 10 cm (4 in).  
To measure gauge, work a swatch of 20 x 20 sc, measure, and adjust your hook size if needed.

## How to Crochet a Navy Blue Nautical Baby Romper (Step by Step)

### Body
The body of the romper is crocheted flat in one piece.  
1. **Start with Navy Blue Yarn.**  
   Ch 58. (58 sts)  
2. **Row 1:** Sc in 2nd ch from hook and in each ch across. Turn. (57 sts)  
3. **Row 2:** Ch 1, sc in each st across. Turn. (57 sts)  
4. **Repeat Row 2** until piece measures 30.5 cm from start.  
5. **Row 16:** Ch 1, sc across first 28 sts, ch 16 (for strap), skip 15 sts, sc in remaining 14 sts. Turn. (28 + 16 + 14 = 58 sts)  
6. **Row 17:** Ch 1, sc in each st across. Turn. (58 sts)  
7. Cut yarn and FO.

### Straps (make 2)
The straps are worked separately in white yarn.  
1. **Start with White Yarn.**  
   Ch 20.  
2. **Row 1:** Sc in 2nd ch from hook and in each ch across. Turn. (19 sts)  
3. **Row 2:** Ch 1, sc in each st across. Turn. (19 sts)  
4. **Repeat Row 2** until strap measures 12.5 cm.  
5. FO, leaving a tail for sewing.  

### Assembly & Finishing
Assembling your romper is the final touch to bring it all together.  
1. Attach one strap to the front of the romper using the tapestry needle and yarn. Sew with small stitches to secure.  
2. Sew the second strap to the opposite side in the same manner.  
3. Sew buttons onto the top of the straps about 5 cm apart.  
4. For the anchor motif, using Red Yarn, create a small anchor shape using sc and attach it to the front center of the romper using the tapestry needle.  
5. Weave in all ends and block if necessary.

## Beginner Tips
- Use stitch markers to keep track of your rows or rounds.
- Count your stitches at the end of each row to ensure accuracy.
- Maintain consistent tension throughout to achieve a neater finish.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- "My project is uneven." – This may be due to inconsistent tension. Try to keep the same tightness as you crochet.
- "I missed a stitch." – Check your work after each row. It’s easier to fix mistakes early on!
- "My straps are different lengths." – Measure each strap as you go to ensure they match.

## How to Care for Your Romper
Hand wash your romper in cold water and lay it flat to dry. Avoid using bleach to retain the color and integrity of the fabric.

## FAQ
### How can I adjust the size of the romper?

Worsted weight, 100% cotton yarn is ideal for this project, as it provides comfort and durability.
