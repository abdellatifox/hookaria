- **Lace Shell**: (3 dc) in the same stitch.

## Size and Gauge
- **Finished Size**: 
  - Larger bag: 30 cm (12 in) tall, 25 cm (10 in) in diameter.  
  - Smaller bag: 20 cm (8 in) tall, 15 cm (6 in) in diameter.  
- **Gauge**: 12 dc and 6 rows = 10 cm (4 in) in lace pattern.  

To check gauge, make a swatch of the lace pattern: 12 double crochets wide and 6 rows tall. If your swatch doesn’t measure 10 cm, adjust your hook size until it does.

## How to Crochet a Winter White Snowflake Lace Bucket Bag (Step by Step)
### Body of the Bag
The body of the bag is worked in rounds to create a solid base with a lacy texture.  
- **Foundation Row**: Ch 4, join with sl st to form a ring.  
- **Round 1**: Ch 3 (counts as first dc), 11 dc into the ring, join with sl st to the top of the beginning ch (12 dc).  
- **Round 2**: Ch 3, *2 dc in next st, 1 dc in next st; repeat from * around, join with sl st (18 dc).  
- **Round 3**: Ch 3, *2 dc in next st, 1 dc in next 2 sts; repeat from * around, join with sl st (24 dc).  
- **Round 4**: Ch 3, *2 dc in next st, 1 dc in next 3 sts; repeat from * around, join with sl st (30 dc).  
- **Round 5**: Ch 3, *2 dc in next st, 1 dc in next 4 sts; repeat from * around, join with sl st (36 dc).  
- **Round 6**: Ch 1, (sc in next st, ch 2) around, join with sl st (36 sc and 36 ch-2 spaces).  
- **Round 7 - 20**: Repeat Round 6 for additional height, depending on your desired length (each round maintains 36 sc and 36 ch-2 spaces).  

### Bag Closure
The closure of the bag adds the final touches to the lace design.  
- **Round 21**: Ch 1, sc in each sc and each ch-2 space around (72 sts).  
- **Round 22**: Ch 3, dc in next sc, *Lace Shell in next sc, dc in next sc; repeat from * around, join (48 Lace Shells).  
- **Round 23**: Ch 1, sc in each shell and chain space around, join with sl st.  
- **Round 24**: Ch 1, *sc in next st, ch 1, skip next st; repeat from * around, join with sl st.  

### Assembly & Finishing
- **Lining**: Cut fabric to a rectangle measuring 24 inches by 18 inches. Fold in half and sew side seams, then turn right side out. Insert lining into the bag and sew the top edge to the bag using slip stitches or whip stitch.  
- **Handles**: Attach one handle to each side of the bag using slip knots or sewing securely to prevent wear.  
- **Weaving in Ends**: Use a tapestry needle to weave in all loose yarn ends.  

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Bucket Bag
- Use stitch markers to keep track of your rounds.
- Count your stitches at the end of each round to avoid mistakes.
- Keep your tension consistent to ensure the bag maintains its shape.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- **My bag is too small.** – Ensure you are following the stitch counts for each round; missing stitches can reduce size.
- **The lace pattern looks uneven.** – Pay attention to your counts; double-check that you're following the Lace Shell correctly.
- **My yarn is fraying.** – Make sure you’re using yarn appropriate for crochet projects and store it properly to avoid damage.

## How to Care for Your Bucket Bag
Hand wash or machine wash on a gentle cycle. Reshape and dry flat, avoiding bleach.

## FAQ
### How long will it take to make the bucket bag?  
It typically takes around 7–8 hours, depending on your individual speed, with about 5–6 hours for the body and 2 hours for lining and assembly. 

### Can I modify the size of the bag?  
Yes! You can adjust the number of rounds in the body to create a larger or smaller bag, following the stitch count guidelines.

### What kind of yarn should I use?  
A 100% acrylic yarn in DK weight is recommended for this pattern, providing the right balance of softness and structure.
