This dusty blue textured baby cardigan pattern is perfect for little ones, combining a cozy design with delightful stitch patterns. It’s an enjoyable project that uses basic stitches like double crochet, half double crochet, and single crochet, making it ideal for those who want to keep baby warm and stylish.

This pattern is suitable for all skill levels, especially beginners looking to enhance their crochet skills while creating something special for a baby. You'll learn how to crochet panels and assemble a cardigan, with options for three different sizes.

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- dc – double crochet
- hdc – half double crochet
- sc – single crochet
- sl st – slip stitch
- sk – skip
- st(s) – stitch(es)
- [ ] – repeat instructions in brackets as indicated

## Size and Gauge
- Finished size dimensions approximate for the body:
  - 0-6 months: Chest: 50.8 cm (20 in), Length: 25.4 cm (10 in)
  - 6-12 months: Chest: 54.6 cm (21.5 in), Length: 27.9 cm (11 in)
  - 12-18 months: Chest: 58.4 cm (23 in), Length: 30.5 cm (12 in)
- Gauge: 12 dc and 8 rows = 10 cm (4 in) in the textured stitch. 
- To check gauge, make a swatch of 20 stitches and 14 rows. Adjust hook size as needed to achieve correct gauge.

## How to Crochet a Baby Cardigan (Step by Step)

### Body Panels (Make 2)
The body panels are made separately and then joined.  
1. **Foundation Row**: With a 5.5 mm hook, ch 50 (size 0-6 months) or ch 54 (size 6-12 months) or ch 58 (size 12-18 months).
2. **Row 1**: Starting in the 4th ch from the hook, dc in each ch across. (48, 52, or 56 dc)
3. **Row 2**: Ch 2 (counts as first hdc), hdc in next st, *sk 1 st, hdc in next st*; repeat from * to * to end. Turn. (24 hdc)
4. **Row 3**: Ch 3 (counts as first dc), dc in each hdc across. (24 dc)
5. **Row 4**: Ch 2, hdc in next st, *sk 1 st, hdc in next st*; repeat from * to * to end. Turn. (12 hdc)
6. **Row 5**: Ch 3, dc in each hdc across. (12 dc)
7. **Row 6**: Ch 2, hdc in next st, *sk 1 st, hdc in next st*; repeat from * to * to end. Turn. (6 hdc)
8. **Row 7**: Ch 3, dc in each hdc across. (6 dc)
9. **Repeat rows 2-7** until the length measures 23 cm (9 in) for size 0-6 months and 25.4 cm (10 in) for larger sizes. End after a Row 7.

### Assembly & Finishing
Once both panels are complete, it's time for assembly.  
- Join the two panels together at the shoulders using slip stitches or single crochet, ensuring the front sides are facing.
- With the 6.0 mm hook, fold down the collar (first 2 rows from foundation) towards the back of the cardigan and pin in place.
- Work a round of sc around the entire edge of the cardigan for a finished border, placing 3 sc at corners for a smooth curve.
- Attach buttons evenly spaced on one side, and create buttonholes on the opposite side by making a ch at the desired height, skipping the next two stitches, and continuing the row.

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Cardigan
- Use stitch markers to keep track of your rows and stitches.
- Count your stitches at the end of each row to avoid mistakes.
- Keep your tension even by relaxing while you work and pausing when needed.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- "My project is uneven." – This can happen due to inconsistent tension. Focus on keeping your tension even throughout the project.
- "The cardigan is too small." – Double-check your gauge before starting. If your swatch doesn’t match, adjust your hook size accordingly.
- "I can't find my stitches." – This often occurs if you’re not counting correctly. Try using stitch markers or counting your stitches often to stay on track.

## How to Care for Your Baby Cardigan
This cardigan is easy to care for. Simply machine wash on a gentle cycle and tumble dry on low, or lay flat to dry to maintain its shape.
