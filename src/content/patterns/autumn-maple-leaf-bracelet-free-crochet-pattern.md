This delightful Autumn Maple Leaf Bracelet features charming crocheted leaves that celebrate the beauty of fall. The bracelet is designed to be adjustable, making it perfect for all wrist sizes. You will use basic stitches, primarily double crochet, to create this lovely accessory. It’s a fun project that brings together the season’s colors.

This pattern is suitable for those with some crochet experience, but it’s also beginner-friendly. You’ll get to practice making small leaves and learn how to assemble them into a beautiful bracelet.

## Why You'll Love This Bracelet
- Quick and manageable – finish it in just over an hour.
- Customizable colors – switch up the colors to match your personal style.
- Adjustable size – make it perfect for any wrist size.
- Fun embellishments – add beads for extra flair or keep it simple.

## Is This Pattern Good for Beginners?
Yes! This pattern involves basic stitches like chains, slip stitches, single crochet, and double crochet. You'll learn to make and assemble little leaves, which can help you grow your skills with fun and simple steps.

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain  
- sl st – slip stitch  
- sc – single crochet  
- dc – double crochet  
- st(s) – stitch(es)  

## Size and Gauge
- Finished size: Approximately 19 cm (7.5 in) un-stretched; adjustable up to 23 cm (9 in).  
- Gauge: 14 dc and 8 rows = 10 cm (4 in). 

To measure gauge, crochet a swatch with 12 dc and 8 rows. If the swatch measures differently, adjust your hook size to get the right tension.

## How to Crochet the Leaves
The leaves are crocheted one by one. You'll create six leaves in total.

### Leaf (Make 6)
**Burnt Orange:**  
1. Ch 13, dc in 4th ch from hook (2 skipped ch = 1 dc), and in next ch. (3 dc)  
2. 3 dc in next ch (leaf tip), dc in next 3 ch. (7 dc)  
3. 3 dc in next ch (leaf bottom), dc in next 6 ch. (10 dc)  
4. Ch 1, turn. Sc in first st, 2 sc in next st, sc in next 7 sts, 2 sc in next st, sc in last st. (12 sc)  
5. Ch 1, turn. Sc in first st, sc in next 10 sts, sl st in last st to finish. (11 sc)  
6. Fasten off, leaving a 6-inch tail for sewing.

**Repeat for 5 more leaves, alternating colors with:**  
**Golden Yellow:** Follow the same steps but use the golden yellow yarn for 2 leaves.  
**Olive Green:** Follow the same steps but use the olive green yarn for the last 2 leaves.

## Making the Bracelet Base
The bracelet base is created with elastic cord, which is easy to adjust.

1. Cut approximately 30 cm (12 in) of elastic cord.  
2. String all 6 leaves onto the elastic, spacing them evenly.  
3. Tie a knot to secure the leaves in place.  
4. Adjust the positions of the leaves as desired before making a final knot.  
5. Optional: Use fabric glue on the knot for extra security.

## Assembly & Finishing
Once you have all the pieces ready, it’s time to assemble the bracelet.

1. Place the leaves onto the elastic evenly spaced apart.  
2. Tie a secure knot with the elastic to form a circle. Trim excess elastic, leaving a small tail for any adjustments.  
3. If using beads, string them onto the elastic before attaching to the leaves for added flair.

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Bracelet
- Use stitch markers to keep track of your stitches and rows.
- Count carefully as you go to avoid missing stitches.
- Remember to practice even tension when working the leaves.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- My project is uneven. – This can happen if your stitch tension varies. Try to maintain consistent tension across all leaves.
- I lost my stitch count! – Keep a careful count as you work or use a stitch marker to mark the end of each row.
- My leaves look different sizes. – Ensure you're using the same hook size and following the pattern closely for each leaf.

## How to Care for Your Bracelet
Hand wash in cold water and lay it flat to dry. This will keep your bracelet looking fresh and vibrant.

Yes, you can use different yarn weights, but keep in mind it may affect the size and look of the leaves.
