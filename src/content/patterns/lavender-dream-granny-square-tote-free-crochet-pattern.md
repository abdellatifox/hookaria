This Lavender Dream Granny Square Tote pattern is perfect for crafting a stylish bag made from beautiful, lavender-themed granny squares. It's fun and easy to make, using basic stitches like double crochet and slip stitch. You’ll enjoy not only the process but also the final product which is practical for carrying your everyday essentials.

This pattern is suitable for crocheters of all skill levels, especially those looking to create a classic tote with simple steps. You’ll learn to crochet granny squares and assemble them into a functional bag!

## Is This Pattern Good for Beginners?
Yes! This pattern uses basic stitches and simple assembly techniques. The step-by-step instructions will guide you through the process, making it approachable even if you’re new to crochet.

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- sl st – slip stitch
- dc – double crochet
- sp – space

## Size and Gauge
- **Finished Size**: 14 × 12 in (35 × 30 cm)
- **Gauge**: 16 dc and 8 rows = 10 cm (4 in) in granny square pattern. Make a swatch and measure to confirm gauge adjustments if necessary.

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### Granny Squares (Make 6)
The granny squares are crocheted in rounds, starting from a magic ring.  
1. **Using Color A, make a magic ring.**  
2. **Round 1**: Ch 3 (this counts as your first dc), 2 dc into ring, ch 2, *3 dc into ring, ch 2; repeat from * two more times. Join with sl st to the top of beginning ch 3. (12 dc + 3 ch spaces, 4 corners)  
3. **Round 2**: Sl st into first ch 2 sp. Ch 3, 2 dc, ch 2, 3 dc in the same sp; *3 dc in the next ch 2 sp, ch 2, 3 dc in the same sp; repeat around.* Join with sl st to the top of beginning ch 3. (24 dc + 4 ch spaces)  
4. **Rounds 3–5**: Repeat Round 2, increasing in each ch 2 corner sp until you have 5 rounds and 60 dc total. Fasten off and weave in ends.

### Assembling the Tote
The squares are joined together to form the body of the tote.  
1. **Layout Squares**: Arrange 6 granny squares in two rows of 3 for the tote body.  
2. **Joining Squares**: Use a whip stitch or slip stitch to join the squares along the edges. Join row 1 (3 squares), then join row 2 squares to row 1 squares. You should now have a rectangular piece made of 6 squares (14 × 12 in).

### Creating the Handles
The handles are crocheted separately and attached to the tote.  
1. **Handle Straps** (Make 2): Using Color A,  
   - Ch 60, dc into the 4th ch from the hook and into each ch across for a total of 58 dc. Fasten off.  
2. **Attach Handles**: Pin each handle to the top edge of the tote, positioned 3 in (7.5 cm) from the sides. Sew in place using a whip stitch.

### Lining the Tote
(Optional) A lining can add a nice finish and extra durability. Pin the lining fabric inside the tote, folding the top edges of the fabric over the crochet edges. Whip stitch to secure.

## Assembly & Finishing
Weave in all ends and gently block the entire tote. For added firmness, consider using fabric stiffener on the lining. You may also want to attach a decorative crochet flower or button for embellishment.

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Tote
- Use stitch markers to keep track of your rounds.
- Count your stitches after every row to ensure you’re on track.
- Check your tension regularly to keep your squares consistent.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- "My squares are different sizes." – Ensure consistent tension while crocheting.
- "The bag feels floppy." – Consider adding lining or fabric stiffener for more structure.
- "I can’t fill my tote to the top." – Ensure you have joined the squares securely at the edges.

## How to Care for Your Tote
Hand wash in cold water and lay it flat to dry. This helps maintain its shape and bright colors over time.
