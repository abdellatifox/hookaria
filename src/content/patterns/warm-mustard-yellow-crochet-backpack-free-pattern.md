## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- dc – double crochet
- sl st – slip stitch
- st(s) – stitch(es)
- sc – single crochet
- sc-2tog – single crochet two together (decrease)

## Size and Gauge
- **Finished Size:** Approximately 30.5 × 38 cm (12 × 15 in) with a 10 cm (4 in) deep base.
- **Gauge:** 12 dc and 7 rows = 10 cm (4 in).

To check your gauge, make a 20-stitch swatch. If it does not match the gauge, adjust your hook size up or down. Proper gauge is important to ensure the finished backpack has the correct dimensions.

## How to Crochet a Warm Mustard Yellow Backpack (Step by Step)

### Body
The body of the backpack is crocheted in rows to create a flat rectangle.  
1. **Foundation:** Ch 40.  
2. **Row 1:** Dc in 4th ch from hook and in each ch across. (38 dc)  
3. **Row 2:** Ch 3 (counts as first dc), turn, dc in each st across. (38 dc)  
4. **Row 3–20:** Repeat Row 2 until piece measures approximately 38 cm (15 in) tall.  
5. **Row 21:** Ch 1, sc across. (38 sc)  
6. **Finish:** Fasten off, weave in ends.

### Base
Now we will create the base of the backpack, which provides stability.  
1. **Foundation:** Ch 30.  
2. **Row 1:** Dc in 4th ch from hook and in each ch across. (28 dc)  
3. **Row 2:** Ch 3, turn, dc across. (28 dc)  
4. **Row 3–6:** Repeat Row 2 until piece measures approximately 10 cm (4 in) deep.  
5. **Finish:** Fasten off, weave in ends.

### Assembly
We’ll join the pieces and add the lining to complete the backpack.  
1. **Lining:** Cut lining fabric to 1 inch larger than body dimensions. Pin lining to the inside of the backpack. Sew or pin in place.  
2. **Attach the Base:** Align the base with the bottom of the body and slip stitch around, joining the two pieces. (38 sts around)  
3. **Attach Straps:** If using webbing, attach at sides of the backpack, securing with strong fabric glue or sew down with yarn.  
4. **Drawstring Closure:** Create a channel for the drawstring by sc around the top edge of the backpack, making sure to leave a gap. Weave the cord through the channel.

## Beginner Tips
- Use stitch markers to keep track of your rows.
- Count your stitches regularly to ensure accuracy.
- Pay attention to tension—maintaining it helps your bag look neat.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- "My project is uneven." – This may happen if your tension is inconsistent. Try to relax while crocheting.
- "The base doesn’t fit the body." – Check your gauge. If you didn't measure, the pieces might not match.
- "I can’t find enough space for the drawstring." – If your channel is too tight, try chaining a few more stitches in that row.

## How to Care for Your Backpack
Hand wash in cold water and lay flat to dry. Avoid bleach, as it may damage the yarn.

## FAQ
### How do I resize this backpack?

Absolutely! Just keep in mind that the weight and fiber content may change the look and feel of your backpack. Make sure to check your gauge!
