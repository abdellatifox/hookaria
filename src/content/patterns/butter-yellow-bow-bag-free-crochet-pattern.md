## Is This Pattern Good for Beginners?

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- sc – single crochet
- dc – double crochet
- sl st – slip stitch
- st(s) – stitch(es)
- bpdc – back post double crochet

**Bow:**  
- Ch 5, dc in 4th ch from hook, dc in last ch, ch 1, turn. (5 sts)  
- Ch 5, dc in 4th ch from hook, dc in next st, ch 1, turn. (5 sts) Repeat until desired size is reached.

## Size and Gauge
- **Finished dimensions:** 30 × 20 cm (12 × 8 in) wide, 10 cm (4 in) deep.
- **Handle height from top of bag:** 23 cm (9 in).
- **Gauge:** 14 dc and 8 rows = 10 cm (4 in).
- To check gauge, work a swatch of 20 dc for 10 rows. Adjust the hook size if needed.

## The Pattern

### Bag Body
The bag body is made by working flat.  
1. Ch 36.  
2. Row 1: dc in 3rd ch from hook and in each ch across. (34 dc)  
3. Row 2–12: ch 2, turn, dc in each dc across. (34 dc)  
4. Row 13: ch 1, turn, sc in each dc across. (34 sc)  
5. Fasten off.  

### Bag Bottom
The bottom portion is crocheted in a spiral for added structure.  
1. Using Butter Yellow, ch 34, then working in a spiral, sc evenly around the bottom.  
2. Continue to sc in a spiral until the bottom measures 10 cm (4 in) in diameter.  
3. Fasten off.  

### Side Seam
The sides of the bag body come together next.  
1. Align the two rectangles (bag body) and sew sides together using sc around.  
2. Ensure you maintain the same stitch count (34) on each side.

### Handles
The handles are worked separately and attached afterward.  
1. Ch 60, turn, work 1 bpdc in each of the next 59 chs. (60 bpdc)  
2. Ch 1, turn, sc in each bpdc. (60 sc)  
3. Fasten off, leaving a long tail. Attach handles securely to the top of the bag by sewing.

### Bow
The adorable bow adds the perfect finishing touch.  
1. Ch 15.  
2. Row 1: dc in 3rd ch from hook, dc in each ch across. (13 dc)  
3. Row 2: Repeat the Bow instructions as stated.  
4. Gather the center and tie off with a length of yarn.  
5. Attach the bow to the front of the bag using yarn.

## Assembly & Finishing
Fold the sides of the bag and use slip stitch to seam both sides and bottom together. Attach the handles securely to the sides of the bag with slip stitches. Weave in all ends and block as desired using a steam block.

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Bag
- Use stitch markers to keep track of your rows and stitches.
- Count your stitches frequently to avoid losing your place.
- Maintain consistent tension for even stitches throughout the bag.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- "My project is uneven." – This might be due to inconsistent tension. Aim to keep your tension uniform throughout the project.
- "The bag is too tight." – You might be crocheting too tightly. Try using a larger hook size to loosen your stitches.
- "I lost my stitch count." – Use stitch markers to help keep track of your stitches, or count as you go.

## How to Care for Your Bag
To keep your bag looking fresh, hand wash it in cold water and lay it flat to dry. Avoid wringing it out to maintain its shape.
