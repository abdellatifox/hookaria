You’ll enhance your skills in color changes and shaping as you create this unique project. It’s a delightful project that you’ll enjoy making and using.

## Is This Pattern Good for Beginners?
Yes! This pattern is suitable for beginners who are familiar with basic crochet stitches. You’ll practice color changes and shaping while creating a useful item.

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- sl st – slip stitch
- sc – single crochet
- dc – double crochet
- inc – increase
- BLO – back loop only

## Size and Gauge
- Finished size: Approximately 15 cm (6 in) tall and 10 cm (4 in) wide.
- Gauge: 12 dc and 7 rows = 10 cm (4 in).

Make sure to check your gauge. If your paper towel holder is coming out too small or too large, adjust your hook size accordingly.

## The Pattern
The body of the paper towel holder is worked in rounds.

## Step 1: Create the Base
Round 1: Ch 2, work 8 sc into the second ch from the hook. Join with sl st to the first sc. (8 sc)

Round 2: Ch 1, inc in each sc around. Join with sl st to the first sc. (16 sc)

Round 3: Ch 1, *sc in next sc, inc in next sc; repeat from * around. Join with sl st to the first sc. (24 sc)

Round 4: Ch 1, *sc in next 2 sc, inc in next sc; repeat from * around. Join with sl st to the first sc. (32 sc)

Round 5: Ch 1, *sc in next 3 sc, inc in next sc; repeat from * around. Join with sl st to the first sc. (40 sc)

Now, we will work the body of the holder.

## Step 2: Crochet the Body
Round 6: Ch 1, sc in each sc around. Join with sl st to the first sc. (40 sc)

Rounds 7–18: Repeat Round 6 for 12 more rounds. Feel free to change yarn colors at the start of any round to create a rainbow effect.

## Step 3: Add Decorative Cloud-shaped Ends
Round 19: Ch 1, *sc in next 10 sc, 5 dc in next sc (this forms a cloud), sc in next 10 sc; repeat from * around. Join with sl st to the first sc.

Round 20: Ch 1, *sc in next 10 sc, 5 dc in next dc; repeat from * around. Join with sl st to the first sc.

## Step 4: Create the Hanging Loop
Ch 20, sl st into the next stitch to create a loop. Fasten off.

## Finishing Tip: Secure Your Ends
Weave in all your yarn ends using a tapestry needle. 

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Paper Towel Holder
- Use stitch markers to keep track of your rounds.
- Count your stitches regularly to maintain even shape.
- Maintain a consistent tension for an even finish.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
"My holder flares out too much." – This may happen if there are too many increases. Count your increases and compare with the pattern. Adjust the number in each round if necessary.

"My clouds look uneven." – This could be due to varying tension on the cluster stitches. Practice working clusters if they don’t look right, or refer back to the technique.

"My paper towel holder is too small." – Ensure you're checking your gauge. If it’s off, try a different hook size to achieve the correct dimensions.

## How to Care for Your Paper Towel Holder
To clean your paper towel holder, spot clean with a damp cloth. Avoid submerging it in water.
