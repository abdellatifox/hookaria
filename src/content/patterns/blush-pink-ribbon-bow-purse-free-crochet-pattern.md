This Blush Pink Ribbon Bow Purse pattern is a charming accessory featuring a delightful ribbon bow. It's designed to hold small essentials, making it perfect for outings or as a stylish gift. You’ll find this project enjoyable and manageable, using basic crochet stitches like double crochet and single crochet.

This pattern is suitable for those with some crochet experience, and even if you're a beginner, you'll pick up valuable skills along the way. Let’s dive in!

## Is This Pattern Good for Beginners?
Yes! While this purse features different parts, it uses basic stitches and clear instructions. You'll learn how to create the body, sides, and bow, making it a lovely project for those just starting out.

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- dc – double crochet
- sc – single crochet
- sl st – slip stitch
- st(s) – stitch(es)
- Bow Ruffle – working in back loops only to give a textured ruffled effect

## Gauge and Finished Size
- Finished size: 20 × 15 cm (8 × 6 in)
- Gauge: 12 dc and 7 rows = 10 cm (4 in)

Check your gauge by making a small swatch. Adjust your hook size if necessary. A tighter gauge can be achieved with a smaller hook and a looser gauge with a larger hook.

## How to Crochet a Blush Pink Ribbon Bow Purse (Step by Step)

### Body of Purse
The body of the purse is crocheted flat.  
1. Chain 37 (37 ch).
2. Row 1: DC in 4th ch from hook, DC in each ch across. (35 dc)
3. Row 2: Ch 3 (counts as first dc), turn, DC in each dc across. (35 dc)
4. Rows 3–12: Repeat Row 2 for 10 more rows. (Total 12 rows)
5. Row 13: Ch 1, turn, sc in each dc across. (35 sc)
6. Row 14: Ch 1, turn, sc in each sc across. (35 sc)
7. Fasten off.

### Sides (make 2)
The sides create height for the purse.  
1. Chain 4 (4 ch).
2. Row 1: DC in 4th ch from hook. (1 dc)
3. Row 2: Ch 3, turn, DC in same st and in each ch across. (2 dc)
4. Rows 3–6: Repeat Row 2 for 4 more rows. (Total 6 rows)
5. Fasten off.

### Bow
The bow adds a cute finishing touch.  
1. Chain 15 (15 ch).
2. Row 1: DC in 4th ch from hook and in each ch across. (13 dc)
3. Row 2: Ch 3, turn, DC in each dc across. (13 dc)
4. Rows 3–4: Repeat Row 2 for 2 more rows. (Total 4 rows)
5. Fasten off and leave a long tail for tying.
6. Fold the rectangle in half lengthwise and pinch the center.

### Assembly & Finishing
1. Join the sides: Using sl st, attach the sides to the main body of the purse. Pin in place and sew with a tapestry needle.
2. Insert the magnetic snap fastener: Position it inside the top edge of the purse, following package instructions.
3. Sew the ribbon bow: Wrap the long tail around the center, tie to secure, and attach to the front of the purse.
4. Optional: Sew a cotton lining for additional structure and durability.
5. Weave in all ends and block the purse if desired.

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Purse
- Use stitch markers to track your rows and stitches.
- Count your stitches at the end of each row to maintain an even size.
- Keep your tension consistent to ensure the purse holds its shape.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- "My purse looks uneven." – Check your stitch count for each row and adjust tension as needed.
- "The sides aren’t attaching properly." – Make sure you pin and stitch them securely without gaps.
- "The bow isn’t coming out right." – Ensure your bow piece is the correct size and adjust the tying for balance.

## How to Care for Your Purse
Hand wash in cool water, lay flat to dry, and avoid wringing to maintain its shape.

## FAQ
### How can I add a lining to my purse?
To add a lining, cut a piece of fabric slightly smaller than the purse dimensions and sew it in place after assembling the body.

### Can I resize this purse?
Yes! Adjust the chain at the start of the body to make the purse larger or smaller, keeping the same stitch count.

### What other colors can I use?
Feel free to substitute the Blush Pink with any worsted weight yarn color, like Lavender or Mint, to fit your style.
