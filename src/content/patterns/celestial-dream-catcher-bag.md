## Skill Level: Intermediate
You should be comfortable with basic stitches such as chain (ch) and single crochet (sc). This pattern features the star stitch, which is a lovely new technique to learn.

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- sl st – slip stitch
- sc – single crochet
- star st – star stitch: *Yarn over, insert hook into st, yarn over, pull up a loop (3 loops on hook). Yarn over, insert hook into the same st, yarn over, pull up a loop (5 loops on hook). Yarn over, pull through all 5 loops. Ch 1 to close the star st.*

## Size and Gauge
- Finished size: 30 × 40 cm (12 × 16 in)
- Gauge: 15 star st and 10 rows = 10 cm.

To measure gauge, crochet a swatch using the star stitch. Measure the number of stitches and rows over 10 cm (4 in) and adjust your hook size as needed.

## The Pattern
The body of the bag is crocheted in the round using the star stitch.

## Step 1: Create the Base
Start with a magic ring.
- Round 1: Ch 3 (this counts as your first dc), work 11 dc into the ring. Join with sl st to the top of the ch 3. (12 dc)
- Round 2: Ch 1, 2 sc in each dc around. Join with sl st to the first sc. (24 sc)
- Round 3: Ch 1, *sc in next sc, 2 sc in next sc; repeat from * around. Join with sl st to the first sc. (36 sc)

## Step 2: Crochet the Body
The body is worked in the star stitch.
- Round 4: Ch 1, *star st in next sc, ch 1; repeat from * around. Join with sl st to the first star st. 
- Round 5: Ch 1, *star st in each ch 1 sp around. Join with sl st to the first star st.
- Repeat Round 5 until the body measures 30 cm (12 in) or your desired length.

## Step 3: Add the Drawstring and Tassels
- To create the drawstring, take a length of yarn, approximately 1.5 m (60 in), thread it through the top of the bag just below the last round. 
- For tassels, cut several lengths of yarn at 15 cm (6 in). Group them into sets and tie them securely at one end. Attach to the drawstring at equal intervals, making sure they hang down for a whimsical effect.

## Finishing Tip: Secure Your Ends
Fasten off all yarn and weave in any loose ends using a tapestry needle. Ensure everything is secured well.

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Celestial Dream Catcher Bag
- Use stitch markers – they help keep track of round beginnings.
- Count your stitches – this will prevent any extra holes.
- Maintain even tension – it makes your finished bag look neat.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- "My bag is uneven." – This is often due to inconsistent tension. Always check your tension and adjust your grip.
- "The star stitches aren't forming properly." – Ensure you’re completing all parts of the stitch correctly; practice will help.
- "My bag is too small." – If this happens, your gauge may be off. Check and adjust your hook size for future rounds.

## How to Care for Your Celestial Dream Catcher Bag
Gently hand wash in cold water and lay flat to dry. Avoid wringing out the yarn to maintain its shape.

## FAQ
### How many stitches do I need for a bag?
This pattern calls for 36 stitches in the final round for a good size; counting stitches as you go will help you keep track.

### Can I change the yarn color?
Absolutely! Feel free to vary colors to make your bag uniquely yours. Just adjust your starting round as needed.

### How long will this take to make?
Depending on your skill level and pace, it may take about 8–12 hours to complete.
