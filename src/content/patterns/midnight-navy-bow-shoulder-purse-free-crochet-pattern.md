This Midnight Navy Bow Shoulder Purse pattern is a stylish accessory perfect for carrying your everyday essentials. The large bow detail adds a lovely touch of sophistication. You’ll be working with basic stitches like single crochet and slip stitch, making it an enjoyable project for intermediate crocheters.

You will learn how to create a chic handbag while practicing your crochet skills. The finished purse measures approximately 30 × 25 cm (12 × 10 in), perfect for casual outings or special occasions.

## Why You'll Love This Purse

- Large bow detail – adds a sophisticated touch to your handbag.

## Is This Pattern Good for Beginners?
Yes! While this pattern is marked as intermediate, it uses basic stitches and clear instructions. If you’re comfortable with reading patterns and ready to try something a bit more challenging, this purse is a wonderful choice.

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- sc – single crochet
- sl st – slip stitch
- hdc – half double crochet
- dc – double crochet
- inc – increase (2 sc in the same stitch)
- dec – decrease (single crochet two together)

## Size and Gauge
- Finished dimensions for the purse: 30 × 25 cm (12 × 10 in).
- Bow dimensions: approximately 20 × 7.5 cm (8 × 3 in).
- Gauge: 14 sc and 10 rows = 10 cm (4 in). To check your gauge, crochet a swatch of 15 stitches and 12 rows in sc, and measure. Adjust your hook size if your gauge does not match.

## How to Crochet the Midnight Navy Bow Shoulder Purse (Step by Step)

### Body of Purse
The body of the purse is crocheted flat and then folded to create the bag.  
1. **Foundation Row:** Ch 38.  
2. **Row 1:** Sc in 2nd ch from hook and in each ch across. (37 sc)  
3. **Row 2:** Ch 1, turn. Sc in each sc across. (37 sc)  
4. **Row 3–14:** Repeat Row 2 for 13 more rows. (14 rows completed)  
5. **Row 15:** Ch 1, turn. Sc in each sc across. (37 sc)  
6. **Row 16:** Ch 1, turn. Dec, sc in next 33 sc, dec. (35 sc)  
7. **Row 17:** Ch 1, turn. Sc across. (35 sc)  
8. **Row 18:** Ch 1, turn. Dec, sc in next 31 sc, dec. (33 sc)  
9. **Row 19:** Ch 1, turn. Sc across. (33 sc)  
10. **Row 20:** Ch 1, turn. Dec, sc in next 29 sc, dec. (31 sc)  
11. **Row 21–25:** Repeat Row 19. (28 sc after these rows)  
12. **Row 26:** Ch 1, turn. Dec, sc across. (29 sc)  
13. **Row 27–29:** Repeat Row 19. (28 sc after these rows)  
14. **Row 30:** Attach Color B. Ch 1, turn. Sc in each sc around, join with sl st. (Approx. 28 sc, adjust as necessary)  

### Bow Detail
The bow detail is crocheted separately and then attached to the purse.  
1. **Bow Foundation:** Ch 10.  
2. **Row 1:** Sc in 2nd ch from hook and in each ch across. (9 sc)  
3. **Row 2–5:** Ch 1, turn. Sc in each sc across. (9 sc each)  
4. **Row 6:** Ch 1, turn. Dec, sc in next 5 sc, dec. (7 sc)  
5. **Row 7:** Ch 1, turn. Sc in each sc across. (7 sc)  
6. **Row 8:** Ch 1, turn. Dec, sc in next 3 sc, dec. (5 sc)  
7. **Row 9:** Ch 1, turn. Sc in each sc across. (5 sc)  
8. Fasten off and weave in ends.

### Assemble the Purse
1. Fold the body of the purse in half, with right sides facing inside.  
2. Join the sides with sc or slip stitch, leaving the top open.  
3. Use the tapestry needle to attach the bow in the center of the purse front.  
4. Cut lining fabric to fit: 30 × 25 cm (12 × 10 in), plus 2.5 cm (1 in) for seam allowance.  
5. Sew or pin lining to purse edges, placing the magnetic snap closure securely.  
6. Fold the top edge of the lining to avoid raw edges and sew around to secure.  
7. Weave in any remaining ends.

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Purse
- Use stitch markers to mark the beginning of each round if needed.
- Count your stitches regularly to ensure you’re on track.
- Keep a consistent tension throughout your work for a polished finish.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- "My project is uneven." – This might be due to inconsistent tension. Focus on maintaining even pressure while stitching.
- "The purse is too small." – Check your gauge and ensure you’ve followed the stitch count carefully.
- "The bow isn't secure." – Make sure to attach it tightly with your tapestry needle and secure any loose ends.

## How to Care for Your Purse
Hand wash cold and lay flat to dry. This will help maintain the crochet shape and design.

## FAQ
### How long will this take to make?

Yes! You can experiment with different yarn types, but be mindful of the weight for proper sizing.

### What if I want a larger purse?
You can increase the foundation chain and adjust the height by adding more rows to the body of the purse.
