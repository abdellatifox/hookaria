You'll learn the flower cluster stitch.

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- sl st – slip stitch
- sc – single crochet
- dc – double crochet
- inc – increase
- sp – space
- flower cluster – work multiple stitches in the same stitch or space

## How to Make a Flower Cluster
1. Yarn over, insert the hook into the specified stitch, and pull up a loop (3 loops on hook).
2. Yarn over again, pull through the first two loops on the hook (2 loops remain).
3. Yarn over again, pull through the last two loops.
4. Repeat steps 1–3 twice more in the same stitch.
5. Yarn over and pull through all loops on the hook to complete the cluster.

## The Pattern

### Step 1: Create the Pillow Body
The pillow body is worked in rounds.

Round 1: Ch 4 (counts as your first dc), work 11 dc into the 1st ch. Join with sl st to the top of the starting ch. (12 dc)

Round 2: Ch 3 (counts as your first dc), dc in the same st, *2 dc in the next st*. Repeat from * around. Join with sl st to the top of the starting ch. (24 dc)

Round 3: Ch 3, dc in the same st, *2 dc in the next st, dc in the next st*. Repeat from * around. Join with sl st to the top of the starting ch. (36 dc)

Round 4: Ch 3, dc in the same st, *2 dc in the next st, dc in the next 2 st*. Repeat from * around. Join with sl st to the top of the starting ch. (48 dc)

Round 5: Ch 3, dc in the same st, *2 dc in the next st, dc in the next 3 st*. Repeat from * around. Join with sl st to the top of the starting ch. (60 dc)

Round 6: Ch 1 (does not count as a stitch), sc in the same st, *dc in the next st, flower cluster in the next st*. Repeat from * around, ending with a dc. Join with sl st to the first sc. (60 st total)

### Step 2: Add the Scalloped Border
The scalloped border adds a playful touch to the pillow.

Round 7: Ch 1 (does not count as a stitch), sc in each st around. Join with sl st to the first sc. (60 sc)

Round 8: Ch 1 (does not count as a stitch), *sc in the next st, ch 3, skip 2 st, sc in the next st*. Repeat from * around. Join with sl st to the first sc.

Round 9: Join a new color in any ch 3 sp. Ch 3 (counts as your first dc), *flower cluster in the next sp, ch 1, skip the sc*. Repeat from * around, finishing with a flower cluster in the last sp. Join with sl st to the top of the starting ch.

## Finishing Tip: Secure Your Ends
Fasten off all yarn and weave in ends using a tapestry needle. Ensure that your ends are secured properly to keep your pillow looking neat.

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Pillow
- Use stitch markers to keep track of the rounds.
- Count your stitches at the end of each round to ensure you stay on track.
- Maintain even tension for a consistent finish.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- My pillow is too tight. – This may be due to crochet tension. Try using a looser grip and check your gauge.
- The shape is off. – Make sure to follow the stitch counts in each round. Count your stitches regularly as you work.
- The flowers are not defined. – Ensure you’re working the clusters evenly and pulling through all loops at the end of each cluster.

## How to Care for Your Pillow
To keep your pillow looking fresh, hand wash it in cold water with a mild detergent. Lay it flat to dry to maintain its shape.
