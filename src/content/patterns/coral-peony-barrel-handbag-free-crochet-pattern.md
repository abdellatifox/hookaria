This handbag is suitable for those with intermediate skills who want to enhance their crochet technique. You’ll learn how to create a handbag base, a flower motif, and how to properly assemble your project.

## Skill Level: Intermediate
- Stitches required: ch (chain), sc (single crochet), dc (double crochet), inc (increase), dec (decrease)
- New stitch: Special stitch for Peony Petal.

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- sc – single crochet
- dc – double crochet
- sl st – slip stitch
- st(s) – stitch(es)
- inc – increase (2 sc in the same stitch)
- dec – decrease (sc two stitches together)

**Special Stitch – Peony Petal:**
1. Start with a dc in the same stitch, followed by 2 dc in the next stitch.
2. Repeat until the specified number of petals is formed for the peony.

## Gauge and Finished Size
- Finished size: approximately 30 cm (12 in) in diameter and 15 cm (6 in) in height.
- Gauge: 12 stitches and 8 rows = 10 cm (4 in) in single crochet.

## How to Crochet a Coral Peony Barrel Handbag (Step by Step)

### Base of the Handbag
The base is worked in rounds. 
1. **Round 1:** Make a magic ring, ch 1, 10 sc into the ring. Join with a sl st to the first sc. (10 sts)
2. **Round 2:** Ch 1, inc in each st around. Join. (20 sts)
3. **Round 3:** Ch 1, *sc in the first st, inc in the next st; repeat from * around. Join. (30 sts)
4. **Round 4:** Ch 1, *sc in the first 2 sts, inc in the next st; repeat from * around. Join. (40 sts)
5. **Round 5:** Ch 1, *sc in the first 3 sts, inc in the next st; repeat from * around. Join. (50 sts)
6. **Round 6:** Ch 1, *sc in the first 4 sts, inc in the next st; repeat from * around. Join. (60 sts)
7. **Rounds 7-10:** Ch 1, sc in each st around. Join. (60 sts)

### Body of the Handbag
The body continues from the base. 
8. **Round 11:** Ch 1, *sc in the first 5 sts, inc in the next st; repeat from * around. Join. (70 sts)
9. **Rounds 12-15:** Ch 1, sc in each st around. Join. (70 sts)

### Peony Motif
This part adds the beautiful flower design.
10. **Round 16:** Ch 3 (counts as dc), *dc in the next two sts, ch 2, skip the next 2 sts; repeat from * around. Join. (17 petals created)
11. **Round 17:** Attach Green yarn. Ch 1, sc in each ch-2 space, *sc in the next st, ch 2; repeat from * around. Join. 

### Finishing the Handbag
Finalize your project for everyday use.
12. **Round 18:** Ch 1, sc in each st around, join.
13. Fasten off and weave in ends.

## Assembly & Finishing
- Sew the magnetic snap closure to the inside of the handbag.
- Cut the lining to fit the inner circumference of the bag, allowing for seam allowance. Pin, then sew.
- Attach the lining securely to the inner edge of the handbag using a whip stitch.
- If adding a strap, sew or crochet the strap securely to the sides of the bag.

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Handbag
- Use stitch markers – they help keep your place while working.
- Count your stitches regularly – this ensures accuracy as your project grows.
- Keep an even tension – it will make your handbag look neater and more polished.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- "My project is uneven." – This can happen if stitch counts vary. Keep track of your counts and check your rows or rounds.
- "The handbag is too tight." – This might be due to too many stitches in each round. Consider adjusting your increases.
- "I can't attach the lining neatly." – Pin it securely before sewing, and take your time to ensure a clean finish.

## How to Care for Your Handbag
For optimal care, hand wash your handbag or machine wash on a delicate cycle in cold water. Lay it flat to dry to maintain its shape.
