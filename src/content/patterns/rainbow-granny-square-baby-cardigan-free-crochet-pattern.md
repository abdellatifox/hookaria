This pattern is suitable for those with an easy skill level, making it a great project for beginners and seasoned crocheters alike. You’ll learn how to create granny squares, assemble them into a cardigan, and finish with a tidy edging.

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- sl st – slip stitch
- sc – single crochet
- dc – double crochet
- sp – space
- sk – skip
- RS – right side
- WS – wrong side

#### Special Stitches
- **Granny Square:** *Ch 3, 2 dc, ch 1, (3 dc, ch 1) in same sp* to create clusters.

## Size and Gauge
- **Finished Size:** 20 inches (50 cm) around the chest, 10 inches (25 cm) from neck to hem, designed for 0-3 months.
- **Gauge:** 10 stitches and 10 rows in granny square pattern = 4 inches (10 cm).

## How to Make a Granny Square
1. Ch 4, join with sl st to form a ring.
2. Rnd 1: Ch 3 (counts as 1st dc), 2 dc in ring, ch 1, *(3 dc in ring, ch 1)* repeat 2 more times, join. (12 dc, 4 ch-1 sps)
3. Rnd 2: Sl st to next ch-1 sp, ch 3, 2 dc in same sp, ch 1, *(3 dc in next ch-1 sp, ch 1)* repeat 3 times, join. (24 dc, 8 ch-1 sps)

## Step-by-Step Instructions
### Part 1: Granny Squares
The granny squares will be made in various colors. 
- **Red Square:** 
  - Ch 4, join with sl st to form a ring.
  - Rnd 1: Ch 3 (counts as 1st dc), 2 dc in ring, ch 1, *(3 dc in ring, ch 1)* repeat 2 more times, join. (12 dc, 4 ch-1 sps)
  - Rnd 2: Sl st to next ch-1 sp, ch 3, 2 dc in same sp, ch 1, *(3 dc in next ch-1 sp, ch 1)* repeat 3 times, join. (24 dc, 8 ch-1 sps)
- Repeat this to create squares in the following colors:
  - Orange
  - Yellow
  - Green
  - Blue
  - Purple

### Part 2: Assembling the Cardigan
The squares will then be arranged and joined. 
1. **Layout Squares:** Arrange 4 squares in a 2x2 layout for the front and 3 squares for the back.
2. **Join Squares:** Using slip stitch, join the 4 front squares together edge-to-edge. (24 sts across) Do the same for the 3 back squares.
3. **Create Armholes:** Slip stitch around the armholes of the joined squares to tidy edges.

### Part 3: Edging and Finishing
Finishing touches will bring your cardigan to life. 
1. **Edging:** With H/8 hook, sc around the entire neckline and edges of the cardigan. Rnd 1: Sc in each stitch around, join with sl st.
2. **Button Loops:** Create button loops by chaining 5 in front panels between the last square and 3 stitches down from the edge. Complete the front panel sc edging.
3. **Button Placement:** Sew on buttons securely where you placed your button loops.
4. **Finishing Touches:** Weave in ends and block the cardigan.

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Baby Cardigan
- Use stitch markers to keep track of your rounds.
- Count your stitches regularly to avoid mistakes.
- Maintain consistent tension for a neat finish.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- "My squares are different sizes." – Check your gauge to ensure all squares match.
- "The button loops are too tight." – Make chains looser for each button loop to ensure easy closure.
- "My edges are messy." – Ensure you slip stitch tightly and evenly when joining squares.

## How to Care for Your Cardigan
Machine wash on a gentle cycle; tumble dry low or lay flat to dry for best results.
