This delightful Cozy Starburst Baby Hat is designed to keep your little one warm and stylish. It features a unique starburst pattern achieved through alternating stitches, creating a textured look. The hat is topped with a fluffy pom-pom for an extra touch of whimsy.

This pattern is great for beginner to intermediate crocheters. You will enhance your skills with alternating stitches while creating a lovely accessory for babies.

## Why You'll Love This Cozy Starburst Baby Hat
- Unique design – the starburst pattern adds a charming texture.
- Fun to make – simple stitches make this an enjoyable project.
- Perfect gift – ideal for baby showers or new parents.
- Quick to crochet – you can make it in just a few hours!

## Is This Pattern Good for Beginners?
Yes! This pattern is suitable for all skill levels. You'll learn to work with basic crochet stitches while creating a fun hat.

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- sl st – slip stitch
- sc – single crochet
- hdc – half double crochet: yarn over, insert hook into stitch, yarn over again, pull through all three loops on your hook.
- dc – double crochet: yarn over, insert hook into stitch, yarn over, pull through, yarn over again, pull through two loops, yarn over, pull through the last two loops.
- sp – space
- inc – increase

## Size and Gauge
- Finished size: 38 × 41 cm (15 × 16 in) for a baby size.
- Gauge: 14 dc and 10 rows = 10 cm 

To measure your gauge, make a small square swatch. If your gauge is off, try using a different hook size.

## Step 1: Create the Base
The base of the hat is worked in the round. 
- Round 1: Ch 3, work 11 dc into the first ch. Join with sl st to the top of the beginning ch. (12 dc)
- Round 2: Ch 2 (does not count as a stitch here), *dc in next dc, 2 dc in next dc; repeat from * around. Join with sl st to first dc. (18 dc)
- Round 3: Ch 2, *dc in next 2 dc, 2 dc in next dc; repeat from * around. Join with sl st to first dc. (24 dc)
- Round 4: Ch 2, *dc in next 3 dc, 2 dc in next dc; repeat from * around. Join with sl st to first dc. (30 dc)

## Step 2: Create the Starburst Pattern
Now we’ll create the starburst pattern with alternating stitches.
- Round 5: Ch 1, *hdc in next 2 dc, sc in next 3 dc; repeat from * around. Join with sl st to first hdc. (30 stitches)
- Round 6: Ch 1, *sc in next 3 stitches, hdc in next 2 stitches; repeat from * around. Join with sl st to the first sc. (30 stitches)
- Round 7: Ch 1, *hdc in next 3 sc, sc in next 2 hdc; repeat from * around. Join with sl st to first hdc. (30 stitches)

What’s happening? We are creating a textured effect by alternating between hdc and sc. This method gives your hat a unique look.

## Step 3: Finish the Hat
Complete the hat with a few final rounds.
- Round 8: Ch 1, sc in each stitch around. Join with sl st to the first sc. (30 stitches)
- Round 9: Ch 1, in the same stitch, work *1 sc, 2 inc in next stitch; repeat from * around. Join with sl st to the first sc. (45 stitches)
- Round 10: Ch 1, sc in each stitch around. Join with sl st to the first sc. (45 stitches)

## How to Add the Pom-Pom
- Cut a piece of yarn about 30 cm (12 in) long.
- Wrap yarn around your fingers or a pom-pom maker about 50 times.
- Tie securely in the middle, cut the loops and trim into a nice shape.
- Attach the pom-pom to the top of the hat using the long piece of yarn you cut earlier.

## Finishing Tip: Secure Your Ends
Fasten off your work, leaving a long tail to weave in. Use a tapestry needle to weave in any loose ends for a neat finish.

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Cozy Starburst Baby Hat
- Use stitch markers – this can help keep track of your rounds.
- Count accurately – always recount your stitches to avoid mistakes.
- Maintain tension – consistent tension gives a more even finish.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- My hat is too big. – You may have used a larger hook than needed. Make sure to check your gauge.
- My stitches look uneven. – This can happen if your tension varies. Practice keeping your tension consistent.
- I'm having trouble with the pom-pom. – It can be tricky! Take your time and cut the loops carefully for a nice finish.

## How to Care for Your Cozy Starburst Baby Hat
Hand wash the hat in cold water and lay it flat to dry. Avoid wringing to maintain its shape.

## FAQ
### How many stitches do I need for a baby hat?
For this hat, you start with 12 stitches and increase as you go. Adjust according to your gauge for a perfect fit.

### Can I change the yarn type?
Yes! Just remember to check your gauge. Bulkier yarn may change the finished size.

### How long does it take to make this hat?
This hat can be completed in about 3–4 hours, depending on your experience level.
