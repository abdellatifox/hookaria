This cozy Crochet Woven Stitch Blanket is perfect for adding warmth and style to your home. With its beautiful woven stitch pattern, this blanket not only provides comfort but also showcases classic crochet techniques. You’ll learn how to create a textured design that is satisfying to make while enjoying the relaxing rhythm of crochet.

This pattern is suitable for intermediate crocheters who are familiar with basic stitches. You’ll master the woven stitch technique, which combines front post and back post stitches for a striking effect.

## Is This Pattern Good for Beginners?  
Not quite! This project is best suited for intermediate crocheters. You will be exploring stitches that require a bit more practice, like the woven stitch, which blends front and back post techniques.

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)  
- ch – chain  
- sc – single crochet  
- dc – double crochet  
- st – stitch  
- sk – skip  
- rep – repeat  
- Woven Stitch: (a stitch that combines both front post and back post double crochet.)  
   - *Foundation Round for Woven Stitch:*  
      1. Work 1 dc around the front post of the previous dc (from the previous row).  
      2. Work 1 dc around the back post of the same dc.  

## Size and Gauge  
- Finished size: Approximately 127 × 152 cm (50 × 60 in).  
- Gauge: 14 stitches x 10 rows in Woven Stitch = 10 cm (4 in) square.  

To check gauge, crochet a swatch measuring 14 stitches and 10 rows. If you do not have the right size, adjust your hook size accordingly.

## How to Crochet the Woven Stitch Blanket (Step by Step)  
**Foundation Chain:**  
The blanket begins with a foundation chain.  
1. Ch 168 (or any multiple of 18 + 6 for desired width).  

**Row 1:**  
1. Starting in the 4th ch from the hook, work 1 dc in each ch across. (166 dc)  

**Row 2:**  
1. Ch 2 (this counts as your first dc), turn.  
2. Work *1 dc around the front post of the previous dc, sk 1 st, and 1 dc around the back post of the next dc*.   
3. Repeat from * to * to end, ending with the last dc in the turning chain.  

**Row 3 to Row 20:**  
1. Continue repeating Row 2 until your blanket measures approximately 152 cm (60 in) in length, or to your desired length.  

**Color Change:**  
1. At the end of Row 20, change to Color B, and continue in the same stitch pattern for the next 20 rows.  

**Repeat Color Change:**  
1. After finishing 20 rows in Color B, switch to Color A for another 20 rows.  
2. Follow with Color C for 10 rows.  
3. Continue alternating between Colors A and B until you reach a total height of 152 cm (60 in) or your desired size.  

**End of Blanket:**  
Complete your final row, then choose the color for your border to finish off.

## How to Weave in Ends  
- Use a tapestry needle to weave in all ends to keep your blanket neat.  

## Adding the Border  
With Color A, work a round of sc around the entire blanket for a clean finish.  
1. Ch 1, sc in same st, *sc in next st, ch 2, skip 1 st, sc in next* repeat until end.  

Optional: Block your blanket to shape and enhance the woven stitch pattern.

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Blanket  
- Use stitch markers to help remember stitch counts.  
- Count your stitches regularly to prevent any mistakes.  
- Maintain an even tension to avoid gaps in your woven stitches.  

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)  
- "My blanket seems too narrow." – This may be due to not chaining enough at the beginning. Ensure your foundation chain is accurate.  
- "I’m getting gaps in my stitches." – Make sure to keep even tension on your yarn throughout the project.  
- "The stitches look wonky." – Count your stitches and check for consistency; it may be time to practice your tension.

## How to Care for Your Blanket  
Machine wash on cold and tumble dry on low heat; avoid bleach to keep your blanket looking great.
