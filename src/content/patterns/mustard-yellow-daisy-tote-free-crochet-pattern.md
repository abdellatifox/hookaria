This Mustard Yellow Daisy Tote is a vibrant crochet bag designed for everyday use, featuring a cheerful daisy motif. It’s an enjoyable project that's perfect for beginners, allowing you to practice basic stitches like double crochet and single crochet while creating a functional accessory.

This pattern is great for anyone looking to enhance their crochet skills while crafting a stylish tote bag. You'll learn how to switch colors, create daisy motifs, and attach straps, making this not only a fun project but a practical addition to your crochet repertoire.

- Quick and manageable – complete this project in about 5 hours.
- Colorful design – brighten your day with a cheerful mustard yellow color scheme.
- Practical use – a stylish bag for everyday errands and activities.
- Fun techniques – practice color changes and learn to create motifs.

## Is This Pattern Good for Beginners?
Yes! This pattern uses basic stitches like chain, double crochet, and single crochet that are easy to learn. It also introduces you to color changes and simple motifs, making it a great way to build your crochet skills.

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- dc – double crochet
- sl st – slip stitch
- sc – single crochet
- sp – space

**Daisy Motif**: 
1. Make a magic ring; ch 2 (counts as first dc).
2. Work 11 dc into the ring, pull tight to close (12 dc).
3. Sl st into the top of the first dc to close the round.
4. Ch 3 (counts as first dc), dc into same stitch, ch 1, *dc two in next stitch, ch 1; repeat from * around (skip each dc, 11 petals). Join with sl st.

## Size and Gauge
- Finished tote dimensions: 35.5 × 30.5 cm (14 × 12 in)
- Handle length: 58.5 cm (23 in)
- Gauge: 12 dc x 7 rows = 10 cm (4 in) in double crochet.

To check gauge, crochet a swatch of 20 dc for 10 rows, measure the stitches and rows in 10 cm (4 in). If your gauge does not match, adjust your hook size until it does.

## How to Crochet the Mustard Yellow Daisy Tote (Step by Step)

### Body of the Tote
The body is worked in rows using Color A (Mustard Yellow).
1. With Color A, ch 40.
2. Row 1: dc in 4th ch from hook (ch counts as first dc) and in each ch across (38 dc).
3. Rows 2–6: ch 3, turn, dc in each st across (38 dc).
4. Change to Color B (White) in the last st of Row 6.
5. Rows 7–9: ch 1, turn, sc in each st across (38 sc).
6. Change to Color A in the last st of Row 9.
7. Rows 10–15: ch 3, turn, dc in each st across (38 dc).
8. Change to Color B in the last st of Row 15.
9. Rows 16–18: ch 1, turn, sc in each st across (38 sc).

### Daisy Motifs
Now, we’ll create the daisy motifs for decoration.
10. Make 4 Daisy Motifs using Color B; attach with sl st around the upper edge of the tote, spacing evenly.

### Handles
The handles are worked separately and attached later.
11. With Color A, ch 60.
12. Row 1: sc in 2nd ch from hook and in each ch across (59 sc).
13. Rows 2–4: ch 1, turn, sc in each st across (59 sc).
14. Fasten off and create a second identical handle.

## Assembly & Finishing
- Attach the handles to each side of the tote using slip stitches or by stitching them securely with a tapestry needle.
- Weave in all ends using the tapestry needle.
- Optionally, sew a button on the inside for closure or decoration.
- Block the bag to shape it as desired.

## Beginner Tips for a Neat Tote
- Use stitch markers to keep track of the beginning of each row.
- Count your stitches as you go to avoid surprises later.
- Maintain consistent tension throughout your project for a uniform look.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- "My stitches are uneven." – This may be due to inconsistent tension. Practice maintaining even pressure on your yarn.
- "The bag is too small." – Make sure you counted your chains correctly and didn't skip any stitches.
- "My daisy motifs look wonky." – If they aren't closing properly, check that you pulled the magic ring tight enough and counted the dc correctly.

## How to Care for Your Tote
Hand wash in cold water, and lay flat to dry. Avoid machine washing to prevent stretching.
